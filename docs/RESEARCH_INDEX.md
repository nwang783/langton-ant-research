## Audit and search entry points

- `audit.md`: authoritative retained chronological scope and corrections, with historical status preserved.
- `audits/`: independent executable checkers, reports, exact negative controls and replay manifests.
- `RECOVERY_INDEX.md`: original provenance and historical missing-artifact distinctions.
- Root-level review/checkpoint files in the research tree: historical local mathematical snapshots, superseded by later audit entries when they disagree.
- `../manifests/experiments.jsonl`: topic, source-checker paths, manifest/report paths, and evidence counts. This index deliberately does not infer “proved” from a filename or a PASS string.
- `../manifests/artifacts.jsonl`: exact hash, size, and Git-versus-archive disposition.
- `../manifests/duplicate_artifacts.json`: byte-identical artifacts, allowing reuse rather than repeated work. Nonidentical experiments can still be logically redundant and require human comparison.

## Before extending a route

1. Identify the exact claim and quantifiers.
2. Read its newest signed scope and mandatory corrections.
3. Search existing negative controls and failed approaches.
4. Check the input/source hashes and complete-field assumptions.
5. Record parameters, command, dependencies, runtime outcome, and new artifact hashes.
6. Separate the finite checker from the analytic implication and from the unresolved global step.

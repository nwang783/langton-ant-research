# Why a residual genealogy wire is not a physical barrier

This is a small exact obstruction to a proposed ribbon shortcut, not a
counterexample to the open U-component merger lemma.

Take the actual simple atomic history D, D at initial pose (0, 0, N). The
first D has ordered residual input I = (0, 1) and output O = (2, 0). Its ten
raw read cells do NOT include I: I becomes a black cell of the new core at
exit head (1, 1, N). The next packet's actual marker reads that old identity.
The output O is born at the first D's tick 8.

The naive straight parent-child edge I--O intersects the SECOND D center
chord, from (1/2, 1/2) to (3/2, 3/2), at (2/3, 2/3). The first-D output O
is still current and is not touched by that second D. Thus a moving center
can cross this chosen genealogy edge without retiring either of its remote
endpoints, in a perfectly actual history with no center self-contact.

The two microscopic words plus true marker are consistent from seven black
cells. Consequently one cannot turn such straight ancestry edges into
forbidden planar obstacles. A valid maintained representation needs the
core-to-residual exchange and the next-marker channel, or a carefully
justified homotopy class relative to the actual past cursor.

A second independent shortcut also fails statically. U's five input points
a=(-1,1), q=(-1,2), b=(-1,4), c=(0,2), d=(0,3) can be connected by the
noncrossing tree a--q--b--(0,4)--d--c. This tree misses U's entire center
chord x=-1/2, -1/2<=y<=7/2 because it connects around the upper endpoint.
It is not a physical ancestry construction: the intermediate cell (0,4) is
before-white. It shows precisely why mere existence of a planar connected
component does not establish a cursor obstruction.

The open task is causal, not static planarity: retain an embedding or region
whose allowed updates are proved for each actual packet and whose relation
to the core/cursor is invariant. Historical connectors through current empty
cells and current live-cell connectivity are both insufficient on their own.

A later exact positive representation is now signed and must be distinguished
from those failed shortcuts. In the EVEN dual, the corrected fine-step center
stays fixed on L while deleting a black incident edge, and traverses a newly
added edge on R. The marked core is a full four-arm star plus one neighboring
edge. See active_center_edge_walk.md and frozen1739. The opposite checkerboard
current-edge graph and pixel connectivity are not this active-star graph.
Even here, however, grafting every disconnected component to all K5 edges is
not a locally inductive history predicate: artificially equal colors on two
untouched residual edges of an actual eight-cell D board lose their old-core
connection. Reachability of that coloring is not claimed. A valid proof needs
restricted port history, not core collapse. One D's exact R-walk already has
a four-edge loop, so atomic-chord simplicity cannot be promoted to fine-walk
simplicity in this representation.

# Why independently sponsored residual outputs do not yet yield a finite budget

2026-10-23 09:08 UTC. This is a limitation of one finite relaxation, not an ant counterexample or a claim that physical D renewal occurs.

Consider a proposed support potential that bounds each fresh residual output through ANY chosen consumed residual input, with a bounded weight depending only on the producer packet/output/heading role. Even allowing a separate input policy for every child output cannot make the C coefficient zero on the current signed pair-role relaxation.

The reason is already present in D, which has exactly one residual input(0,1) and one residual output(2,0). LA/LB's independently reconstructed role graph contains

 -> D output(2,0),heading3: least unexcluded C gap3,dy2;
 -> D output(2,0),heading2: least unexcluded C gap5,dy1.

The role indices are2 ->3 ->2. The normalized protected transfers are

sourceD -> targetD at pose(1,0,heading1), protected output(2,0), exact gap3;
sourceD -> targetD at pose(3,0,heading3), protected output(2,0), gap5 in the signed overflow>=5 bin.

The total ancestral displacement is(-1,3), with8C. Both checker sectors and the signed pair constraints are respected; arbitrary intervening words and actual simultaneous contexts are NOT supplied.

A zero-C-cost potential Q would need

2 <=Q(role2)−Q(role3),
1 <=Q(role3)−Q(role2),

which sum to3<=0. There is no alternate residual input policy for these unary D states. More generally this cycle alone forces a speed coefficient at least3/8 for any such independent-role sponsorship potential.

This does NOT obstruct a potential that couples the live core, joint input identities, multiple generations, actual intermediate chronology, or complete white contexts. It does not preclude a summed/branch-sensitive quantity that charges the hidden C-producing activity. The pair-role model intentionally forgets that activity, and the exact cycle need not have a physical realization.

This check was performed BEFORE building a large all-input min-max search. The earlier potential optimizers include a live core term and therefore are not ruled out by this narrower obstruction.

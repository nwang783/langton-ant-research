## Exact producer covers

Use target U coordinates and let its inputs be

 a=(-1,1), q=(-1,2), b=(-1,4), c=(0,2), d=(0,3).

This input shape is the same for U,UC,UCC. Normalize the target pose sector to0.
For ANY recurrent-eight packet to create a fresh residual output at q, choose
its output slot, heading and thus unique head position. There are exactly136
sector-compatible placements. Every placement's complete RAW+M11 entry context
meets BOTH sets {a,c} and {a,d}. These are precisely the two inclusion-minimal
cover sets among all subsets of {a,b,c,d}. The full output-slot inventory is
finite; no bound on intervening time, age, distance or seed mass is used.

If two named old residual identities occupy one such cover pair, neither can be
touched by a packet while both survive as those identities. A required-white
read conflicts with black; an entry input retires the old residual identity. Entry-core overlap is
impossible for an old CURRENT residual, so that identity must already have
retired before entry even when the color matches. Thus the packet that births q
cannot run while both members of either cover pair are already current and
survive it. Later color restoration does not restore a retired old identity.

## Birth-order theorem

Give every factor-entry residual identity virtual birth -infinity. Every fresh
output receives its actual SOURCE PACKET INDEX as birth, including recycled
outputs. At a U-family consumer write B_x for its exact input identity's birth.
The last producer of q must leave at least one member of each cover pair to be
born at that packet or later. Therefore

 B_q <= max(B_a,B_c),  B_q <= max(B_a,B_d).                 (1)

Equivalently, B_q<=B_a OR (B_q<=B_c AND B_q<=B_d). In particular q cannot be
the uniquely latest-born one of the five consumer inputs. Simultaneous births
are allowed: this is not a strict inequality when a producer births q together
with one of the other named inputs. If q is an entry identity (1) is trivial.
No assertion about its actual pre-entry physical birth or global root owners
is made. If a later candidate factor is cut out of an older history, reset entry
identities must remain virtual-old, not assigned invented packet ages.

## Produced C-age corollary

At the common consumer C clock, let A_x be source-included/consumer-excluded
C age for an identity produced inside the factor, and +infinity for an entry
identity. The monotone C clock coarsens actual packet birth order, giving

 A_q >= min(A_a,A_c),  A_q >= min(A_a,A_d).                 (2)

Equivalently A_q>=A_a OR (A_q>=A_c AND A_q>=A_d). The C-age statement is weaker
than (1), since different zero-C packets can share a C clock. By signed KV,
produced c,d have ages>=5; entry c,d have virtual age+infinity. Hence

 A_q<5 implies A_a<=A_q,                                  (3)

and a must itself be produced, not a virtual-old entry identity. Using JO's
lower-input minima, produced q age3 forces a age3. For U/UC, q age4 permits
a age3 or4; UCC's signed exact-age4 hole excludes q age4 altogether. More
generally, if A_a>A_q then BOTH A_c,A_d<=A_q. No individual minimum is raised.

No recurrent producer has all five U input positions among its outputs in ANY
sector-compatible relative pose. The maximum overlap is four. There are exactly
24 four-cohort templates across the eight source types, counted once for the
common target input shape (each applies to all three target tails).

Poses are source-normalized (target head x,y,heading change). For each of these,

Two further templates, UC/UCC at(1,5,2), have q outside the known source context.
Here the fifth identity MUST ALREADY be current immediately after the source,
with a birth strictly before that source, and remain current until consumption.
It may be a previously produced identity or an entry identity; no different
root or new initial input is inferred. Equality of source birth is impossible
because no source has all five output positions. The other fifteen templates
are not excluded by this static producer-cover argument. Surviving producer
placements are not claimed to be jointly accessible physical histories.

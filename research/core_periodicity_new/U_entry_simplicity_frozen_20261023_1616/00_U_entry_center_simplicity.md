# U-family entry forces a simple corrected-center path

## Theorem

Let an actual finite factor of the complete recurrent-eight language begin
with U, UC, or UCC. Partition every full packet into its actual D/V/U base and
zero, one, or two C atoms. Replace each atomic raw excursion by its straight
corrected-center chord, where the center is

    head + rotation_heading(−1/2, −1/2).

The resulting continuous polygonal path is injective in path time. In
particular, no two nonadjacent atomic chords cross, touch, or overlap, and no
two distinct atomic entries have the same center or full head pose.

This is AUXILIARY CENTER simplicity, not microscopic self-avoidance. A fine
physical cell may still be read repeatedly. The statement does not imply
zero incidence rank, no sibling reconvergence, a finite C bound, renewal,
V1/U1 closure, or capture. The scope ends when the complete-eight factor ends.

The same conclusion holds for an infinite complete-eight factor beginning
U-family: any hypothetical contact would occur in some finite complete prefix.

## Complete first-contact assembly

Suppose a center contact occurs, and choose its chronological first later
point. Use the outgoing earlier chord and incoming later chord, with
0 <= s < 1 and 0 < t <= 1. The signed horizon theorem gives an atomic ENTRY
C gap n from two through six. Normalize the earlier entry to (0, 0, N).

The new assembly checker independently regenerates EVERY canonical geometry
from exact contact parallelograms, sector parity, and the horizon's exact
alpha ordering. It compares its universe with the separately signed frozen
catalogues, including proper crossings, endpoint touches, and collinear onset.
The case counts are:

- Gap two: 8 geometries.
- Gap three: 45 geometries, 21 proper and 24 nonproper.
- Gap four: 23 geometries, including collinear first entry.
- Gap five: 45 geometries, 21 proper and 24 nonproper.
- Gap six: 8 geometries.

All 129 cases are accounted for exactly once. The assembly introduces no
new spatial radius, C threshold, or word-length search.

## Surviving boundary geometries

Only five geometric rows survive as boundary exceptions. Every one excludes
U-family as the first full packet:

1. Gap three, earlier D, later V at (−1, 0, E), proper crossing. The signed
   universal atomic three-C left-pose law restricts the source to factor
   entry or immediately after factor-first plain D. Thus the first full
   packet is D or DC, not U-family.

2. Gap three, earlier C, later V at (−1, 0, E), proper crossing. The same
   atomic law restricts this C to the first C of factor-first DC.

3. Gap three, earlier V, later plain D at (−1, 0, E), nonproper contact. The
   signed complete nonproper reduction gives this sole gate. The universal
   atomic left-pose law then puts the V base at factor entry, or immediately
   after factor-first plain D. The first packet is V, VC, VCC, or plain D.
   This law explicitly includes a terminal D with only its actual marker;
   no subsequent complete packet is assumed.

4. Gap four, earlier D, later C at (−1, −1, N), nonproper contact. The signed
   incoming-bud halfplane theorem forces the earlier D to be factor-first.
   Its containing first full packet is D or DC.

5. Gap five, earlier D, later C at (0, −1, W), nonproper contact. The signed
   predecessor-history addendum forces the earlier D to be factor-first,
   again with first full packet D or DC. Its target may be an internal C.

Every other row is excluded by its signed local module. In particular,
all gap-two and gap-six cases are gone. All V/U-first proper cases are gone.
C-first proper gap five is gone. The D-first proper gap-five late-helper gate
is excluded by the signed protected-main helper-history theorem. The same
conditional helper-history theorem removes the sole D-to-U alternative in
the complete nonproper gap-five reduction; its required immediate helper
premise is supplied there independently.

Therefore the set of possible first full packet IDs for ANY first contact
is a subset of {D, DC, V, VC, VCC}. It is disjoint from {U, UC, UCC}. This
contradicts the assumed U-family entry and proves the theorem.

## Useful quantitative corollaries

All surviving first-contact source entries have C clock zero relative to
factor entry. Their target entry clocks are three, four, or five; if the
contact is at the final C endpoint, its endpoint clock is at most six.
This is a C-clock statement only, never a raw duration or head-box bound.

A separate exact footprint lemma gives a physical read bound for ANY simple
atomic center path. At a fixed physical cell, only 60 distinct entry centers
can read it. Their maximum multiplicities sum to

    28*1 + 20*2 + 12*3 = 104.

Thus a U-family-entry factor has at most 104 raw reads of any physical cell.
Its following actual M11 adds at most two, giving 106 including that marker.
The standalone footprint lemma is separately frozen so this small numerical
calculation is not hidden inside the first-contact dependency assembly.

For an infinite complete factor starting U-family, each fixed physical cell
is read at most 104 times. Consequently such a path eventually leaves every
fixed finite set of physical cells. This follows from a finite union of
finite visit sets; it does NOT prove highway behavior or bound the rate of
escape.

## Reproducibility and limitations

The checker hashes all 17 frozen dependency manifests and records a complete
129-row case ledger, precise module references, and permitted entry IDs for
every boundary row. The predecessor and core-shadow modules preserve actual
ordered identity survival and full/atomic clock shifts. Internal target C
cuts never require after-cut tails to finish.

Real boundary controls are retained, not discarded. The eight-/nine-cell
three-C endpoint controls and thirteen-/sixteen-cell four-/five-C endpoint
controls are compatible with this theorem because they do not begin U-family.
A separate actual simple-center U-entry reconvergence control shows why
incidence rank cannot be inferred from geometric simplicity.

No result here is asserted for an arbitrary D/V/U/C word, a standard highway
outside the retained language, or an ant history lacking the stipulated
complete factor decomposition.

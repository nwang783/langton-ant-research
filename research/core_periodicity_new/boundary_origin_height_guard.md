# Localizing the first-producer age-three exception below a height cut

23 October 2026 12:58 UTC. Author analytic corollary, independent review
requested. This is a compact necessary transition guard, not record closure.

Let H(x,y)=y-x. Suppose every initial residual identity actually CONSUMED in
an entire complete-eight factor has height at most F. No bound is imposed on
UNUSED initial black cells. At the first packet all residual inputs are such
initial identities, so its source head p at heading h satisfies

    H(p)+max_i H(rot_h(input_i)) <= F.

Literal reconstruction of all eight packet geometries and four headings gives

    max_j H(output_j)-max_i H(input_i) <=4.

Hence every first-packet output has height at most F+4. This conclusion uses
only consumed initial inputs, not an unjustified whitening of unused blacks.

## Sharp directional condition for a lower age-three U event

The signed1230 boundary theorem and signed1146 lower-pair equivalence say:
if either lower input of U/UC/UCC is produced at C-age3, both are co-born from
the FIRST factor packet, a V/VC/VCC source. If the consumer head is(p,h), that
source is at p+rot_h(-1,0), heading h+3 mod4. Its two lower-pair outputs have
local source slots(1,0) and(2,0). Its actual input slots are(0,3),(1,2).

Their maximum initial input height is exactly H(p)+kappa[h], where

    kappa[N,E,S,W]=(4,4,-4,-2).

Consequently a produced age-three lower input requires

    H(p) <= F-kappa[h].

Outside this low head region BOTH produced lower input minima are therefore
four. Entry identities keep infinite age, so no produced-age premise is
incorrectly assigned to them. This is a valid monotone per-control age guard;
it does not attempt to impose the nonmonotone joint LN order as an upward guard.

## Entire unfunded prefix and finite near-domain use

For an outer unfunded strict record at S and initial activation weight I,
every consumed initial identity has I+2H<S, hence

    F=floor((S-I-1)/2).

The conclusion applies to intermediate U-family packets throughout that
prefix, NOT merely the outer goal. In normalized U1, S30 and I48 give F=-10:
any produced lower-age3 U event must have incoming head height at most

    N:-14, E:-14, S:-6, W:-8.

All higher U poses may soundly use produced lower age minima four, retaining
the global first-source exception only in those low regions.

More generally, let a finite retained near-pose domain have
B=min_p(H(p)+kappa[h_p]). Choosing any finite valid activation weight
I>=max(I_required,S-2B) makes F<B and removes the produced-age3 exception
from EVERY retained U pose. Raising I preserves all initial-activation funding
inequalities; it changes finite activation-jump constants, not their finiteness.
The hidden/far domain still retains the boundary exception unless separately
proved below its own cut. No unsupported global intermediate min4 rule is used.

This makes the signed physical boundary information usable in a compact
height/age product without tracking first-source identities. No new numerical
model was run here. No U1/V1 closure, funding-count change, C/rank budget,
infinite renewal or capture theorem is asserted.

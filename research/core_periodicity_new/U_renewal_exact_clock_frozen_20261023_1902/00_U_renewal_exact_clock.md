# Every actual directed U-cycle renewal has exactly four Cs

Then t-s=4 exactly. Its three upper input identities were already current before the creating U entered. If produced within the factor, each has full-producer start C-clock at most s-1. Entry identities predate the whole factor.

No arbitrary atomic word, guessed excursion box, depth cap, current-token ancestry reassignment or strict speed potential is used. This does not bound the length or number of renewal chains, nor U count or confinement.

## 2. Exact turning number

In physical coordinate angles, the four atomic chord directions at entry C-clock j relative to s have unwrapped angle

    alpha_X + (j-s)*pi/2,

where alpha_D=alpha_C=pi/4, alpha_V=atan(3), alpha_U=pi/2. If the preceding atom has C increment c in{0,1}, the turn to the next chord is

    c*pi/2 + alpha_next-alpha_previous.

This is the actual principal turn, not merely a congruence: for a non-C predecessor it lies in[-pi/4,pi/4], and for C it lies in[pi/2,3pi/4]. The preceding C atom has alpha_C=pi/4. All bounds are strict within(-pi,pi).

Starting with the creator U's incoming vertical tangent at P and ending with the breaker U's vertical tangent at E, these principal turns telescope to(t-s)*pi/2. The bridge turns, including its join from that final vertical tangent, are

    +pi/4, +pi/2, -pi/2, -pi/4,

whose sum is zero; its final tangent at P is the creator's vertical tangent. Hence the total turning of the closed Jordan polygon is exactly(t-s)*pi/2. A simple closed polygon has total turn either+2pi or-2pi. As C count is nonnegative, this forces t-s=4.

This calculation remains valid when the goal is terminal: only its actual U raw word, actual internal cuts and actual following marker are used, never a fictitious following full packet.

## 3. Older support and the first-factor creator

Since t=s+4, b<=s-1. Consequently its genuine full producer lies strictly before the creating U; the identity is already current at that U entry and survives until the breaker.

Each is still a current entry identity until the breaker and therefore remains a separate singleton historical all-input incidence component. They are distinct from the five entry inputs already consumed by the creating U. The old east pair belongs to the creating U's component, possibly merged with other components by support, but not with these unconsumed entry identities. The first breaker therefore consumes exactly four historical components: that east-pair component and three distinct singleton roots.

The argument does NOT extend that entry-root conclusion to a later creator in the original factor: older produced support may already belong to the same historical component.

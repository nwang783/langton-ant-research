# A fresh U-family component has only six first-helper ports, with exact clocks

Scope: an actual complete recurrent-eight factor. Reset the residual genealogy immediately before a genuine U-family packet S starts: every current residual identity is a separate root, and the active core remains distinguished. S consumes five roots and creates its own component C, whose current residual identities at S's full exit are exactly its 9+3c fresh outputs, where c is 0, 1, or 2 according as S is U, UC, or UCC.

Let P be the first strictly later full packet consuming any one of those source-output identities. If no such packet occurs, C is unchanged forever within the retained factor. Otherwise there is the following dichotomy:

1. P consumes an identity outside C, so it genuinely merges C with an outside historical component.
2. Every input of P belongs to C. Then P is exactly one of the six gates below, in coordinates normalized so S begins at (0,0,N):

| Source | First helper | Heading | Consumed source cells | Source-entry C age |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| U | D at (2,3) | W | (1,3) | 5 |
| U | DC at (2,3) | W | (1,3) | 5 |
| UC | D at (1,7) | S | (1,6) | 6 |
| UC | V at (2,8) | S | (1,6), (2,5) | 6 |
| UCC | D at (-3,6) | E | (-2,6) | 7 |
| UCC | V at (-4,7) | E | (-2,6), (-1,7) | 7 |

In particular, an isolated source component cannot have a U-family packet as its first helper. Its first helper starts exactly five C units after the source's full exit, not merely at an arbitrarily late return.

This does not assert that any listed gate is physically attained. It also does not restrict what happens after this first helper. In particular, the two V gates are genuine unresolved same-component V candidates, not certified positive controls or excluded transfers.

## A current output-tree bridge fixes the clock

Use doubled corrected-center coordinates. The source U chord has midpoint W=(-1,3). For each of the six gates, its first atomic base exits at a vertex E in the source output graph. Close the actual future center arc from W to E by the source-output tree path from E back to W. The literal paths are recorded in the data file.

The path is simple. Each bridge edge is a distinct source output identity, current until P begins. It is therefore protected against intervening atomic centers by the signed current-residual barrier. Every source atomic chord portion from W through its complete C tail is checked directly against the bridge and meets it only at W. This covers the pre-full-installation interface, when a fresh output identity cannot yet be invoked as a current ledger edge. The final consumer base meets the bridge only at E. Actual U-entry suffix simplicity then makes the closed polygon Jordan.

Let alpha be the normal first-base chord angle: pi/4 for D and atan(3) for V. The signed exact-clock principal-turn lift along the actual atomic center arc from W to E is

    n*pi/2 + alpha - pi/2,

where n is the full source-entry-inclusive, consumer-entry-exclusive C age. For source tail count c, the explicit closing bridge turns sum to

    pi/2 - alpha - (c+1)*pi/2.

These identities are exact: all intermediate bridge vectors are diagonal, and the only non-quarter-diagonal angle is the initial V chord, which cancels alpha at its bridge join. Hence the total Jordan turn is

    (n-c-1)*pi/2.

It equals plus or minus 2*pi. The two formal solutions are n=c+5 and n=c-3. Since 0<=c<=2 and n>=0, only n=c+5 survives.

## What this adds and what remains open

The statement is an exact first-touch port/clock ledger. It retains the full producer output identities and cannot be replaced by static component connectivity. It gives no global bound on births of U chains. In the all-component-helper branch, D or V may create further unlocked output stock, and later multi-producer support remains unresolved. C growth and V growth have not been turned into finite fuel.

## Complete output-origin coverage

To install a residual at z, a later packet a must have some output slot r and
heading h, with head z-rot_h(r). The physical pose sector leaves exactly136
possible placements over all output slots of all eight recurrent packets.
Their complete contexts are reconstructed from literal RAW+actual M11.

EVERY such producer context intersects {A,C}; EVERY such producer context also
intersects {A,D}. Those are the only inclusion-minimal subsets of the four
source outputs that cover all136 producers. A protected current residual
identity cannot meet a strict packet context: a white read contradicts it,
a core overlap contradicts the exact partition, and an input retires it.
A recycled physical cell would install a NEW identity, not preserve the old one.

Consequently, while old A and old C both survive, no packet can install z in
the residual ledger. The same holds while old A and old D survive. Since z
is not residual just after source DC, a first-producer argument gives the
all-age invariant, at every later marked boundary:

if old A is current and either old C or old D is current, then z is NOT residual.

Equivalently, before z can enter the residual ledger, either old A has retired,
or BOTH old C and old D have retired. Births and retirements are the signed
ordered ledger, not physical black-lifetime ownership. The actual black bit at
z may change repeatedly; that does not create a residual identity without O.
No assumption about other roots, older component backlog, or elapsed Cs is used.

## Exclusion of simultaneous four-old-sibling U consumption

A complete finite input-mask alignment shows that U,UC,UCC can simultaneously
contain all three old spare cells B,C,D only at head(3,-1,N). Their five inputs
are precisely{A,z,B,C,D}, with z in the recycled lower slot(-1,2).
In particular a packet consuming all four OLD source output identities would
need z already residual BEFORE that target. But all old A,C,D then survive
through every strict intermediate packet, so the invariant forbids that z.
Therefore:

No U/UC/UCC can simultaneously consume all four old outputs of one DC,
regardless of C age or helper configuration.

The target's own recycled output at z does not repair its missing BEFORE-input.
This is an exact all-age exclusion of a simultaneous multi-identity transfer,
not an exclusion of any individual old-output transfer.

If a U-family packet consumes all three old spares B,C,D, then the old main A
must have been retired STRICTLY earlier. The target input at physical A must be
a different replacement identity. Thus a bulk-three-spare cleanup cannot simply
carry the old main into the same U: it requires an explicit earlier main
retirement and regeneration. It may still occur; no positive or negative full
supported word is supplied here.

The conclusion extends by translation and genuine quarter rotation. It does
not bound total C, total joins, future descendants, or physical lifetime fuel.
It must not be read as a new minimum-age rule, a U/record closure, or capture.

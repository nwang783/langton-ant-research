# A U-family base supplies only one possible parallel distinguished input, at exact C age six

Inside an actual complete recurrent-eight factor, let S be any genuine U, UC, or UCC full producer, normalized to entry (0,0,N). Consider one of its nine BASE-U output identities feeding the distinguished input of a later retained packet at heading N or S, parallel or antiparallel to S.

The only possible transfer is the arm identity t=(1,2) into a plain D at (1,3,S). Its exact full-source-entry-inclusive / consumer-entry-exclusive C age is six. There is at most one such identity per genuine U-family producer. No first-retirement, first-cohort-touch, or whole-cohort survival hypothesis is needed. C-tail outputs are not classified by this theorem.

The distinguished roles are the signed genealogy-role-speed roles:

- D and DC: (0,1).
- V and VC: (1,2).
- VCC: (0,3).
- U, UC, and UCC: (0,3).

In particular VCC uses its lower geometric V slot, unlike V/VC. The direct enumeration below uses this convention explicitly.

## Exact direct inventory

For each of the three source tail types, enumerate each of its nine base output cells, each of the eight retained consumer types, and each relative heading N or S. This gives 432 distinguished-input alignments.

- 192 have wrong active sector.
- 231 consumer complete raw-plus-marker contexts meet the source's permanent left star L={(0,0),(0,1),(1,0),(1,1)}.
- Six meet a forbidden past source atomic chord.
- The remaining three are precisely the same t-to-plain-D pose, one per source tail type.

Actual full-pose immediate-adjacency exceptions are retained when testing source chord contacts. The checker reconstructs the literal raw and marker requirements and complete output masks. Thus no separate q/east classification needs to be imported to certify this supply bound.

## Current three-edge bridge

Use doubled corrected centers. Let W=(-1,3) be the creating U chord midpoint. The consumer D has chord (3,7)→X=(1,5). Close the actual future center arc from W to X by

    X=(1,5) → T=(3,3) → L0=(1,1) → W=(-1,3).

The three physical edges are respectively the still-current arm t, the permanent left-star cell (1,1), and the permanent left-star cell (0,1). The bridge is simple. The source U remainder and every possible source C tail meet it only at W. The complete final D chord meets it only at X. The signed current-edge barrier and the U-entry simple atomic-center suffix make the closed polygon Jordan.

The atomic principal-turn lift is n*pi/2-pi/4, because the source U tangent has angle pi/2 while a normal D chord has angle pi/4. Closing joins are (+2,-2,-2,-1)*pi/4, totaling -3*pi/4. Thus the total closed turn is (n-2)*pi/2. It is plus or minus 2*pi, so the only nonnegative C-age solution is n=6.

The bridge uses no other old source output. Other cohort identities may already have retired without affecting this argument.

## Source-epoch supply consequence, conditional only on the epoch bookkeeping

In a source epoch containing u genuine U-family producers, their BASE outputs can supply at most u distinguished inputs in a parallel/antiparallel consumer epoch of age six, and none in any other such age. Any additional C-tail output supply belongs to the source epoch's unique positive-C boundary packet and must be accounted separately. The transport analysis is checking the exact boundary constant and the combined V contribution.

This is a distinguished-input capacity statement, not an all-input capacity or a bound on U births. The source output can of course have other non-distinguished consumers, subject to the separate signed port laws.

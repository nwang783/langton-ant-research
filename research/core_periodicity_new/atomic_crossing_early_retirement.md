### Internal-target safeguard

If b is a C atom, its consuming full packet may finish AFTER b, so we do NOT require the clearer's full C tail to end by clockN. Those extra options are admitted conservatively. When b is a V/U/D base, a distinct earlier consuming packet really must finish first, and a tail ending after clockN is impossible. This avoids importing the invalid full-tail completion assumption at internal atomic cuts. The two C-target catalogue cases already have a hard slot without relying on that assumption.

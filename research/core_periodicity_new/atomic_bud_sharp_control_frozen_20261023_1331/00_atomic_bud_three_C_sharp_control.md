# Atomic bud maturation is physically sharp at three C turns

23 October 2026 13:31 UTC. Author actual finite control, independent replay
requested. The control has no relocation, cap or hidden intermediate word.

On the explicit fourteen-black-cell board in the JSON certificate, the actual
complete recurrent-eight factor is

    D,D,D,DC,VC,VC,D,V.

Starting at(0,0,N), the first D creates its exit bud at(2,0). The SECOND D,
at(1,1,N), creates its own exit bud at(3,1). The final V at(0,1,E), after exactly
three C turns, consumes BOTH: the first bud as selected input(1,2), the second
as lower input(0,3). Both produced ages are exactly three, equal to the elapsed
post-exit C count because both source Ds have zero C. The second source is
strictly interior; this sharpness is not an entry-source-only phenomenon.

The checker reconstructs the entire initial board, directly executes every
RAW and genuine following M11, commits only RAW, and verifies physical head
continuity, ordered residual inputs/fresh outputs and the actual exit core.
Both produced-consumption HB/count-five queries pass. There are149 raw fine
steps,14 initial black cells and35 final black cells. No minimum-board-size
claim is made. Initial helper identities remain entry identities.

This proves that the newly signed uniform atomic three-C maturation cannot
be increased to four globally and that atomic buds are not permanently
untouchable. It does not produce a repeated renewal, arbitrarily long bounded
fuel family, unfunded record, infinite complete-eight word or capture
counterexample. It also does not contradict a three-C halfplane persistence
statement AT a consumer entry: the buds are present there and are consumed
BY that final V, not by an earlier strict packet.

# Exact all-input helper alternatives for one DC spare cohort

23 October 2026 09:37 UTC. Complete author finite template enumeration passes;
independent review requested. This is a necessary local mask layer, not a
physical word, initial-root import theorem, or unrestricted renewal exclusion.

Normalize source DC at (0,0,N), with continuing A=(2,0), spares B=(2,3),
C=(3,1), D=(3,2). Keep A current and suppose a helper retires at least one
original spare, has EVERY residual input among these three spares, and no other
source output has yet retired. The only possible complete-context templates
among all eight recurrent packets and all physical sector headings are:

- unary D at (2,4,S), retiring B;
- binary V at (3,5,S), retiring B and D together.

No positive-C packet is initially available under these isolated-cohort input
conditions. Completeness is finite: any candidate consuming one spare has its
head fixed by its heading and by the input slot aligned with that spare. The
checker lists and tests every such placement, all input slots and all eight
packet types; every nonconsumed current cohort identity must miss the target
complete context, including the five-cell core.

## Direct unary branch

If the first helper is D, its replacement at (0,4) is retained. Among helpers
using only the resulting four-identity cohort, preserving A, and retiring one
of the two remaining original spares without first retiring that replacement,
the sole option is D at (3,3,S), retiring original D. Its replacement is (1,3).
With both replacements retained, the sole next original-retirement option is
D at (4,1,W), retiring C. This agrees with the separately audited conditional
unary-preclearance order. Extra descendant rewrites can change these conditions.

## Binary merger cannot silently erase its replacements

The V template consumes B,D and creates six fresh residual identities at
(3,5),(3,4),(2,5),(1,5),(1,4),(1,3).
Together with continuing A and untouched original C, these form an eight-identity
post-V cohort. Keep all eight identities current until C retires, except of
course C at its own removal. There is NO recurrent-eight packet that retires C,
preserves A, and has every input inside that cohort.

In particular, the superficially available unary D at (4,1,W) needs the fresh
V output(3,4) white. Discarding that replacement would fabricate a cleanup.
The complete enumeration also rules out other D/V/U-family cohort-only ways to
retire C while all six V outputs survive.

Therefore, if main A remains current, then before original C retires, either
some V-born identity has itself been genuinely retired, OR the packet retiring
C consumes an identity OUTSIDE this fixed eight-identity post-V cohort. The
outside identity need not be initial or have a new initial-root label. It may
be an old dormant identity or a later descendant. Retirement does not mean
physical erasure, and every replacement retains its actual color/identity.

This is a concrete descendant-retirement versus additional-input alternative,
not a bound on joins, branches, support Cs, elapsed time, or total fuel. Live
head continuity and temporal colors remain to be imposed. The earlier unary
strategy is a local alternative, so this V-branch condition is not compulsory
for arbitrary spine renewals.

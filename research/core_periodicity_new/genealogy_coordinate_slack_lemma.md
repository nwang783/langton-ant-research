## Edge and path inequality

For every such edge,

3e <= 4(n-d) + Phi(h)-Phi(h').                       (1)

For fixed h,h' and child packet, check the least n>=A in the required residue
class. Every larger allowed n increases the right side by a nonnegative multiple
of16. Substitution of every literal residual output gives nonnegative margin;
there are1,088 fundamental output cases for +y, or4,352 across all four rotated
coordinate functionals. This is a finite table proof, not an extrapolation from
sampled C gaps. The table is generated by check_genealogy_coordinate_slack.py and
stored in chronology_dcuv/genealogy_coordinate_slack.json.

Consequently, along ANY finite auxiliary ancestry path,

3*N_non_UCC <= 4*(Delta C - Delta y)
                 + Phi(h_start)-Phi(h_end).         (2)

Rotating the coordinate frame by r quarter-turns replaces Delta y by the y
coordinate of the rotated displacement, and Phi(h) by Phi(h+r modulo4). In
particular every non-UCC regeneration charges coordinate-speed slack; the
endpoint potential error is at most6. No assumption about branches, dormant
lifetimes, or chronological activity of other roots is made.

Entry identities may also be included: give all of them virtual birth clock -4
and heading equal to the factor-entry heading. A child packet starting at C clock
b has heading h_entry-b modulo4, so its virtual-root gap b+4 has the correct
residue and is at least its A. The same finite edge inequality applies.

## Exact zero-slack ordering

For +y, equality n=d (spatial speed1 with no slack) is possible in this relaxation
ONLY in the following five output cases:

- DC: parent heading2 to child heading3, n=3, output(3,1) or(3,2).
- VC: parent heading3 to child heading0, n=3, output(2,5).
- UCC: parent heading0 to child heading0, n=4, output(-1,7) or(0,7).

Thus every maximal zero-slack ancestry segment has at most two initial non-UCC
edges, necessarily DC then VC when both occur, followed entirely by UCC edges
through those upper outputs. This is a literal ancestor-ordering restriction,
not a claim that the UCC transitions really occur. The other three directions
are exact rotations, not reflections.

The simpler consequence N_non_UCC<=3*slack+2 follows by splitting at positive
integer-slack edges. Inequality(2) is sharper: its asymptotic factor4/3 is optimal
for the limited age+heading+output-slot relaxation. The abstract heading cycle

## Remaining precise question

The sole recurrent zero-slack gate is UCC upper-output regeneration at exact
C age4 and equal producer/consumer packet heading. For a source UCC normalized at
(0,0), target UCC chosen input(0,3) has pose(-1,4,0) or(0,4,0), corresponding to
source output(-1,7) or(0,7). These two alignments survive the existing signed HB
and saturated count4 producer relation. That is OPEN, not physical feasibility.
A targeted finite literal control search is only discovery and gives no theorem
when it exhausts a packet-depth bound.

Even excluding these exact alignments would sharpen spatial speed rather than
by itself bound the total C count. A global capture theorem would still require
a connection to actual recurrent defects, all branches and complete white
contexts. Finite initial roots do not bound total descendants, and no dormant
branch is discarded here.

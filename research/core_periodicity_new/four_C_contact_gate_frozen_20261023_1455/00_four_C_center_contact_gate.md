## The conditional gate

At a chronologically first center self-contact with atomic ENTRY C-clock gap
four, normalize the earlier atom to (0, 0, N). Then its atom is D or C, and
the later atom is C at (-1, -1, N). Contact is at the earlier chord's start
and the later C chord's end. It is a straight collinear contact, not a proper
transverse crossing. This result concerns actual atomic cuts inside a complete
recurrent-eight factor, not arbitrary newly licensed atomic words.

The open gate is not an artifact: the complete factor

    DC, D, VC, VC, VCC

actually realizes the D-to-C case. Thus this theorem must not be promoted
to a no-four-C-center-contact result.

## Complete canonical geometry

Use the signed first-contact conventions: outgoing earlier chord parameter
0 <= s < 1 and incoming later chord parameter 0 < t <= 1. Relative heading
is N. The exact contact parallelograms give 23 canonical geometric cases.
For collinear chords, the first contact enters the earlier chord at its start:
otherwise the later traversal would already have touched that old chord at

The checker derives all integer head boxes from the exact parallelogram

The remaining V/U-first cases have a hard source-cohort output that is in

- U to D(-1, 1, N): use (0, 1).
- U to V(-1, -1, N): use (0, 0).

Each old identity must retire strictly before the later cut. The complete
1,725-option table checks every containing full source, age zero through four,
consumer and input slot. Every option either fails signed HB/count clauses,
is temporally impossible, creates an earlier NONADJACENT closed center-chord
contact, or cannot reach the target through the exact final zero-C semigroup.

The later cut may be INTERNAL C. Therefore an earlier consuming full packet

used only if the full exit really has the target clock and heading. Every
closed-contact option has at least two Cs since the earlier source, so an
ordinary shared endpoint of adjacent atoms is not falsely counted as a new
nonadjacent contact.

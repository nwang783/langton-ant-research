# A V-family base has only one parallel reproductive distinguished port

Scope: an actual recurrent-eight factor whose corrected atomic-center path is simple, in particular any retained factor beginning with U/UC/UCC. Let S be a genuine V, VC, or VCC producer, normalized to entry (0,0,N). Its three odd base output identities are

    A=(0,1), B=(1,0), C=(2,1).

The even base output r=(0,0) gives a causal lock:

- While the original A identity is current, original r is current.
- While the original B identity is current, original r is current.

Consequently A has no parallel or antiparallel distinguished-input consumer at all. B can have only D or DC at (1,-1,N), at exact full-source-entry C age four. Neither A nor B can supply the distinguished input of any parallel V-family or U-family packet.

Thus C=(2,1) is the ONLY possible base output that can supply a parallel reproductive distinguished input into V-family or U-family. Each genuine V-family base contributes at most one such reproductive identity, with actual once-only spending. The theorem does not assert that any C consumer is physically attained. C-tail outputs remain separate.

## The causal r lock

Enumerate every retained packet/input/heading alignment that could consume original r. Of the 276 alignments over all three source tail types, 138 have wrong sector and 129 meet a forbidden past source atomic chord. All true immediate full-pose adjacency exceptions are retained.

Only three plain consumer poses remain for each source:

| Consumer | Pose | A requirement | B requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| D | (1,0,W) | before-white | active core |
| V | (3,0,W) | before-white | before-white |
| U | (4,1,W) | before-white | before-white |

None consumes A or B as an input. Therefore the original r identity cannot retire while either original A or original B remains current. It also cannot retire simultaneously with such an A/B consumer: its own finite classification requires that sibling already absent before its packet.

A residual identity cannot silently enter the active core. This is the same current-residual/core partition and barrier principle used in the signed earlier context enumerations, not a static connectivity argument.

## Distinguished consumer inventory with r held

Use the actual signed roles: D/DC choose (0,1); V/VC choose (1,2); VCC chooses (0,3); U/UC/UCC choose (0,3). In particular VCC does not use the V/VC selected slot.

Enumerate A or B into these roles at heading N or S. There are 96 alignments across the three source tail types. The causal lock holds r current through every alleged original A/B consumer. Of the 96 alignments:

- 84 require r in the complete context, impossible while that r remains current and is not allowed to retire there;
- six meet a forbidden past source chord;
- six survive: B into D or DC at (1,-1,N), one pair per source tail type.

This argument is all-age and does not assume that A or B is the first source output to retire, nor that the rest of the V output cohort stays intact.

## Exact clocks

These clocks are additional port information, not needed for the coefficient-one reproductive bound.

For any possible r retirement, its single current physical edge closes the future center arc from source V entry E0=(-1,-1) to the consumer base exit L0=(1,1). The source complete atomic path meets that edge only at E0, and the consumer base only at L0. Atomic principal turn is

    n*pi/2 + alpha_consumer - alpha_V.

Closing turns total alpha_V-alpha_consumer-pi/2. Jordan turning therefore forces (n-1)*pi/2=+/-2*pi, whose only nonnegative solution is n=5.

For B into D/DC, use the current two-edge bridge

    Z=(3,-1) → L0=(1,1) → E0=(-1,-1),

consisting of original B and causally held original r. Source V/C tails meet it only at E0; the consumer D base meets it only at Z. Closing turns are pi/2, pi/2, and alpha_V-5*pi/4, totaling alpha_V-pi/4. Atomic turn is n*pi/2+pi/4-alpha_V. The total Jordan turn is n*pi/2, forcing n=4.

Both bridges retain actual source and consumer partial-tail interfaces; no historical edges or ghost connectors are used.

## Transport consequence and limitation

A source epoch with v genuine V-family bases has at most v direct base-identity contributions to parallel V/U distinguished-input reproduction, not 3v. The separate B-to-D/DC supply is nonreproductive and occurs only at source-entry C age four. The C port can also feed nonreproductive consumers, but an individual identity is spent only once.

This is a causal role-capacity theorem. It does not itself bound V counts, prove existence of an amplifying control, or close the global conjecture. Perpendicular transfers and the unique positive-C boundary packet's extra outputs require the separate epoch ledger.

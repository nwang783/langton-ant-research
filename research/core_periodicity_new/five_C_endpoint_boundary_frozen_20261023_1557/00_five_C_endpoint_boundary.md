# The remaining five-C D-to-C endpoint contact starts at factor entry

## Statement

corrected-center contact has the already normalized nonproper gate
D at (0, 0, N) to C at (0, −1, W), with atomic entry C gap five. Then the earlier
D base is the FIRST atom and first full packet of the factor. Its containing
full packet may be D or DC.

This is a boundary classification of one first-contact gate, not a ban on
arbitrary later five-C transfers. Combined with the separate complete 1550

The sixteen-cell control in 1550 realizes the remaining exception.

No next full packet after the later C is assumed. A later C can be internal,
and its unseen attached tail need not finish before the cut.

## Scope safeguards

The proof follows exact ordered residual identities, not merely cell colors.
A retired entry identity does not revive after recycling. It makes no fresh
root, disjoint helper, incidence-rank, bounded D-run, or fuel assertion.
Its earlier-contact rejections occur at nonadjacent actual atoms. Target C
and its containing full packet are not silently replaced by a completed
consumer tail. The first-contact premise is essential to the center-repeat
cuts, while the fresh-output and helper contradictions are literal history
obstructions on their own.

## Statement

factor. Normalize its head to(0,0,N), and suppose B is a later marked boundary,
with exactly THREE Cs from A included to B excluded. Thus B's heading is E.
If its head is(X,Y) with X<=0 and Y>=-1, then the exact current residual identity
at r=(1,-1), born in the packet IMMEDIATELY PRECEDING A, is STILL CURRENT
immediately before B. Its age there is3+c, where c is that predecessor's C count.

The result is invariant under translations and quarter-turns. B must be a real
marked boundary with its actual marker, not merely an unvalidated endpoint pose.
No target input identity, initial-origin condition or record premise is used.

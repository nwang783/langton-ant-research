# A U-family output cohort cannot have an all-cohort first consumer

Inside an actual complete recurrent-eight factor, let S be any genuine full U, UC, or UCC producer. Let R be its exact set of 9+3c fresh output identities, where c is its C-tail count. If a later full packet P consumes any identity from R, choose the first such P. Then P has at least one input identity outside R.

Equivalently, if the genealogy is reset at S's entry, so the component formed by S consists exactly of these fresh outputs, that component's first later touch must genuinely import an outside component. The literal outside-cohort statement does not require a reset. It does NOT imply a merger of arbitrary unrestarted historical components, because an identity outside this producer cohort may already be in the same historical component.

## Reduction to the six audited all-cohort gates

Assume every input of P belongs to R. All R identities are still current before P. The independently signed first-helper theorem leaves exactly two candidate poses per source, at source-entry C clocks 5+c:

- U: D or DC at (2,3,W).
- UC: D at (1,7,S), or V at (2,8,S).
- UCC: D at (-3,6,E), or V at (-4,7,E).

In the coordinate frame of S's full exit, these are D/DC at (2,-1,W) or V at (3,-2,W). In every case P begins five C units after S's full exit. The theorem below rules out all six candidates, not merely their all-cohort input assignment.

## An immediate-successor guard

Let F be the immediate full successor of S. Its entry is the origin with heading N in the source-full-exit frame.

If F is U-family, its fresh permanent left star meets the full context of P, contradicting permanent-star nonreading.

Otherwise F is D, DC, V, VC, or VCC. Every such packet creates the same genuine fresh base-output guard z=(2,0) in this frame. Its own C tails preserve z. The guard lies in P's full context but not among P's inputs, so this guard identity has to retire strictly before P. It cannot silently become an active-core cell or a before-white cell while current.

All 2,760 guard-consumer alignments are recorded, with the complete source cohort held current. Exact past and future atomic-chord filters preserve actual immediate full-pose adjacency exceptions. The rows divide as follows:

- 1,380 wrong sectors;
- 1,090 complete contexts meeting the unchanged source cohort;
- 36 forbidden past source/successor chord contacts;
- 58 forbidden future-P chord contacts;
- 25 U-family guard clearers whose own permanent left stars meet P's full context;
- 171 remaining low W-headed guard clearers.

Each of the 171 survivors has, in the source-full-exit frame, entry heading W, entry y in {0,1}, and full exit y at least one. The additional U-family rows removed by their future star occur only for UC/UCC V-helper cases, at relative pose (3,-1,N). Their left-star cell (3,-1) is in the future V helper's core. No new permanent island count or physical-area argument is used.

## Unbounded zero-C runs cannot clear the guard in time

Let k be the guard consumer's C clock relative to S's full exit. It begins before P at relative clock five, and its W heading forces k=1 modulo four. Hence k is one or five.

At clock one, a prefix from the source full exit consists of N-oriented zero-C packets, one DC/VC/UC turn, and W-oriented zero-C packets. Literal endpoints give positive N y increments, turning y increments at least two, and nonnegative W zero-C y increments. Every clock-one packet entry therefore has y at least two. This excludes all remaining guard clearer entries, whose y is zero or one.

At clock five, a positive-C guard consumer finishes too late. A zero-C clearer finishes W headed with y at least one. Subsequent zero-C W packets cannot decrease y: the D, V, and U y increments are one, one, and zero. Thus they cannot reach P at y=-1 or y=-2.

No guard retirement is possible. This contradicts either alleged all-cohort first helper. The first consumer of a genuine U-family output cohort must therefore import an identity outside that cohort.

## Limits

This is an exact cohort/port-history theorem, not a global U-chain birth budget. It does not say that the imported identity predates S, that it belongs to a previously unmerged component in the original genealogy, or that every later consumer has this import property. All such stronger statements remain open.

## Statement and prerequisites

In an actual complete recurrent-eight factor, suppose a U/UC/UCC middle-lower
input q=(-1,2) is a PRODUCED residual identity of source-included,
consumer-excluded C-age exactly3. Its producer must be the FIRST packet of that
factor. Equivalently, no complete recurrent-eight packet can precede that
producer within the same factor. This is an ordered-identity boundary theorem,
not a claim about arbitrary ant prehistory before the retained factor.

Use the signed LN classification: q and a=(-1,1) are born together in one
V/VC/VCC packet. In producer coordinates the source is(0,0,N), the consumer is
at(0,-1,E), and its lower inputs are original output identities(1,0),(2,0).
The three upper goal inputs are older. For VCC the signed1026 complete one-C
classification leaves only strict continuation VC,D. We retain all three U
consumer tails. Only the weaker JO input minima are used below; there are no
KV exact-four holes or stronger upper-five guards in the new shadow table.

## Consequences and limits

The LN classification fixes one unique target pose and one unique lower pair
for each of the three possible source packets. Thus a complete recurrent-eight
factor has AT MOST ONE U-family event with produced q-age3: its producer is the
first packet, and the event consumes those original two identities. Recycling
cannot create a second event with the same old identities.

The signed unfunded-U1/I>=28 exclusion follows a fortiori: source-first V/VC
requires initial residual inputs of activation34>target28; source-first VCC's
exit core ties the target. No new general input minimum is asserted without
retaining this factor-entry exception. Initial identities remain exempt.
No all-age U1 exclusion, V1 closure, nonreplenishing fuel, total-C bound or
capture theorem follows; funding coverage remains11/13.

## Explicitly omitted operation

At every packet boundary, remove ONLY its old five-cell core and install the
new chosen five-cell core, disjoint from the unchanged residual ledger.
The next head position is chosen freely, but its heading is NOT reset: the
actual packet C suffix fixes the next heading. Conserved sector is checked
at every chosen position. All796 endpoint-position junctions are broken.
Every real context-complete trajectory is a special case without those
position resets, so forgetting them is a conservative local relaxation.

Full concatenated-word chronology, ordinary all-packet pose kernels and the
global k6/k7 factor language are NOT asserted. They depend on physical head
transport. The checks below apply to every local packet and to every actual
consumed residual identity in this relaxation, not to unspecified global
literal histories.

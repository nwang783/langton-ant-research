# A foreign-output shadow blocks every named D-rectangle crossing

Status: author frozen, pending independent review. This is a named-template result, not arbitrary-tail simplicity.

Normalize the first postforeign D entry to (0,0,N). For integers a>=0,b>=1, let

    P(a,b)=D^(a+1) C D^b C D^(a+1) C D^b V.

The final V starts at (-1,0,E), independently of a,b. Its chord (-3,1) to (3,-1) crosses the first D chord (-1,-1) to (1,1) at strict parameters1/2,1/2. This is a proper-crossing candidate, not an exact four-C pose return. The a=0,b=1 body is the retained prepared DCV crossing control T10. That control remains real without a foreign predecessor.

No actual F/Q/B can be followed by any P(a,b), on any physical board or with any earlier history. The reason is a genuine new last-R identity produced by the foreign, not old-eight maturation:

* Q produces its local(-3,-2) by own RAW R57/read58. Relative to its postforeign D entry this is g=(-1,-2).
* B produces its local(-2,-3) by own RAW R67/read68, at the same normalized g=(-1,-2).
* F produces its local(-2,-1) by own RAW R47/read48. Its normalized shadow is gF=(-2,-1).

Each is an actual foreign full output with no later foreign read. All are outside the postforeign core. The proof follows the SAME last-R identity through literal physical reads; no external/core status is silently equated with physical death.

## Exact all-length stage bounds

The D/C RAW footprints have x in[-1,2], D y in[-1,1], and C y in[-1,2]. The normalized stage heads are exactly:

1. N D rail: (j,j), 0<=j<=a.
2. C1: (a+1,a+1,N).
3. W D rail: (a+1-j,a+2+j), 0<=j<=b-1.
4. C2: (a+1-b,a+2+b,W).
5. S D rail: (a-b-j,a+b+2-j), 0<=j<=a.
6. C3: (-b-1,b+1,S).
7. E D rail: (-b-1+j,b-j), 0<=j<=b-1.
8. Final V: (-1,0,E).

Every physical read before final V has y>=-1. Thus Q/B's g_y=-2 is never read and remains BLACK. Final V first reads g at own R8/read9, an immutable contradiction.

For F, the N rail has x>=-1 and cannot touch gF_x=-2. Every subsequent stage before the last E-facing D has y>=0 and cannot touch gF_y=-1. That last D starts at(-2,1,E), and its first visit to gF is own R8/read9. The shadow is again BLACK and the requested R is impossible.

These affine inequalities include every RAW read of every stage at every nonnegative count and every permitted moving index. Following-marker reads are not omitted: they are actual later RAW reads already included in the physical word. The contradictions themselves are inside D/V RAW, before any final marker.

## Controls and limitations

The checker retains 216 exact finite regressions over a=0..5,b=1..6 with no prefix or a plain creating-U prefix. The all-length theorem comes from the affine read bounds, not extrapolation from those samples. It separately replays both complete genuine U-entry positive prefixes UQVCCV and UBVCCV, whose fourth V takes a postforeign original V identity into CORE and deletes it at the actual following marker. They remain positive; their failed extensions are not silently called captured or simple forever.

Twelve prescribed A,VCC,VCC controls with possible creating U/UC/UCC prefixes are retained with exact failure owners. The Q/B failure is the second VCC's SECOND C tick8/read9, not its first C. Exact four-C full-pose nonreturn in a complete G8 factor was already signed as1344; these are regressions, not a renamed proof of that theorem.

Mixed V/U rails can reach below the D-only y cone and may retire or rewrite the shadow. They are not excluded. This result supplies a necessary foreign-output restoration requirement for a larger proper-crossing return map, not a budget, general barrier or arbitrary-tail simplicity theorem.

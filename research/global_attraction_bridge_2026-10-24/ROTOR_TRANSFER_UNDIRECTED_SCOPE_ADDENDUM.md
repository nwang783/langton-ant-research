# External theorem graph-scope clarification

24 October 2026. This addendum preserves the frozen rotor-transfer package.

Angel and Holroyd's *Recurrent rotor-router configurations* assumes an infinite, connected, simple, undirected, locally finite graph throughout. Its finite-perturbation invariance and reflecting-boundary statements are used in the frozen note only to identify a tempting **mechanism comparison**, not as theorems already applicable to the directed checkerboard graph of the ant. Any proposed conjugacy invoking those statements must check their graph hypotheses too.

The selected Florescu–Levine–Peres excursion theorem is explicitly formulated for directed Eulerian graphs. Its graph premise genuinely matches the ant's directed checkerboard sector; its simple cyclic exit mechanism is precisely the premise refuted by the literal controls.

This does not change the algebra, full-field examples, infinite 108-window obstruction, or their independent audit. No external recurrence theorem is asserted for the ant.

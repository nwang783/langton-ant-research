# A closed two-mode scattering class with exact future-relevant geometry

24 October 2026. Author proof and complete finite controls; independent audit pending.

## Result and scope

A close packet of four original inputs returns to the same fresh standard ray after a nontrivial 10,712-read scattering. It composes with the retained isolated-input rule. For every finite word in these two packet types, with every free-flight parameter at least 100, the complete finite initial board has the prescribed sequence of scatterings and then a canonical104 tail. An arbitrary finite archive strictly west of the source's entire future footprint can be retained.

The new content is an exact causal invariant, not just concatenation of turn words. At every packet entry the ENTIRE actually future-read current black field is exactly one of two fixed pruned kernels, of radii 5 and 22, together with the still-unread ORIGINAL packet inputs. All older generated debris is permanently separated by an advancing physical western boundary. Consequently the sampled quantity max(rho−22,0) strictly decreases at every nonterminal entry while positive. Here rho is the radius of the entire actual future-relevant cohort, not a local crop or a predicted template.

This is a closed sufficient class with finite control and unbounded finite pending-input geometry. It is not a classification of arbitrary ray inputs, a finite-state representation of all full boards, an autonomous input-regeneration scheme, or a universal attraction theorem. Every packet is paid for in the initial seed. The exploratory 32-node causal tree that suggested this mode remains incomplete and is not a coverage premise.

## 5. Exact evolution of the ENTIRE future-relevant entry field

Let t_j be the cut just BEFORE the first read of packet j. Let U_j be all original packet cells whose first reads are at or after t_j, including the current packet. Let C*(t_j) mean all current BLACK lifetimes that are actually read at some later update. Then

    C*(t_j) = (q_j+C_(e_j)) union U_j.                 (1)

Both inclusions need proof. Every initial comparison-kernel member in C_e is consumed before the end of its recovery, by106 or7448, and the actual recovery is identical. Every U_j cell is consumed at its certified packet read. Conversely, a comparison-kernel bit outside C_e is never read by that reference's complete future. Before the next packet the actual/reference words coincide. At the next guard all cells of the previous initial kernel and its complete output lie strictly behind that guard, and all later guards advance. Thus a reference-unread initial bit cannot become relevant after the next scattering. Every older actual archive cell is already behind the current guard and stays forever unread. This proves (1) without assuming that “irrelevant under one comparison” implies “irrelevant under arbitrary scattering.” The advancing guards supply exactly that missing implication for this class.

In particular there is no free restarting of a stock budget. Newly born reference inputs are allowed and fully represented, but their support is in one of the two certified local kernels. The only unbounded geometry at an entry is the fixed original pending packet cohort.

Let F be the largest x-coordinate of ANY original packet cell, fixed once at the initial seed. Since every packet lies on the same diagonal, (1) gives exactly

    rho(t_j) = max(r_(e_j), F−q_j.x),
    r_S=5, r_M=22.

Consequently the sampled geometric quantity

    V_j=max(rho(t_j)−22,0)=max(F−q_j.x−22,0)

satisfies the exact transition law

    V_(j+1)=max(V_j−(q_(j+1).x−q_j.x),0).

Every nonterminal head increment is at least206. Thus V strictly decreases whenever positive, by at least min(206,V_j), and is zero at the last entry. This is not a claim that rho decreases at every fine update, nor that whole relevant cardinality decreases: for example the actual SMMS control changes its relevant entry counts23 ->131 ->127 ->14 while its radii662 ->456 ->228 ->5 decrease. The causal geometry, rather than raw relevant mass, closes this particular class.

The mathematical all-word/all-gap closure uses the signed source and the explicit guard induction above, not sampled words or a claim that the causal tree is exhaustive. The new result shows that some genuinely close multi-input scattering can be added to a recursively closed class without allowing its older generated dependency to grow. It does not show that arbitrary close packets enter these modes. Changing another exposed input may leave the guard, the transverse ray class, or canonical behavior; those are new proof obligations, not licensed transitions.

The NW terminal pair class from the preceding signed package may not be appended freely to the arbitrary western archive allowed here: that change of normal needs its separate southern-field restriction. This theorem uses NE modes only. The existence of a finitely controlled sufficient class and its sampled potential gives no universal occurrence or global capture conclusion.

These are whole finite-field source stages already independently certified, not a hypothetical word imposed on a cleared region. The checker also reconstructs the full historical last-exit edge and its two true R identities in all three supplied whole histories.

## What this changes, and what it does not

The contrast is exact: the completed-drought tree has every selected edge jump<=29865, while the whole historical tree has a selected adjacent-cell jump tending to infinity across the same family. Resetting a temporal forest can erase the old-to-new connection whose latency one hoped to charge.

This is not a contradiction to the interval theorem. It is not a bound on the maximum whole-history jump, only a proved unbounded lower witness. It does not prove that unbounded consumed lifetime ages universally force such connectors, nor that a single nontranslating orbit must regenerate them infinitely often. Every family member captures.

Any proposed global chronological invariant must specify whether it retains the full historical forest, a chosen interval tree, or only a future-relevant part. These are different objects even when the complete actual black field is retained throughout.

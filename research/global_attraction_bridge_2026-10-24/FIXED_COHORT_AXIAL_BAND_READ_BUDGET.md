## 1. Statement

At any chosen cut T of an actual finite-board trajectory, fix ONCE the entire currently black lifetime cohort C0. Let c3 be the update consuming its third distinct member, or infinity if that never happens. Let J be ANY fixed band consisting of w consecutive physical columns, or w consecutive physical rows, where w>=1.

The number of actual read updates t with T<=t<c3 and p_t in J is at most

    B(w) = 270504 + 312w.                                (1)

In particular the bounds are 270816 for one coordinate line and 317304 for a 150-line band. Every return and repeated physical read is counted, not just distinct sites, crossings or strict records.

There is NO pristine-half-plane, record, capture-guard avoidance, relevant-mass-cap or active-component hypothesis. The band is fixed in physical coordinates throughout this one cohort comparison. This is not an elapsed-time bound: arbitrarily long flights can take place outside the chosen band.

## 2. The sharp 52w contact bound for one diagonal104 piece

Consider any finite or infinite repeated 104-turn piece whose period restores the heading and has displacement (plus/minus2,plus/minus2). This includes all genuine canonical pieces in the signed source decomposition. Start the piece at its actual pose; a cyclic phase shift just changes its 104 base positions Q_i.

The ant alternates horizontal and vertical moves. Therefore, in any 104 consecutive read positions, exactly 52 have even x and 52 have odd x; the same holds for y. The coordinate parity sequence consists of repeating two-even/two-odd blocks, with a possible cyclic shift, because each horizontal move flips x parity and each vertical move leaves it unchanged.

For a vertical band, phase i follows Q_i.x+k*d_x, where d_x is +2 or -2 and k ranges over consecutive nonnegative copy indices. Each eligible column can occur at most once for this phase, and must have Q_i.x's parity. If the band has w_even even columns and w_odd odd columns, its total reads are consequently at most

    52*w_even + 52*w_odd = 52w.

The argument for horizontal bands is identical. Truncating at either end cannot increase the count. The coefficient is sharp for an infinite canonical piece and a band sufficiently far in its direction of travel: every parity-compatible phase visits every eligible column exactly once.

More generally, an actual word decomposed into arbitrary pieces of total length L and s such diagonal104 pieces has at most L+52sw reads in any fixed axial band, regardless of the lengths or positions of the periodic pieces.

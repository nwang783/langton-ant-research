Therefore any initial board producing the same precontact word has exactly the same color at the contact cut on every cell previously read. It may differ at unread cells, but not at the four witnesses above. The desired pure continuation also determines its complete read path. Hence the desired concatenated word is impossible, regardless of how many unread initial cells are altered.

This is an obstruction to that exact causal concatenation. It is not a claim that no different trajectory can prepare or clear the interface.

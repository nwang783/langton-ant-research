## 1. The necessary moving-band correction

Use the premises of the signed fixed-band return-packing lemma, except that return i uses the translated band J_i=J+z_i and its permanent output on that band is contained in S_i=S+z_i, where S is one fixed finite physical-cell shape. The band still has w>M lines and every chosen return strictly crosses its own band outbound and back. Assume the ENTIRE relevant cohort has size at most M throughout the interval under consideration.

Then the correct bound is

    4N(w-M) <= M * |union_i (S+z_i)|,                    (1)

not M|S|. If the return intervals are pairwise disjoint, remove the outer M on the right.

The proof is the same actual-lifetime accounting as before. Each return pays at least 4(w-M) permanent births on its own band. A physical permanent birth is charged at most M times, because every charging marked lifetime is alive and belongs to C* at that update. Different permanent births occupy different PHYSICAL cells. They need not occupy different cells after applying a different translation to each episode.

For any ordering of the frame offsets,

    |union_i (S+z_i)|
      <= |S| + sum_{i=2}^N |(S+z_i) minus (S+z_{i-1})|.  (2)

Indeed newly available cells at step i are outside the union of all earlier sets, and hence outside the immediately preceding set. This gives an explicit boundary-migration charge rather than an unlicensed conservation of normalized capacity.

For an axis-aligned w-by-W rectangle, displacement (dx,dy) creates exactly

    wW - max(0,w-|dx|)*max(0,W-|dy|)

new possible cells relative to its preceding translate, at most W|dx|+w|dy|. Equations (1)--(2) therefore imply

    4N(w-M) <= M * [wW + W sum|dx_i| + w sum|dy_i|].      (3)

For temporally disjoint return intervals the leading M on the right is again omitted. At zero frame displacement this reduces to fixed physical capacity. A recurrent normalized pattern alone says nothing about the added migration term.

These statements do not establish that arbitrary returns fit a fixed transverse shape S or that their frame displacement is controlled. They identify the precise resource a translating-frame localization theorem would have to preserve or pay for.

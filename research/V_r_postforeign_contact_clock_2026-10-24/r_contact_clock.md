## Portable conditional lemma

Suppose its SAME original r output is CURRENT at foreign entry and A's COMPLETE RAW plus actual following M11 context MISSES that cell. Append any finite ordinary RAW tail over D/C/V/U. This is not a postforeign input-age theorem. It is a conditional held-edge Jordan clock. In particular, if the true source-to-foreign age is at least four, that SAME r cannot lose CURRENT status before a global first contact. It is still current at the ENTRY of any such first-contact atom; nothing is asserted through that atom.

## Limits

The portable clock is conditional on genuine preforeign r survival, complete foreign MISS, and simplicity through a proposed loss atom. It does not preserve an altered V cohort, assume r survives a foreign which reads it, or assert any postforeign maturation. The THIRD application closes only the stated old-U/S-facing-V pose and its n3/4 interfaces, not all old-U contacts or full third-step simplicity.

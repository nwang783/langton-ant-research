# Clean transverse standard-wake crossings

Date: 22 October 2026. These are exact restricted scattering statements, not the
arbitrary-finite-seed highway conjecture. The complete state and the source of
each infinite background are explicit. An all-parameter finite-support transfer
is proved separately below; an infinite-background calculation is not promoted
by itself into a finite-seed conclusion. No novelty or external-review claim.

## 1. Conventions and exact background

Use white/right R, black/left L, flip and move. Headings N,E,S,W are 0,1,2,3.
The supplied standard.json has word W of length104, initial heading3 (west),
initial head0 and drift d=(-2,-2). Let p_i be its104 pre-update read positions.
The finite13cell canonical seed is called C. Both C and the infinite mature past
below are independently checked to execute W forever.

The mature incoming field at time0 is the XOR of every past standard write:

    H0 = XOR_{0<=i<104} XOR_{j<0} {p_i+j*d}.

Every fixed cell occurs only finitely many times in this expression. Its black
indicator is well defined. It is a backward-infinite deposited wake with its
active head at0, west; it is NOT a finite initial state.

Let R(x,y)=(y,-x). For r=1 or3, the complete settled bilateral wake of orientation
r is

    T_r = XOR_{0<=i<104} XOR_{j in Z} {R^r p_i+j*R^r d}.

Partitioning by the full drift gives12 odd translation classes, hence12 disjoint
black lines b+Z*e, where e=R^r d. This is an infinite static old rail.

For a in{0,1}, s in{0,1,2,3}, use offset

    v(a,s,n)=(a,s-a-4n),
    X(r,a,s,n)=H0 XOR (T_r+v(a,s,n)), head0, west.

The sixteen stored reference cases have n=20. The full classification at this
point refers to these specified clean backgrounds and their mature translates,
not arbitrary old wakes or arbitrary finite supports.

## 2. Exact mature normalization

The lattice generated by d=(-2,-2) and e=(+/-2,-/+2) has index8. Its cosets are
exactly the pair (x mod2, x+y mod4), represented by a,s. Translating an old rail
by e leaves its entire settled field unchanged. Translating it by d is equivalent
to one more undisturbed period of incoming transport, provided the encounter is
still in the mature pre-contact regime.

More explicitly, H0 XOR {writes of one W period}=H0+d. Also
v(a,s,n+1)-v(a,s,n)=(0,-4), which differs from d by one static-rail period up to
sign. Consequently the complete contact fields at different n are translates,
not just matching local samples.

An independent exact phase/line determinant enumeration gives the first contacts
at time104n+c. For r1, c for s0,1,2,3 is -257,-266,-267,-360; for r3 it is
-361,-370,-371,-464. These are independent of a. A common mature domain is n>=5.
There every candidate incoming phase-line intersection has nonnegative epoch,
so the earliest one genuinely is the first change from the standard input.
This statement does not cover arbitrary offsets already intersecting the active
head or the previously traversed incoming geometry at time0.

Every mature clean transverse encounter has one of these eight offset cosets
for each of the two chiral orientations. Rotations and choice of a whole-period
phase-zero boundary transfer the statement to other incoming compass directions.
The independent normalization checker/proof is recorded in the audits directory.

## 4. All-future verification, not repetition heuristics

Fields are stored as XORs of periodic lines/rays and a finite set of flipped
cells. A direct per-read parity replay checks the optimized representation on
every read of each stored recovery word.

For a proposed infinite period, its phase read q+k*d_out, k>=0, intersects each
old line/ray in an empty set, singleton or interval of epochs. Parallel rays are
clipped intervals; nonparallel intersections are exact integer determinants.
Each finite flip contributes one epoch at most. Earlier copies of the new period
contribute threshold half-lines, with intra-period order explicitly included.
The mismatch function is therefore an XOR of interval indicators. Checking that
its boundary set is empty proves the exact color at every epoch. Heading closure
and nonzero displacement are checked from the word. RLRL is used rather than
RL when invoking this checker so all full drifts have component magnitudes2.

Every same-sector result also passes a separate finite-kernel extraction. The
checker intersects the ORIGINAL infinite black background with the whole certified
future read set, including all infinite tail phase rays. This intersection is
finite in all eight cases. Its complete finite set K is then replayed with the
older independent set simulator and finite-field infinite-tail oracle, which
accept both the finite recovery and eternal standard output. No removed black
cell is ever read. In the eight RL cases this intersection instead has infinite
black phase rays, recording the infinite initial fuel needed by those outputs.

## 5. Uniform finite-support transfer

This is stronger and more geometric than selecting a finite kernel after the
fact. For each same-sector reference, let its twelve static black lines be
b+Z*e as above at n20. For integers k>=0 and M>=0, define the COMPLETE finite input

    B(k,M)= C union {b+k*d+j*e : b one of twelve bases, -M<=j<=M}.

All these black cells are installed at time0, head0 west. The two sets are
disjoint: the old rail has coordinate sum near -80-4k, whereas C is near0.
The input has exactly13+12(2M+1) black cells. Increasing k moves the whole old
rail farther down the incoming highway; increasing M makes it longer in both
directions. There is no recoloring or marker installation during the run.

**Finite crossing theorem.** Every one of the eight same-sector inputs B(k,M)
with k>=22 and M>=38 executes the same reference recovery, shifted by104k updates
and k*d, and then its certified standard output forever. Per-class sufficient
bounds are smaller:

### Incoming-gap guard

Put bback=-d=(2,2), and D0=H0 XOR C. In the normalized frame of the reference
crossing, the discrepancy caused by starting with C rather than an infinite
incoming past is exactly

    D0+k*bback.

This follows algebraically from cancellation of the common standard flight
writes; no approximation by a mature wake is made.

For each full-drift translation class c, let m_c be the lowest longitudinal
coordinate of a black cell of D0, if that class has any. D0 is bounded below
in every class, and these minima are obtained by exact XOR interval boundaries.
The following elementary support-union identity is the useful reusable lemma:

    union_{k>=K} support(D0+k*bback)
      = union_c {c+z*bback : z>=m_c+K}.

The union is an ordinary support union, NOT an XOR sum. The least support point
m_c supplies every larger longitudinal coordinate as k varies. Thus avoiding
these half-rays proves invisibility of every sufficiently delayed incoming cap.

The checker tests all finite recovery reads and all infinite tail phase rays
against these class half-rays. For opposite-direction parallel tails the largest
longitudinal coordinate is the first one; transverse intersections are finite
and solved exactly. For same-direction parallel tails, the relevant class must
be absent. The northeast-output case indeed lies on different transverse classes
from the incoming discrepancy, so it does not later run into the omitted cap.
The resulting largest required K is22.

### Finite-old-rail guard

Independently intersect EVERY prescribed read with EACH original static black
line, not merely with their net XOR background. This matters when two backgrounds
cancel at a crossing cell. All static indices j touched anywhere in the finite
recovery or infinite tail form a finite interval within[-M0,M0]. The largest
M0 is38. Thus deleting all farther old-rail cells changes no actual read, and
adding any further cells of that same rail changes none either.

First-difference induction combines the two guards. The complete true finite
field has exactly the prescribed colors on every future read, so it follows the
reference recovery and output for every k>=K and M>=M0. The smallest protected
finite input in each class is additionally replayed literally and its full endpoint
passes the independent finite-field tail oracle. That replay is a control, not
the source of the all-parameter quantifier.

## 6. Compositional significance and unresolved side effects

The clean crossing map is not simply “continue through.” One compatible class
turns northwest, another reverses the incoming direction and exits northeast.
Thus there is no monotone incoming-direction progress bound supplied by this
map alone. Histories that contain extra black debris on recovery reads can also
change the outcome entirely; a pair of local highway directions is not a complete
interface.

The certificates do show precisely how to transfer a local mature model to
complete finite incoming/old rails, and which outgoing ray guards must be kept.
They do NOT prove that arbitrary real trajectories split into clean crossings,
that all scars are permanently retired, or that the graph of later crossings is
finite or well founded. Recoveries add finite black debris, which is harmless
for the certified outgoing future but may be active on a later return. Those
unaccounted return interactions are exactly the remaining closure obligation.

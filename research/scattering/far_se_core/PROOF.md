## The scoped theorem

Assume the established fixed-pose, five-cell recurrence and incidence lemmas:
a putative recurrent mass-five sequence has one fixed local black cell s and
four escaping remote black cells, with one of the reviewed four-cycle or
four-edge shapes. Then s cannot satisfy u(s)=x(s)-y(s)>=502.

This is an exclusion of an entire half-plane of possible fixed local cores,
not just of the displayed affine rectangle seeds. It does not prove that
mass six is eventually attained, and does not settle the arbitrary-finite-
seed highway conjecture.

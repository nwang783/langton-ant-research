# An all-length TWO-C cleanup endpoint guard

Author certificate, pending independent review. This is a conditional endpoint guard, not a classification of all A losses or arbitrary-tail simplicity.

## Actual interface

Let an actual complete-eight interval be followed by its FIRST complete F/Q/B and an immediate ordinary V. Normalize this first returned V to (0,0,N). Allow any finite ordinary D/C/V/U connector, with exactly TWO C atoms, that reaches a further V at the exact relative entry (0,4,S). There is no bound on the lengths or orders of its zero-C runs. No other foreign occurs in this connector.

The claim is that the stipulated final V cannot be followed by any next complete seven-atom D/C/V/U/F/Q/B RAW plus its actual next M11. The final V itself may have a legal RAW and actual next-M11 prefix, and its original A may physically die there through CORE. This is not a claim that all erosion is terminal.

## All-length support instead of endpoint enumeration

The unique two-C decomposition has N, W and S zero-C stages.

- After the first V, head=(1,3,N). All N zero-C motion increases y. Every N RAW and the first C RAW read at y≥2.
- The first C raises head y by one and turns W. Every W head has y≥4. W D/V increase y and U preserves it. Every W RAW, including the second C, reads at y≥3.
- After the second C, heading is S. Each completed zero-C S base preceding the stipulated helper V must EXIT with y≥4, because remaining S motion cannot increase y. Relative to its actual EXIT, every D/V/U RAW read has nonnegative y. Thus all those S RAW reads occur at y≥4.
- The final helper V at (0,4,S) has every RAW read at y≥1.

The checker verifies all 56 zero-C RAW read offsets, all 15 C offsets, and all 18 helper V offsets. Run length and word order do not enter these inequalities. Actual executed marker prefixes between atoms are parts of those consecutive RAW atoms, not omitted extra reads. Only the final helper's following actual M11 is left to the next-context obligations.

## Preserved negative-halfplane boundary

The first V's entry-core holes (-1,0) and (-1,-1), deleted by its own L1/L2, remain physically WHITE. Exact foreign shadows at y≤0 retain their SAME R-born black identities. The mandatory immediate U before a first B supplies a further WHITE old-U core hole at (-2,-1) in the first-V chart. That hole was U L1-deleted; B and the first V miss it.

No intervening connector or final helper RAW can touch any of these guard cells. All CORE operations are included: a color repair inside CORE would still be an actual RAW read, and the support inequalities exclude it at these cells. This is not residual-only accounting.

The first V's original A=(0,1) is untouched by the connector (y≥2), and the helper V RAW misses it. It is alive in helper EXIT CORE, not a CURRENT residual output. Its physical next-word read2 deletion remains real and is not used as a white-hole substitute.

## Twenty-one direct next-context contradictions

At the helper EXIT (-1,1,S), reconstruct each actual next D/C/V/U/F/Q/B context plus M11 directly. Every first guard demand conflicts with its preserved color:

- D marker L11 needs the first V's WHITE (-1,0) hole BLACK.
- U/F/Q/B own RAW L12 needs its WHITE (-1,-1) hole BLACK.
- C and V need specific still-BLACK foreign shadows WHITE, except the prepared B→V case.
- The B→V case instead needs the genuine immediate preforeign U's WHITE core hole BLACK at V own L13.

The actual complete-eight-to-first-B relation has only plain U as predecessor, checked over all eight full types. Thus the last guard is genuine, not an assumed prepared color. Some additional RAW targets can complete and fail only at their following M11; their physical owners are recorded explicitly.

This recasts the fixed V,C,C,V erosion control as a whole endpoint class with arbitrarily long ordinary rails. It does not assert that every first loss of original A reaches this endpoint, and it does not exclude other cleanup endpoints, more than two Cs, another foreign, or a different proxy source.

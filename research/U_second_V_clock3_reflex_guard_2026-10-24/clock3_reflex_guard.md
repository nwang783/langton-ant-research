# Two clock-three first-contact loops cannot enclose the actual old U half

Status: author geometric proof, pending independent review. Only two exact chronological FIRST new-contact states are covered.

Assume the second V's chronological FIRST new proxy contact is with the old U, as in the necessary frontier. The contact is its terminal point `B0=(-1,3)`, the old U's strict midpoint, with source parameter1/2 and consumer parameter1. No age or identity law is used in this proof.

## A genuine Jordan loop and its reflex corner

Take the old proxy from B0 along the remaining old U and all subsequent actual old/foreign/first-C pieces, then the second V back to B0. Simplicity through the first returned atom is signed. The chronological FIRST-contact premise excludes a second-V intersection before B0. Thus this actual B0-to-B0 subcurve is a Jordan loop.

Let `beta=atan(1/3)`, strictly between0 and pi/4. The old U tangent is N. The second V tangent is SW, angle `3pi/2-beta`. The true total ordinary C count is4: three before the foreign and one in the first returned RAW C. The atom immediately preceding the foreign is not C by the signed actual complete-eight/foreign adjacency table. Therefore the exact repaired-proxy lift gives open turn

`(4+2)pi/2 + alpha_V-alpha_U = 3pi-beta`.

The closing turn at B0 is `-pi+beta`. Total turn is `+2pi`, so the loop is CCW.

At B0, the outgoing old-U ray is N `(0,1)`. The reverse incoming V ray is NE `(1,3)`. The CCW bounded interior is the reflex sector of angle `2pi-beta`, not the small NE sector. The actual reverse old-U ray `(0,-1)` lies strictly inside this sector.

The incoming **actual old-U half-chord**, from B0 downward to old U start `(-1,-1)`, initially lies strictly inside. It cannot touch or cross the rest of the loop: another old/old or old/foreign/first-return intersection contradicts signed prior simplicity, and an intersection with the second V before its terminal B0 contradicts chronological FIRST contact. Hence the whole half-chord, including the genuine old U start, lies strictly in the bounded interior. There is no omitted endpoint-touch or incoming-segment exception.

## Whole-loop vertical support, including every intervening old stage

At true preforeign clock3 with foreign heading E, all intervening old atomic phases after the creating U are N0, W1, S2, E3. These are actual zero-C runs with their real connecting C atoms; zero-length phases and C tails are allowed.

- The source U remainder has doubled y at least3.
- After that U base, N0 heads have physical y at least4; their corrected centers have doubled y at least7. N-heading zero-C D/V/U do not lower y.
- After C1, W1 heads have y at least5, corrected doubled y at least9. W-heading zero-C atoms do not lower y.
- From the W-to-S C2 onward through S2 and the S-to-E C3, y is nonincreasing toward the E3 stage. Every S2 and E3 center has doubled y at least `2*y_A+1`, because E3 heads cannot lie below the later foreign entry head y_A. The C3 endpoints obey the same bound. This proves the bound for the **whole intervening path**, not just its endpoints.

The actual repaired foreign and returned pieces are:

A point strictly below every point of a Jordan loop is in its unbounded component, since its downward ray reaches infinity without meeting the loop. It cannot be the strict bounded-interior point forced above.

This contradiction excludes both exact states.

## Limits

This is not an assertion that enclosure alone is contradictory. The contradiction uses the exact incoming old-U half, the correct reflex side, and a proved whole-loop support bound. It does not transfer that bound to unrelated earlier clocks or use any historical identity wire. It excludes only these two chronological FIRST-contact configurations, not arbitrary later contacts or returned-path self-contacts.

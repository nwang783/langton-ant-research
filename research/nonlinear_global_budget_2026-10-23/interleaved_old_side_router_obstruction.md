# Alternating ports obstruct disjoint-support old-side routing stations

## Narrow statement

The five-band wave suggests a sufficient, but unconstructed, finite-supply
itinerary: route the original reverse arrival into endpoint type A, then
A's reverse arrival into type B, then B's reverse into channel(12,1).
Complete local two-corner controls do NOT automatically compose into this
itinerary merely by placing their stations successively farther toward
the old side. Already the FIRST TWO required routes have alternating ports,
so disjoint old-side routing paths are impossible.

This is a topological obstruction to an independent-station/blank-background
proof, not a theorem that the actual router is impossible. It neither
forces a specific bad turn nor assigns a quantitative K charge to a
crossing. A physical crossing transient might still implement the router.

## Ports in the exact endpoint gauge

Use transverse coordinate t=x-y and longitudinal coordinate u=x+y. The
remote endpoint is toward decreasing u, the old side toward increasing u.
The relevant complete standard READ strips are, in increasing t order:

    A's reverse input at old side: [-72,-62]   (route2 input)
    A's original outward input:   [-40,-30]   (route1 output)
    B's original outward input:   [ -2,  8]   (route2 output)
    original exceptional reverse: [ 42, 52]   (route1 input).

These four bands are disjoint. Longitudinal translation by any multiple
of e=(2,2) changes u but leaves this strict transverse order unchanged.
Exact phase0 pose normals can differ from strip centers, but cannot alter
the port ordering.

Suppose the two claimed compact old-side routing reactions can be placed
behind one cut u=U, with all their nonstandard/turning segments in u>U,
and exactly their two incoming/outgoing clean legs crossing that cut. Impose the explicit DISJOINT-SUPPORT independent-
station hypothesis: their
complete head/read-path traces are disjoint in that old-side halfplane.
This is a sufficient separation device one might propose for reusing local
controls, NOT a necessary consequence of the phrase no mutual interaction.
Read traces can overlap while their actual colors remain compatible; the
topological lemma alone does not rule out that possibility.

## Elementary alternating-endpoint lemma

Let four distinct boundary points of a closed halfplane appear in order
b2,a1,b1,a2. Any compact connected paths in the halfplane, one joining
a1 to a2 and the other joining b1 to b2, must intersect, provided their
interiors avoid the boundary. The same holds for ports in four disjoint
ordered boundary intervals.

Proof. If the two paths were disjoint, remove self-loops from the first to
obtain a simple arc with the same endpoints. This arc, together with the
finite boundary interval between its endpoints, is a Jordan curve. Exactly
one endpoint of the second path lies on that boundary interval. Push the
boundary interval an arbitrarily small distance outside the halfplane to
avoid the second endpoints. The two second endpoints then lie on opposite
sides of the resulting simple closed curve; the second path, whose interior
stays in the halfplane, must cross the first arc. Contradiction. Equivalently
use the standard crosscut separation theorem for a disk after compactifying
a sufficiently large containing half-disk.

Choose route1 endpoints in the second and fourth displayed bands, and
route2 endpoints in the first and third. They alternate, so the lemma
applies. Increasing station2's u coordinate does not solve this: its later
outbound SW standard leg must cross the earlier connector somewhere.
Moving it earlier merely relocates the unavoidable overlap. A deformation
with more pristine quarter-turn corners cannot remove the port alternation.

## What must be checked instead

At least one of the following must enter a valid physical construction:

- a verified crossing interaction with the COMPLETE earlier settled trail
  and passive anchor debris retained;
- a single jointly certified cap implementing both routes, not independent
  blank controls;
- a different endpoint history whose port pairing is nonalternating;
- an explicitly accounted additional corridor excursion or boundary port,
  outside the two-compact-reaction hypothesis.

Intersection by itself does not prove an ant defect: an earlier visit may
leave a white cell, and histories and turn phases matter. Conversely a
local corner's terminal guard only checked against its supplied field; it
cannot be silently reused after another connector writes across it. This
precisely identifies the missing hypothesis in the suggested progressive-
NE construction, without treating that suggestion as a global theorem.

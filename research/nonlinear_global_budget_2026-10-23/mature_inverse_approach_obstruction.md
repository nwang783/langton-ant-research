# Mature inverse-standard perpendicular approaches cannot reach the1564 launch strip cleanly

## The proposed shortcut and the exact boundary

One possible way to prepare the1564 wave from its alternative last SW
writer is to work backwards. Preserve every initial color on the ENTIRE
future1564 READ set, but modify colors on future-unread cells. A finite
backward prelude ending in a certified inverse-H NE tail would reverse
into a genuine forward standard-H ancestry.

The171 retained short quarter-corner contracts suggested an inverse
perpendicular approach followed by a short turn into that NE tail. In
the mature-wave region, this shortcut is blocked BEFORE any short corner
begins: every same-sector clean perpendicular inverse-H approach hits an
incompatible future-fixed color while crossing the wave hull.

This is NOT a no-launch theorem. A near-kernel approach, a preceding
nonstandard transient which revisits/repairs the crossing cells, or a
connector in the future-unread SW region is not excluded. The obstruction
assumes no earlier prelude visits have changed the relevant first-approach
cell colors. Compatible overlap after such visits requires a new check.

## Exact inverse convention

Let F be the usual Langton step, and let J act on a state by

    J(B,p,h) = (B,p-D[h],h+2).

Then J F^(-1) J^(-1) is an ordinary FORWARD Langton step on the same board.
For an original standard word H, its backward-conjugate turn word is
I=swapRL(reverse(H)), with the exact conjugate pose; this is not assumed
to be spatially opposite standard H or to have zero K.

The1564 wave's conjugate backward start is(-6,-7), south, with sector
(x+y+h)mod2 equal to1. A canonical I NE tail corresponding to the final
SW[-2,8] writer has phase-zero pose(-14,-16)+n(2,2), heading east.
The normalized supplied short inverse corners have incoming north or
south and output this east phase. Their incoming phase-zero normal lies
between-5 and11 after exact output alignment. Translation parity is NOT
ignored; the normalized table's sector0 is shifted to physical sector1.

## Finite exhaustive mature approach types

In the wave's mature future, a physical point is read if its full
(t=x-y,a=x mod14) class is among the557 lists. Required initial colors
are exactly the five-band bulk colors, and depend only on(t,x mod2).
The finite eligibility fringe and two kernel toggles are absent there.

A sufficient maturity bound is u=x+y>=78: for every future-read class the
smallest physical x-coordinate is at most12, so u>=78 and t>=-54 imply
x>=(78-54)/2=12 and hence full eligibility. I at heading north has slot
u offsets[-4,6], and at heading south[-6,4]. Thus incoming phase-zero
u0>=82 or84 respectively keeps every constrained approach slot mature.

Sector1 makes u0 odd. The mature wave's pattern repeats under(14,14),
so u0 modulo28 has14 possible odd values. The incoming I phase-zero
x parity has two possibilities, and its heading is north or south.
There are therefore exactly56 types.

`check_mature_inverse_perpendicular_approach.py` reconstructs all56 from
literal H and w. Begin I far outside the wave band (normal about+101 for
north input or-101 for south input), where its earlier past is wholly
future-unread. Continue the complete I word until its canonical phase-zero
head can reach the central short-corner range: normal<=11 from above or
normal>=-5 from below. Every type encounters a fixed future-wave first
color incompatible with its required I symbol before reaching that cut.
The rejection cells' normals range from-54 through30. This is a complete
finite periodicity reduction, not extrapolation from an arbitrary long run.
The JSON retains a specific contradiction for each type.

## Proof without a simulation horizon

Use the same557 physical(14,14) read classes and ordered-slot certificate.
555 classes have zero copy-index span. The other two have span one.
On every full future ray the first required read color is exactly the
constant color of B; there are197 black and360 white required classes.
The only two finite eligibility-boundary mismatches against B are exactly
the displayed toggle cells. Their corrections are outside each class's
full-list future ray. All read lists alternate. The exact general orbit
argument therefore proves indefinite repetition on this simpler state.
The supplied check additionally replays two literal periods, but those
finite periods are not the proof of continuation.

This is a normal form of the same prepared mechanism, not a claim that
arbitrary full infinite standard bands arise from a finite seed.

## Exact cost of N periods on any finite current board

Independently of how a current board was produced, an actual prescribed
turn word forces the INITIAL color at each distinct read cell: it must be
black if its chronological first symbol is L, and white if it is R.
Placing black exactly on the first-L cells realizes that finite word.
Thus the number of distinct first-L cells is the exact minimum starting
black mass for the prescribed word and pose.

For N consecutive copies of this1564 word, direct interval counting on
each of its557 translation classes gives

    minimum starting black mass = 197N+1, for every N>=1.

Each of the555 zero-span classes contributes either N or zero black cells.
The two span-one lists have only finite endpoint corrections; their total
correction is+1. In particular the wave CANNOT persist forever on ANY
finite current board. Its net black increase is nevertheless+104 per
period: among mature read classes197 start black,301 finish black, and358
have odd write parity. Equivalently the word has834 R and730 L turns.

There is no contradiction. The wave consumes a positive density of
pre-existing black rail cells while leaving an even denser altered wake.
The black increase is not a renewable input-fuel certificate.

This sharp finite-supply obstruction does NOT establish a bound in the
ORIGINAL seed mass. Earlier long clean standard flights can create
arbitrarily many black rail cells with bounded original seed mass and
essentially no K. A bounded-original-mass route which presents longer and
longer five-band supplies would still produce unbounded finite K-rich
prefixes (614 per period), even though every one eventually runs out.
Constructing or excluding that old-cap itinerary remains separate and open.

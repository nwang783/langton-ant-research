# A clean standard leg cannot cross an untouched settled perpendicular strip

## Literal standard profile facts

For standard104 and any rotation or cyclic phase, the full future read
orbits occupy eleven consecutive transverse levels and BOTH x-parities
at every level. At each stable physical read class the chronologically
first read is R. Its settled wake has twelve black full-drift classes,
including black at both extreme read levels. These are exactly the literal
profile facts already reconstructed for the signed slab-packing proof.

Only one settled black class will be needed below; no assertion that all
eleven levels contain black is used.

## Perpendicular blocking lemma

Let an earlier completed d=(-2,-2) standard segment have an untouched
settled wake through a longitudinal slab. Choose ONE of its black classes
(t0,a0), t=x-y, a=x mod2. Within the settled slab its cells are

    (a0-2k, a0-2k-t0),  u=x+y=2a0-t0-4k.

Consider a later claimed clean perpendicular standard leg, with drift
(2,-2) or(-2,2). Its read strip is eleven consecutive u levels, say
[c,c+10], and includes both x-parities at every such level. Assume this
ENTIRE eleven-level crossing band lies in the old class's genuinely
settled longitudinal slab, with no intervening edit to that class. Also
assume complete local read histories at the chosen crossing cells belong
to the new leg, not an incomplete initial/final boundary slice.

There is at least one u in[c,c+10] satisfying

    u congruent 2a0-t0 modulo4.

The corresponding actual old black cell q has x parity a0 and therefore
lies on a physical read class of the perpendicular leg. By the complete-
history assumption its first new-leg read is R. No previous new-leg read
has flipped q, and the old settled field still makes q black. The actual
first turn at q must instead be L: contradiction.

Thus under these exact settled-bulk/history hypotheses a perfectly clean
standard crossing is impossible. The same argument works with opposite
orientations or rotations. It is NOT enough merely that two geometric
read hulls intersect; the untouched settled black point and its complete
new local history are what force the contradiction.

## Consequence for the alternating-port router

`interleaved_old_side_router_obstruction.md` forces the first two requested
old-side routes to intersect if both are compact behind one corridor cut.
If their unavoidable intersection is through an interior clean standard
connector and its entire crossing band is settled and untouched, the
lemma shows that the later route cannot keep its stipulated clean word.
Moving the stations farther longitudinally does not legalize that crossing.

This does not exclude a physical router. It locates the extra obligation:
a crossing has to be absorbed into a finite anchor/reaction region, a
verified nonstandard crossing transient, a deliberately edited wake, or
an additional excursion outside the two-route geometry. A local corner
certificate on a pristine supplied field does not certify this operation.
Nor does the lemma by itself force an adjacent-K event or supply a global
crossing/fuel budget.

## Why current-field fuel and original-seed fuel differ

The translating-word fuel theorem says that n copies of the1564 word require
at least197n+1 black cells on its current input board. This remains TRUE.
The184 initial bits are not that board: seven earlier zero-interior-K
standard flights have written long rails. The wave's fuel is produced by
those histories, converting initial geometric separation into prepared
colored supply. Current black mass at the certified endpoint is10805+2876n,
not184.

A global potential is allowed to measure such latent geometric work. But it
cannot simultaneously identify current prepared supply with original seed
mass or collapse every longitudinal clean flight at zero resource cost. K
compression is only a COUNT identity, not a state/resource identity.

The signed common-settled-slab packing inequality12J-1<=W is unaffected. It
bounds the number J of disjoint clean strips under its untouched-bulk
hypotheses. This family keeps J and W bounded while consuming unbounded
longitudinal rail length inside those strips. Thus even a perfect proof of
no unlimited transverse widening would not alone bound total defect count.

## Scope of any remaining spatial target

A full-radius bound is logically very different from a mass-only bound. For
fixed radius r about the head there are finitely many normalized finite inputs.
If every one eventually captures, their maximum K is finite; conversely any
finite K bound for every radius implies capture via the exact renewal theorem.
This finite-configuration observation is not a useful new proof or explicit
bound. It merely prevents mistaking 'allow spatial dependence' for already
having supplied the missing well-founded rank.

No total-seed-distance, moment, area, or nonlinear spatial formula has been
proved here. The inward spiral and the present family are consistent with
some such candidates, but consistency is not evidence of a universal bound.
The precise new obstruction is that any valid global resource must retain
unbounded longitudinal geometry or another genuinely unbounded state variable,
not just finite seed mass, transverse order, or drift-phase labels.

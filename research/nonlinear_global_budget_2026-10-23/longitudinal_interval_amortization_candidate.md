# A spatial interval/refill ledger: local theorem and exact missing hypotheses

23 October2026. A genuinely spatial CONDITIONAL amortization candidate, not
a universal budget. It keeps longitudinal interval lengths, complete fields,
and dormant archives. The radius/totality equivalence note supplies no proof
of the hypotheses below.

## 1. What an interval ledger must remember

A useful prepared-archive record has at least:

    (physical normal strip, axial copy interval [a,b], phase,
     complete bulk profile, finite endpoint edits, last actual write history).

The length b-a is indispensable. The184-cell family has fixed mass, fixed
normal width and identical seed projection modulo(14,14), but writes an
interval of length proportional to n and consumes n nonstandard periods.
Charging just a root, cap, band, graph component or interval endpoint cannot
see this supply. A constant cost per stored interval fails immediately.

Do NOT discard a record when its ancestry root is inactive. In the retained
20-cell root-reactivation family, a token born at time53 at(2,5) is consumed
at624n-1283, and its A0 root regains both infinite standard spines. The root
is inactive for572n-1123 intervening updates. The ledger must retain that
physical unspent token throughout its dormancy. Relevance is not age.

Likewise initial-root pair repeats in the inward spiral occur at new physical
longitudinal copies, translated(-1,-1) with the SAME three-root owner word.
An interval ledger can distinguish those positions; a root-pair budget cannot.
Same-component reconvergence is not fresh funding either: the signed9-cell
DC,VCC,V,V control creates a cycle using two earlier outputs of the same root.

## 2. A valid local no-refill rule

The COMPLETE mature outputs of the five-band1564 and three-band4352 normal
forms have524 and652 black drift classes. In addition to the two-word
nonconsumer matrix, a stronger simple ordinary-writer obstruction holds:

    EVERY normal t level in[-54,30] respectively[-16,40]
    carries at least one full output-black drift class.

Any parallel clean standard104 writer visits BOTH axial parities on ALL11
normal levels of its read strip. Along each such parity class it eventually
visits every residue of the14- or26-periodic output. Its full-list first color
is white, as required for a finite-input ordinary highway. Therefore if its
read strip meets even ONE normal level of an unchanged infinite mature output,
it encounters infinitely many required-white cells that are black.

No finite cap can repair that infinite discrepancy. Thus a parallel clean
standard writer cannot simply repaint a spent interval in place on unchanged
mature bulk. It must avoid it, receive an unbounded bulk repair, use a different
nonstandard consumer, or interact at a finite boundary where the complete-
history hypotheses fail. The rule is independent of ancestry labels.

For the actual184 producer, the two exterior helpers and later endpoint rail
are additional background, not erased. They cannot weaken the ordinary-writer
obstruction on these occupied normal levels: an additional disjoint black band
adds support rather than removing any of these output blacks. The separate
countermodel analysis tests supplied nonstandard consumers on those enlarged
fields; that is not inferred from the normal-form matrix.

For repeated supplied-wave use of one exact spent mature interval, the earlier
repair lemma also makes refill quantitative. A finite edit set resetting n
copies to a1564 consumer requires at least159(n-1) flips for the admissible NE
alignments, or180(n-1) SW. The analogous4352 self-refill costs479 or478 per
full copy, respectively. These numerical n-1 formulas use the supplied canonical
word cuts; for arbitrary cyclic cuts the safe n-2 replacement follows because
a cyclic shift increases the maximum slot span by at most one. Those are real
physical XOR edits, not fresh roots.

## 3. Why unweighted black/white capacity already fails globally

The two known nonstandard waves have positive black gain104 and184 per
period; standard104 has positive gain12. Inside a fixed finite spatial region,
white-cell capacity pays for these positive-gain bulk rules. This correctly
sees why the184 family can spend a long prepared interval while increasing
current black mass.

But the inward spiral's bulk word S=RLLR has ZERO net black change and positive
K density. Any universal 'each defect consumes net black/white count' claim
therefore fails even on the signed capturing spiral. The interval ledger needs
transport/order information in addition to color density.

For S started at(0,0),N, its input black sites are(1,0),(1,1), and its output
black sites are(0,0),(0,1). Each period transports two black tokens one unit
west with no net mass change. Its rotations transport inward on all four arms
of the signed inward spiral. This is a genuine geometric resource, invisible
to black count.

## 4. A precise weighted-capacity local theorem

Fix a lattice diamond G={q:||q-c||_1<=R} and a constant M=20000000. Put

    w(q)=M+R-||q-c||_1,
    Phi(B)=sum over q in G of w(q)*(1-1_B(q)).

Phi is a nonnegative integer weighted WHITE capacity. The complete finite
field B is retained. Consider a finite continuous physical trajectory entirely
read inside G, partitioned into:

* bulk copies of standard104, the supplied1564 word, or supplied4352 word,
  with any proper physical rotations/translations;
* RLLR copies whose two black-token transports move one unit toward c in
  the relevant coordinate, with neither transport crossing that coordinate
  of c;
* arbitrary remaining cap/interaction blocks.

For each first-kind bulk word of length P, the black gain a=R_count-L_count
is positive (12,104,184). The weight is1-Lipschitz, so relative to its first
read q0 every weight differs from w(q0) by at most P. The exact weighted black
increment satisfies

    Delta(sum w*black) >= a*w(q0)-P^2 >= M-P^2 >= P,

because P<=4352 and M>=4352^2+4352. This is an actual signed sum over all
R/L reads; repeated cells and every earlier history are retained. Hence ALL
K events ending inside such a word, including boundary windows, are at most
P and are paid by the weighted-capacity drop.

For an inward RLLR copy the two black transports increase total black weight
by exactly2. At most4 K events end inside it, so K<=2 times its Phi drop.

For each arbitrary cap i let L_i be its literal length and let

    D_i=max(0, Phi_after_i-Phi_before_i)

be the capacity it refunds. Telescoping, with no dropped seam convention,
gives the valid conditional bound

    K_total <= 2*Phi_initial + 2*sum_i D_i + sum_i L_i.

Indeed Phi never becomes negative, each bulk is paid at rate at most2, and
cap events cost at most their actual length. Also D_i<=max_G(w)*L_i, so a
bound on total cap reads suffices. This is a local spatial amortization theorem,
not finite-state enumeration. It supplies a concrete integer potential and
exact inequalities a candidate global classification could try to establish.

The spatially finite ROI is essential: applying an infinite white reservoir
would give an infinite meaningless potential. A final certified highway may
leave G AFTER the last counted event; it need not be kept in the finite ledger.

## 5. Countercontrols and what is NOT established

The184 family passes this candidate: its full certified pre-capture itinerary
lies in an O(n)-diameter region; all seven writer flights and n wave periods
are positive-gain bulk words, while the six fixed routers, launch and fixed
endpoint recovery are finite cap blocks. The weighted capacity is more than
large enough to pay614n+O(1). This is not a sharp bound and does not pretend
the original mass alone is its initial value.

The20-cell reactivation family also passes without retiring dormant roots:
all long flights are ordinary positive-gain bulk; the five port transients and
fixed774-read recovery carry their FULL cap debt. The old token remains in B
until actually consumed. The signed all-parameter stage geometry is an imported
dependency, not reproved here.

For the inward spiral choose c=(-n-1,n). North-rail S copies transport black
west from x=1-k to x=-k, both right of c. West-rail copies transport black
south from y=2n-k+1 to2n-k, above c. South-rail copies transport east from
x=-2n+k-3 to-2n+k-2, left of c. East-rail copies transport north from y=k-1
to k, below c. Thus ALL its S arms satisfy the inward condition. Its six corner
letters per circuit are retained as cap blocks; none is assigned a fresh-root
credit merely for existing. The mass-neutral quadratic-defect control is
therefore compatible with weighted spatial transport, while it refutes the
unweighted black-capacity simplification.

The decisive missing hypotheses for a GLOBAL proof are all still open:

1. A spatial ROI and anchor controlled a priori by INITIAL radius/mass, or an
   independently summable rule paying for outward boundary flux.
2. A covering of arbitrary actual nonstandard motion by a finite profile/
   transport atlas with paid transitions. The listed words do not exhaust ants.
3. A bound on refunded capacity sum D_i and cap reads that does not simply
   rename the unknown defect count. Root count, age and component merging do
   NOT give such a bound.
4. A proof that mass-neutral transports are inward for the relevant adaptive
   anchor, or a separate ordered interval rank when they are not. Choosing an
   anchor after seeing an arbitrary finite path is not a well-founded invariant.

In particular arbitrary cap edits can replenish a spent interval, outward
mass-neutral transport can INCREASE Phi, and new consumer words can escape the
current two-word matrix. The local theorem is offered as a testable amortization
architecture and exact list of obligations, not as an accomplished global rank.

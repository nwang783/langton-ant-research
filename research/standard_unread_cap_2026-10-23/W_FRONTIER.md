# Standard104 has a genuinely finite-age complete w frontier

23 October2026. Separate author continuation, frozen for independent audit.
This is a conditional full-state theorem for the GIVEN standard104 stream,
not a claim that arbitrary finite seeds enter that stream. It is oriented:
the separately signed24-cell control shows the analogous claim for all z
support is false even in a standard trajectory.

## 1. Exact theorem

Use the standard phase pose(0,0),W, word H, drift d=(-2,-2), and paired black
lattices w=k0,z=k1. Let an ACTUAL complete finite-board trajectory have this
infinite standard future. Then, after a finite board-dependent time:

1. At EVERY physical phase, the COMPLETE current active w black component
   has at most42 edges. It is exactly a translate of an explicitly certified
   phase-dependent finite component.
2. EVERY current black lifetime in that entire component was born by an R
   within the preceding240 physical updates. This includes all of its cycle
   witnesses and archived side branches, not just the head's deleted token.
3. All old emitted12-edge w-trees and every initially unread finite archive
   remain on the actual board. None can reattach to this moving w frontier
   after the stated clearance time. They may still form large dormant w
   archives with one another and with initial debris.

The age240 bound applies after at least two standard periods. Clearance of
arbitrary initial debris requires an additional finite GEOMETRIC threshold,
not a bound in terms of its mass. No fixed original-mass capture-time bound
or universal component bound before standardization follows.

More explicitly, let I be the13-cell source and let F be the actual finite
unread initial field. Every actual standard realization has initial board
I union F, with F disjoint from the entire infinite H read set, by uniqueness
of each cell's first required color. At period2 its exact board is

    (K+2d) union E,       E=G union F,

for the fixed28-cell K and9-cell G below. Define

    M_E = min{X+Y: (X,Y) is a w vertex incident to an E edge}.

For EVERY integer period n>=2 satisfying

    6-8n < M_E,

the conclusions above hold at ALL104 phases of that period and forever after.
When E is empty there is no archive clearance condition. In the concrete24-cell
cap control, E has20 edges and M_E=-10, so n>=3 suffices, even though its z
erasures still require original support at every phase18 for all later n.

## 3. The decisive oriented separation identities

The exact finite certificate proves BOTH of the following w-vertex statements:

1. Distinct translated T copies are vertex-disjoint. The complete set of
   integers m such that V_w(T) meets V_w(T+m d) is exactly{0}.
2. At EVERY phase i, including an isolated w cursor,

       [V_w(K_i) union {w_i}] intersect V_w(T+m d) = empty

   for EVERY negative integer m.

These statements range over ALL integer offsets, not a finite number of old
copies. For any two finite doubled-vertex sets A,B, an intersection with a
translated copy is possible only if

    a-b = 2m d

for some a in A,b in B. Each pair determines at most one integer m. Exhausting
the finite vertex pairs therefore gives ALL offsets exactly. There are only
104 fixed phase fields to check. The second check has NO negative offset at
any phase. A period cannot even temporarily pick up a previously emitted
w-tree and later detach it again.

Consequently all old T copies are genuinely disjoint from the current entire
w frontier and its active vertex, at every fine update. They cannot relay a
connection from more remote copies: the first identity makes all those
copies mutually disjoint too.

This is precisely where the z orientation differs. Its output forms growing
rails rather than separated trees, and a fixed original unread cap can keep
supplying a topologically indispensable connection indefinitely.

## 4. Retaining arbitrary finite archives and proving clearance

Let Y be the union of all V_w(K_i) and all active vertices w_i over one period.
The certificate obtains

    max{X+Y:(X,Y) in Y} = 6.

Physical translation by n d translates doubled vertices by2n d=(-4n,-4n),
reducing their coordinate sum by8n. If6-8n<M_E, the entire moving frontier,
including an isolated active vertex, has smaller coordinate sum than EVERY
E vertex. Thus E has no w-vertex contact with it.

An arbitrary E may connect many old emitted trees, form cycles, and preserve
non-LIFO archives. That is harmless for this scoped statement: no emitted
T copy contacts the moving frontier, no two T copies contact each other,
and E itself has no frontier contact after clearance. Therefore no path in
the COMPLETE w graph can join E or an old tree to the active frontier.
The full active component is EXACTLY the component of w_i within K_i,
translated by n d. If w_i is isolated in K_i it remains isolated in the full
board. This proves the claimed full-state forgetting, without erasing any
archive or assuming it is intrinsically small.

## 5. Full frontier age, not just consumed-token age

Every edge of every finite K_i is checked against ALL reads of the same
physical cell in the bi-infinite geometric H pattern. These reads are the
finite set of pairs(slot j,integer copy k) satisfying

    q_j+k d = x.

For each K_i black cell, its last read before phase i is R; its copy offset
is at least-2; and its elapsed age is at most240. No birth is assigned from
a later convenient occurrence, and deletions/rebirths are not conflated.

For actual period n>=2, all these last-R copies have nonnegative actual index,
so they really occurred inside the actual H future. Initial F has no read and
cannot replace such a lifetime. Hence the translated K_i edges have exactly
the certified ages. All K_i fields have at most42 black edges. Since the full
active w component is contained in the translated K_i after clearance, both
the mass42 and age240 conclusions follow.

The older116 bound for the DELETED token remains true. The new240 bound is
intentionally different: it covers EVERY edge of the whole active component,
including side branches and all possible w-cycle support, with the actual
complete board retained.

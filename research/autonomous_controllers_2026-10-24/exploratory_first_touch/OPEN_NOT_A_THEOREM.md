# Earliest incident read versus first active C face: exploratory only

24 October 2026, 16:46 UTC. NOT submitted for independent audit. This is a
possible future ownership lens, not a result about cycle count or WHITE S1.

For each C face v, let B(v) be the earliest read of ANY of its four incident
physical cells, and A(v) the first time the face is actually active, if ever.
The first incident read is on the side opposite the currently active C face:
that active face already had an incident read at the previous step. Thus a
first-incident R joins an already active source face to a first-touched target
face; a first-incident L is an explicit prepared-input exceptional root.
For complete WHITE there are no such BLACK first-incident roots. The finite
full-drift quotient gives a functional first-touch graph, including C faces
that are touched but never active. No classification inference follows.

On the twenty exact supplied word/reverse/swap/reverse-swap controls, the
WHITE first-touch cycles happen to use the same physical R roots as the
WHITE first-activation cycles. This is DISCOVERY DATA ONLY. Standard's tree
parents do differ: for example class(0,-5) is first touched by phase62 from
an earlier period but first activated through the root45/L49 edge. There is
NO proof that the cycles agree in general. Do not promote sample equality.

A local exact ownership observation, still only a proposed route here: before
its first activation, a C face cannot itself produce R births. Every incident
cell already read from its active neighbor was read once as R and remains
BLACK until an L through it first activates the face. Additional initial
BLACK cells on unvisited ports are possible for prepared backgrounds and
must be retained. At first activation, the at-most-three other pre-produced
BLACK incident edges carry actual owners from neighboring faces. This gives
finite port TYPES, but their owner times, full-drift offsets, later returns,
and surrounding fields remain unbounded. It is not a finite-state controller.
The trivial initial constant-C run forms do not constitute a new macro-alphabet
classification and have not been presented as such.

The signed F49 prepared two-L leaf from the cap-four analysis is a necessary
countercheck: both leaf consumptions have R owners earlier INSIDE its finite
factor, although the surrounding router still uses explicit prepared fuel.
Consequently leaf depth alone does not yield an initial-stock payment or a
strict descent/ascent of source ownership. The author subsequently traced the
two producer faces to DISTINCT BLACK-root first-entry basins. This is therefore
NOT a counterexample to the complete periodic ONE-WHITE-basin target, and a
basin-merger payment might survive. B has opposite-heading SN, not SAME.
A finite blank-prefix initial-head tree is also not the full-quotient target.

Current mathematical gap: no argument proves that a complete WHITE one-cycle
controller must have a missing R owner, nor that selected excursions on distinct
C cycles must strictly interleave. The signed triangular/interleaving results
are conditional constraints, not a solution of that gap.

The two scripts here are scratch discovery tools, with hardcoded retained
paths. They do not certify all future backgrounds or an exhaustive word class.

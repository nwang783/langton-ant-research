## Guard1: AA requires a fresh initially BLACK cell

Suppose modules j-1 and j are both A. At the physical cell

    q_j=(0,-1)+j*delta,

A_j demands L5. The ONLY possible earlier read in the entire module language
would be B_(j-1).R7, but that module is A. The A table has no other read, and
all other B entries are in module j or later; module j is also A. Hence L5 is
the FIRST read of q_j in the whole bi-infinite module history.

Therefore AA is impossible in a complete WHITE front. In a one-sided actual
module tail starting at a finite field, each AA pair instead consumes a BLACK
bit already present at that tail-entry cut. No prior module in that tail can
have created it. Distinct pair indices j give distinct physical q_j because
delta is nonzero. This is an actual lifetime-source argument, not a stale
mask or a claim that every input of A is fresh.

## Guard2: BB is impossible on ANY board

Suppose modules j and j+1 are both B. Choose the physical cell

    (0,-3)+(j+2)*delta.

The first B reads it at local phase17 with R. The next B reads the SAME cell
at local phase15 with R. These are consecutive reads of that physical cell:
there is no later read there in the first module and no earlier read there in
the second. In a literal BB factor the read times are17 and109, at(-2,-1)
relative to the first module. The two R reads contradict the flip rule
regardless of all earlier colors, initial BLACK inputs, or unread fields.

## Exact classification and conditional finite-seed capture

A complete WHITE bi-infinite sequence has neither AA nor BB, and consequently
must alternate ABAB... or BABA.... Conversely those are the actual standard
front. In particular EVERY complete WHITE periodic word in this two-module
language is a phase of a power of standard104. This conclusion is all-period;
it is not an enumeration through some maximum period.

More generally suppose an actual trajectory from a FINITE field stays in this
module language forever after a specified cut. BB never occurs. By Guard1,
the number of AA pairs is at most the number of BLACK cells at that entry cut.
Thus only finitely many AA pairs occur, and after the last one the modules
alternate forever. Such a trajectory eventually follows standard104.

The finite field here is the whole field AT ENTRY to the module tail, not
necessarily the original time0 seed. This conditional statement says nothing
about whether an arbitrary trajectory ever enters the two-module language.
It does not cover extra routers, alternate phases, other closed excursions,
changes of axis, or arbitrary mixtures of the supplied1564/4352 waves.

# A bounded literal owner-repair attempt for prepared80

24 October 2026. Author exact finite-family check; independent audit pending.
No autonomous controller was obtained.

The two stable BLACK-first roots of prepared80 are at phases15 and21. A
natural way to try to supply their missing R owners is a pose-returning loop
inserted before an original read, rather than declaring a prelaid BLACK cell
self-fed. We tested exactly one or two RRRR/LLLL insertions at all80 original
phase cuts. Two insertions at one cut retain their stated order. Every loop
returns the actual same position and incoming heading; the rest of the
backbone word is unchanged. The full drift remains(4,4).

There are13120 labelled insertion choices and3468 distinct resulting words.
All complete physical histories were sorted with their NEW full period and
full drift; phase order was not substituted for physical chronology.

-3462 distinct words have a stored consecutive same-turn witness in a complete
 stable class, so cannot repeat on any initial board.
-Six are stable alternating prepared words, all still requiring inputs:
  LLLL at39,54,or63 gives I5/O13/S3;
  LLLL at39 and63, or54 and63, gives I8/O12/S3;
  LLLL followed by RRRR at60 gives I4/O16/S1.

Thus none of this exact finite family supplies a complete WHITE front. In
particular the obvious one- or two-loop R-birth repairs do not remove the
external root obligations. The stable variants are diagnostics of necessary
input roots, not newly materialized finite-seed autonomous controls.

This does not exclude three insertions, a longer pose-return excursion,
reordered backbone reads, changed drift, an actual router, or another controller.
It is not an all-period theorem obtained by imposing a visit cap.

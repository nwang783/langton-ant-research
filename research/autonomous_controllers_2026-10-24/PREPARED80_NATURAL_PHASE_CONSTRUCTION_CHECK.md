# A bounded whole-block construction check using prepared80

24 October 2026. Author exact supplied-word check; independent audit pending.
This is one constructive attempt, not a controller-classification theorem.

Use one through four full80-letter blocks, each at one of these phase cuts:
0,10,27,42,66,1. The first five are the actual starts of the five literal
RRRRLLLLRL modules. Phase1 supplies the missing quarter orientation. Every
block restores its incoming heading; no free rotation or invented router is
inserted between blocks.

All1554 labelled words were tested with their actual full displacement and
complete full-drift chronological histories. Counts by number of blocks are:

- one:6 stable prepared words;
- two:6 stable prepared,22 nonalternating,8 zero-drift;
- three:6 stable prepared,210 nonalternating;
- four:6 stable prepared,1158 nonalternating,132 zero-drift.

The24 stable words are precisely the powers of a single selected phase block.
They retain2n BLACK-input roots and14n output roots at n blocks, and S=3. No
mixed-phase word in this bounded family gives a complete WHITE front. A zero-
drift word is not a translating candidate. Each nonalternating case retains
an explicit pair of consecutive same-turn events in one COMPLETE stable
physical history, so unread colors cannot repair that repeated word.

This does NOT say that every finite pair of these factors is an illegal
abutment: the rejection concerns the full repeated supplied word and its full
drift, not necessarily one finite factor. Nor does it exclude other phase
cuts, a genuine intervening router, more blocks, or changed local reads.
It is not a new stock monotonicity theorem over an empty mixed language.

No arbitrary visit or width cap was used. The period bound is solely the
explicit definition of this finite construction family. No new controller
was produced; no general exclusion is inferred.

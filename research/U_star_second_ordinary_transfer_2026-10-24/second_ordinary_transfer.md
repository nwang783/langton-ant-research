# Exact two-ordinary transfer and a non-iterable boundary control

Status: author checked, pending independent review. This is a finite continuation theorem, not an arbitrary-tail invariant.

## Statement and physical cut

Start with an actual U-entry complete-eight interval. Fix any genuine creating U/UC/UCC in that interval, normalized to U-base entry `(0,0,N)`, with its four same-original R-born star identities at `(0,0),(0,1),(1,0),(1,1)`. Append its first complete foreign A in F/Q/B and two immediate ordinary RAW atoms b1,b2 in D/C/V/U, with their actual following M11 compatibility.

At **CUT3**, after A RAW, b1 RAW, and b2 RAW, but before the third ordinary RAW read0, at least two of these same original identities are CURRENT and form an adjacent corner. CURRENT means physically BLACK and outside the actual CUT3 entry core. An original inside that core is physically alive but not current. No future M11 read has been executed at CUT3. Recolored replacements are never counted.

The corner may be LEFT, TOP, RIGHT, or BOTTOM. Unlike the signed CUT2 three-port theorem, BOTTOM cannot be omitted. No simplicity of b2, no old-eight maturity after A, and no new producer interpretation of b1/b2 are assumed.

## Exhaustive finite certificate

For exactly 48 fixed words A+b1+b2+M11, reconstruct literal before requirements and exact reads. Align each original star cell to every TRUE before-requirement cell, in all four headings, and deduplicate. This is a local footprint enumeration, not a prefix search.

The 9,912 aligned states partition as follows:

- 4,884 wrong active sector;
- 1,080 original CURRENT cell in foreign entry core;
- 2,628 original BLACK versus a required before-WHITE;
- 227 contacts of the repaired foreign or FIRST returned chord with the old creating U chord, prohibited by the independently signed one-return simplicity theorem;
- 1,093 compatible necessary-only transfer states.

Every compatible state retains an adjacent pair of same original CURRENT identities. Cases outside the alignment universe lose no original current identity. Any actual loss must either have a first physical L (hence an initial TRUE requirement) or enter CUT3 core (hence a TRUE requirement in future M11), so it is represented.

This universe includes the **119 newly touched poses** whose four originals were all current at CUT2. Merely extending the prior 262 nontrivial CUT2 states would miss them. The 1,093 states comprise that 119 plus the 974 compatible extensions of the prior nontrivial states. Twelve retain BOTTOM only. None of these alignment rows certifies a genuine creating-U history.

Its second U chord is `(3,3)` to `(-5,3)` in doubled corrected centers; it meets the hypothetical old U chord `(-1,-1)` to `(-1,7)` at `(-1,3)`, strict midpoint on both. The foreign and first D chords miss that old U chord.

`F, D, UCC, V, D, D`, followed by the actual next M11 prefix.

It replays from 29 black cells, all others white, with initial head `(4,-3,E)`. The JSON gives the complete board and all 173 actual reads. These four star labels are initial prepared labels, **not** genuine U-created R births.

## What a closed state would still need

The four corner names alone are not a closed transfer system. A correct continuation state needs:

1. Actual relative head/heading and current full packet/atomic tail ownership.
2. Each genuine source-R identity's physical alive/dead and CURRENT/CORE status; replacements need distinct birth tags.
3. Every already constrained cell's current physical color/parity. A future outside-return interface must declare its entry core and exact first-read requirements, matching this retained archive before adding newly read cells.
4. The actual previously executed proxy/held-edge geometry if a no-contact hypothesis is used. A barrier claim requires a current original identity to MISS the actual continuation context.

That constrained-cell archive can grow with the visited footprint. No finite bound, finite-state closure, arbitrary-tail survival, or genuine U-entry erosion is proved. The stronger proposed implication that erosion below two requires an earlier contact with the creating U chord or another certified old barrier remains open; the 29-cell control satisfies, rather than refutes, its contact alternative.

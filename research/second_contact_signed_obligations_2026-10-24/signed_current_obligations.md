# Actual contact masks matched against signed current-tag obligations

## Scope and purpose

This is a small causal obligation table, not an enumeration of long histories. Use the original U-entry complete-eight prefix, its first foreign F/Q/B, one immediate ordinary RAW atom and the chronological first new contact of a second immediate ordinary RAW atom, with one actual final M11. The remaining windows considered here are old D/C at true preforeign age three and old V at ages three or four.

The table matches every actual combined before-WHITE or entry-core condition against explicitly proved SAME CURRENT identities. An old output without a currentness proof is not silently treated as WHITE, erased, spent or irrelevant. Its retirement, replacement, core-export and dormant-output obligations are retained.

## The only currentness rules used

1. Signed 13:25: a genuine atomic EXIT bud stays current for true post-EXIT ages zero, one and two. The actual atomic exit clock is retained. In particular, an old C at entry clock zero exits at clock one; its own bud (1,2) is still current at foreign clock three. An old D/V base exit is clock zero, so its generic exit-bud rule does not imply currentness at age three.
2. Signed 13:44: the genuine atomic common-R8 output is current before atomic entry age three. A second C's actual source-entry shift is retained. It is not assigned its containing full producer's age.
3. Signed 13:19: the specific internal-C1 buds of full VCC and UCC require full-entry age at least five before retirement. In an old-C2 chart the full entry is at minus one, and foreign age three is full age four. This exact stronger tag is retained without claiming the same bound for other outputs.
4. Signed 20:36: the original V-family r=(0,0) first retires at exact full-source-entry age five.
5. Signed 20:43: the original V-family B=(1,0) first retires at exact full-source-entry age four. Original A=(0,1), if retired, has the sole consumer V/VC(0,4,S) at source age two. These are original-identity laws with actual source C tails, not a whole-cohort first-touch assumption.

Every full atomic prefix bud/common fact is literally checked against the full source's actual final R and O set. The source's true full completion clock is also recorded.

## The genuine V age-three disjunction

At a foreign age-three cut, either original A is still current, or its exact age-two V/VC cleanup helper has already executed. In the latter case the helper's six actual V-base outputs are all current at its true age one, by the independently signed universal produced-input minimum two. Its actual own C tail preserves those six last-R identities. The six slots are regenerated literally, rather than guessed from distinguished-input offsets.

The table tests both causal states. It never assumes that source A's absence means physical WHITE, and it does not import helper maturation through the foreign. The cleanup-helper state is contradicted in every one of the 141 age-three placements. The original-A-current state has 114 cuts and twenty-seven necessary remaining placements, for each V/VC/VCC source tail. Thus every surviving age-three V state must retain original A at the foreign entry. Deeper ancestry of those twenty-seven placements is separate work.

The source-role counts are not counts of attained histories and do not identify different tails as different physical contact geometries.

## What remains in each unresolved row

The machine-readable table retains actual first-read owners and identifies: an original output that must already have retired before a required WHITE bit; a BLACK requirement needing either original survival or a genuine replacement/core export; a foreign entry-core cell requiring the old residual identity to be absent; and each dormant source output missing the entire combined context. The historical incoming EXIT bud is also retained as an obligation once its generic minimum age three no longer proves currentness.

No unbounded rail lengths are truncated. No full source output seal, functional reset, finite geometry quotient, second-atom simplicity, arbitrary-tail induction or global seven-word trajectory coverage is inferred.

# All-input support recycling

23 October 2026. Conditional physical theorem plus exact relaxation limitations.
The main package and archive/mask addenda passed independent review under LM;
the sealed-cohort theorem also passed analytic review. See frozen hashes.
The universal finite-seed highway conjecture remains unsolved.

- `reconvergence_budget.md` retains EVERY packet input in a historical incidence
  DAG and proves beta=v+4u-r0+c, with delta beta=a-g. It also gives a valid but
  weaker iterated-cubic duration bound under a beta budget.
- `cubic_rank_corollary.md` uses signed HS to improve that conditional bound to
  O((m+beta)^3), independent of initial diameter. Consuming k still-current
  siblings from one source necessarily adds at least k-1 to beta. Neither
  the identity nor the inherited duration bound proves beta is bounded.
- `aged_cleanup_countermodel.md` gives an all-time abstract resource model with
  eight fixed initial roots, exact DC/V/D input/output COUNTS, produced input
  age eight, collision-free current lattice cells, all-input coordinate speed
  at most one-half and the numerical LB root boxes. Every original spare is
  retired before continuation, but same-root helpers make beta grow forever.
- `cactus_topology_obstruction.md` proves that the model's historical graph is
  planar, has one infinite end per root and bounded finite side-cleanup lobes,
  yet unbounded cycle rank. This is abstract graph planarity, not an embedding
  respecting literal identity coordinates, packet masks or the live head.

- `renewable_u_archive_countermodel.md` strengthens the relaxation with an
  indefinitely renewed same-root U archive, not just unbounded V count. Its
  abstract graph is a planar one-ended ladder; exact masks remain omitted.
- `u_bulk_spare_mask_lemma.md` identifies a literal failure of that exact
  archive update: a U-family input mask containing all three original DC
  spares necessarily also consumes its main. There is one possible pose.
- `sealed_cohort_import_lemma.md` proves a genuine conditional merger budget:
  if those four DC outputs are the whole live component and all survive to
  that U consumer, its fifth input imports another component, delta beta=3.
  Such distinct targets are at most r0-1. A pre-existing live archive defeats
  this certificate, and component live mass is nondecreasing.

The resource model expressly omits the exact masks, five-cell core, full white
context, full272 role potential and successive head endpoint continuity. It
is not an infinite finite-seed ant or literal packet history. Likewise the
physical cubic theorem is restricted to actual context-complete recurrent-eight
factors, not arbitrary UNION6 residual defects.

`check_all_input.py/json` supplies only finite controls: 96 generic resource
DAGs with 12,288 packet updates, and 2,048 main generations / 6,128 packets of
the explicit aged model, checking 8,160 produced-input ages and 2,040 cactus
cycles. The all-time claims follow from the written formulas and graph
construction, not those finite controls. Independent scripts live separately
under the repository's audits directory when signed.

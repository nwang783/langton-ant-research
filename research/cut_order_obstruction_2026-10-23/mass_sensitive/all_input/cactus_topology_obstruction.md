# Planar one-ended ancestry still admits unbounded sibling reconvergence

23 October 2026. Abstract graph corollary of the explicit
`aged_cleanup_countermodel.md`; not a literal packet or ant construction.

That construction's historical all-input graph is a disjoint union of eight
locally finite cactus graphs. To see this, inspect one family. Its main
identities and DC packet vertices form one infinite alternating directed ray.
Every source DC produces three spare identities a,b,c in addition to its
continuing main identity. At the following family turn:

- a,b enter one V helper, making exactly the four-edge undirected cycle
  DC--a--V--b--DC;
- the six V outputs are terminal identity leaves;
- c enters a D helper whose one output is another terminal identity leaf.

No side output is ever used again, and different sources' helper vertices
and spare identities are distinct. Each such finite cycle-and-bush lobe
therefore attaches to exactly ONE source DC vertex on the main ray. There
are no additional cycles or cross-lobe connections. Every edge lies in at
most one simple cycle, proving the cactus assertion and identifying one
independent cycle per V helper.

Consequently the graph has all the following features simultaneously:

1. a directed acyclic orientation from eight fixed initial roots;
2. at most two incidences at each identity vertex and bounded packet degree;
3. one infinite end per root component, exactly eight ends in total;
4. a planar embedding, with all initial roots on the unbounded face;
5. only bounded-size finite side-cleanup lobes on each infinite main ray;
6. historical cycle rank beta(K)=max(K-8,0), hence unbounded rank.

For (3), removing any finite vertex set leaves exactly one infinite component
in each sufficiently long main-ray tail; every component not meeting a tail
is finite. For (4), embed each alternating main ray on a horizontal line and
each finite lobe in its own small disjoint rectangle above its source. The
four-cycle can be drawn as a small quadrilateral, with its finitely many
pendant trees outside it. This is an ABSTRACT graph embedding. It need not
place vertices at their prescribed identity-cell coordinates, and cannot be
used as a literal spatial packet embedding or a transported-head realization.

All-source finite root sets, directed acyclicity, abstract planarity, finitely
many infinite ends, and bounded finite cleanup bushes thus cannot, even
jointly, force a historical reconvergence budget. Infinite cleanup need not
spawn a second infinite lineage. The literal omitted premises remain exact
relative packet masks, complete core/white context, simultaneous actual input
coexistence at a pose, and successive endpoint continuity of one live head.
This does not refute a topology lemma that retains those extra physical data.

The construction's mass, ages, all-input coordinate speed and numerical LB
root-box checks are in the companion note. No finite-seed infinite ant orbit,
actual sibling-cleanup chronology or uniform physical rank growth is claimed.

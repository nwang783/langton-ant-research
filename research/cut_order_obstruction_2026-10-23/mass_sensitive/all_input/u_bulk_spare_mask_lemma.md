# A literal U cannot bulk-clear all DC spares while preserving its main

23 October 2026. Exact relative-input-mask lemma; no packet-history existence
or global finite-fuel conclusion. It distinguishes the renewable resource
archive in `renewable_u_archive_countermodel.md` from literal packet geometry.

Normalize a source DC at head(0,0,N). Its four fresh residual outputs are

    main A=(2,0), spares B=(2,3), C=(3,1), D=(3,2).

Every U-family packet U,UC,UCC has the SAME five normalized residual inputs

    J={(-1,1),(-1,2),(-1,4),(0,2),(0,3)}.

The literal signed masks are in
`../../../core_periodicity_new/chronology_dcuv/literal_residual_geometry.json`.
Only the input geometry is needed: arbitrary other residual identities and
arbitrary intermediate history may be present.

Claim: if a translated genuine quarter rotation p+R_h J contains all three
spare cells B,C,D, then h=0 and p=(3,-1). Its complete input set is

    {(2,0),(2,1),(2,3),(3,1),(3,2)}.

In particular it ALSO contains A. Thus a single literal U-family packet
cannot consume all three still-current ORIGINAL spare identities and keep
A current. Two other inputs from an unrestricted prepared archive do not
remedy this obstruction.

## Exhaustive analytic proof

For h=1 or3, the rotated J has vertical span one, while B,C,D have vertical
span two. These headings are impossible.

For h=0, J has a left column of three cells at heights1,2,4 and a right
column of two cells at heights2,3. Since C,D occupy the right source column,
and B is one column to their left, C,D must use J's right column. Their
consecutive heights force the unique translation p=(3,-1). J's left column
then has heights0,1,3, containing B at3 and A at0.

For h=2, the right column has heights-4,-2,-1 and the left column has
heights-3,-2. C,D must use the right column. Its only consecutive pair is
-2,-1, forcing vertical translation3; the left column then has heights0,1,
which miss B at3. No placement remains. This checks all headings without a
spatial search window. The sole h=0 placement is sector-compatible; omitting
the sector test cannot have hidden another placement.

## Ordered identities and rank

The claim concerns identities still current at the proposed target. If any
spare has previously retired and its cell has a later-born replacement,
the hypothesis about all three ORIGINAL inputs fails. A helper may consume
fewer spares, perform multistep cleanup, or consume A as well; none is excluded.

If a U-family packet actually consumes all four still-current outputs of
this source DC, they are already in one historical incidence component.
Consequently its delta beta is at least3. The fifth input makes delta beta4
if it belongs to that component, or3 if it is from another component. This
is the general all-input sibling-join identity, not an irreversible initial
resource. No simultaneous consumption or actual supported word is asserted.

The renewable-U resource model preserves A and directly consumes three
spares plus two archive identities, so this lemma explicitly identifies one
literal premise that it violates. More general physical U archives, delayed
consumption of A, or distributed helper cleanup remain open. Independent
review requested.

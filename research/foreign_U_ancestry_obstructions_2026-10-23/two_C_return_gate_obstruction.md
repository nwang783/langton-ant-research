# A causal obstruction for the smallest south-facing foreign return gate

Status: author proof complete; independent audit pending.

## Exact claim

Normalize a genuine creating U to (0,0,N), with original left-star tags L={(0,0),(0,1),(1,0),(1,1)}. Follow it by ANY complete retained-eight connector containing exactly two C words. There is no bound on the connector's zero-C rail lengths. Let the next word be the first foreign word. None of these three gates is possible with its actual ending M11:

F at (-3,-3,S), Q at (-3,-5,S), or B at (-2,-6,S).

Each is one of the previously signed prepared foreign-miss / returned-DC gates. Its foreign RAW word misses every original star cell. All three exit at (1,-1,N); the following D10 then C15 would physically L-read the original star tag (1,0) at C phase1, or DC phase11. Thus this is one exact candidate reset mechanism, not an arbitrary target.

The obstruction concerns EXACTLY TWO C words from creating U to foreign entry. It does not exclude six-C or higher windings and does not prove seven-word termination.

## The missing cell is an actual creator deletion

All three foreign ending markers require global cell q=(0,-1) to be black. It is respectively their single marker-only input at local (-3,-2), (-3,-4), or (-2,-5). The foreign RAW word never reads q. Its first actual read is the following D's phase1, which supplies M11.

But the creating U reads q exactly once: L at source phase3, leaving it white. If q is black at foreign entry, it must have been reinstalled during the retained-eight connector. Moreover q lies outside that foreign entry's active core, so some preceding literal atom must have emitted q as a residual output. Expand the complete packets into the actual atoms D10, C15, V18 and U28. This expansion is a literal word identity, not a new parser assumption.

The signed U left-star law keeps the SAME four L identities current throughout this connector. They cannot occur in an intervening atom's active entry core.

## Before the first C

The creating U exits at (0,4,N). The zero-C atoms D,V,U retain heading N and have nonnegative y displacement. Their residual outputs, and C's outputs, have normal y coordinate at least zero. Therefore no atom before or at the first C can emit q, whose y coordinate is -1.

## From the first C through the second C

Let H(x,y)=y-x. It is initially 4 at source U exit. For N-heading zero-C atoms, H increments are 0,2,4 for D,V,U. The first C adds1, so its W-heading exit has H at least5.

W-heading zero-C atoms have H increments 2,4,4. Every normal residual output of D,C,V,U has x+y at least zero, and rotating W makes this exactly its H offset. Consequently no atom between the first C and the end of the second C can emit q, since H(q)=-1.

These are monotone statements for arbitrary finite runs, not a bounded enumeration of paths.

## After the second C

The heading is S, and no C remains before foreign entry. Only D,V,U zero-C atoms can emit q. Since q is odd and an active S entry has even coordinate sum, only odd normal outputs can align with q. D has none. V has three and U has five.

The checker reconstructs ALL eight active output alignments. Every one places a still-current old star tag in the producer's entry core:

V slots (0,1), (1,0), (2,1), at entries (0,0,S), (1,-1,S), (2,0,S);
U slots (-1,2), (0,1), (1,0), (1,2), (2,1), at entries (-1,1,S), (0,0,S), (1,-1,S), (1,1,S), (2,0,S).

Each entry core meets L, contradicting genuine tag survival. Thus no S-heading atom can emit q either.

All possible producer phases are excluded. Since q was deleted by the creator and the foreign RAW word does not read it, the actual foreign ending marker cannot occur. The three reset gates are impossible with exactly two C words, regardless of how long their zero-C portions are.

## What this does not settle

No U-created foreign-reset history has been attained here. Prepared suffix feasibility is not used as creator ancestry. Six-C and higher connectors can include additional orientations and must be treated separately. The repaired kinked-proxy Jordan calculation has a six-C branch when the foreign word follows C; moreover old-center self-crossings can obstruct the Jordan hypothesis. Neither issue is silently discarded by this lemma.

The literal checker verifies the creator deletion, all three exact foreign poses and marker ownership, atomic expansion, both monotone output facts, and all eight final-epoch core conflicts. It performs no trajectory or prefix enumeration.

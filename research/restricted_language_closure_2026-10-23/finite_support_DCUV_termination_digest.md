# Finite-support termination of the D/C/U/V movement language

23 October 2026. Dependency-bound mathematical digest, independently checked.
This is a restricted-language theorem, not a proof of universal highway capture.

## 1. Exact theorem and conventions

A physical ant turns R on white and L on black, flips the cell, then moves.
The complete initial black set is finite; the head and heading are arbitrary.
Use these exact turn words:

- D = LLLLRLRRRR (10 reads).
- C = LLLLRLRRRRLRRRR (15 reads).
- V = LLLLRLRRRRLRRLRRRR (18 reads).
- U = LLLLRLRRRRLRLLLLRRRRLRRLRRRR (28 reads).

A complete packet owns its raw word and has the actual following marker
M11 = LLLLRLRRRRL. The marker is real future behavior, not an imposed or
invented continuation. The recurrent-eight packets are
D, DC, V, VC, VCC, U, UC, UCC.

**Theorem.** No trajectory on a finite-support black board has an infinite
complete recurrent-eight factor. More generally, no such trajectory has an
infinite tail formed by concatenating the four raw words D, C, V, U.

Thus every occurrence of this movement language eventually ends. The theorem
allows arbitrary behavior after that exit. It neither asserts eventual entry
into this language nor controls repeated exits and re-entries. Infinitely
prepared black backgrounds are outside its scope.

## 2. Core theorem: a U-entry factor cannot continue forever

The independently checked finite-support marked-V theorem proves this for a
complete-eight factor starting with U, UC or UCC. Its dependency chain is:

1. The corrected atomic-center path of any such actual U-entry factor is
   injective. Ordered residual identities are retained until their actual
   retirement; a recycled cell receives a new identity.
2. A produced input can be consumed only up to 294 C-clock units after its
   full producer ENTRY. Every candidate packet context, including WHITE
   guards, is disjoint from produced identities older than 330 at an already
   actual cut. The latter statement gives exact next-packet recognition,
   not merely an overapproximation that forgets old black obstructions.
3. At most 4851 packets per consumer epoch use a perpendicular-produced
   input. Parallel sources have positive even ages 2 through 294. At most
   three relevant extra C-tail outputs are born in each source epoch.
4. U bases cannot provide the distinguished input of a parallel V/U packet.
   Each V base can provide such an input only through its original output
   C=(2,1), and that identity is spent at most once. These are causal
   current-identity laws, not comparisons of static output masks alone.
5. A V-base C cannot feed an ordinary V or VC in the same heading. The only
   parallel ordinary-target transfer reverses heading at relative pose
   (3,3,S). Two consecutive such transfers return to the original entry
   pose, contradicting center injectivity. VCC targets are not excluded.
6. Mark every external-root V node and every positive-C V-family boundary
   VC/VCC. Every other V is a plain V whose parent must be marked. No mark
   has two children. This is the step converting local geometry into a
   uniform per-epoch bound.

For explicit conservative constants, let r0 be the number of residual entry
identities, R their entry-head radius, H=20R+211, and A=4851+3*147=5292.
There are at most A produced-input external roots and one positive-C V
boundary per C clock. At a fixed consumer clock, V nodes inject into marks
at the 148 labels j,j-2,...,j-294, so

    v_j <= r0 + 148*(A+1) = r0 + 783364.

Entry identities are counted once globally, not once per source clock.
For j>H+294, v_j<=783364. The actual selected-input spending inequalities
then give, for j>H+594,

    N_j <= 2*A + 295*783364 = 231102964.

Here N_j is the number of packet starts in epoch j. Skipped clocks count zero.
Full-source entry clocks never reset at C-tail installation or at a genealogy
cut. The constants are deliberately conservative, not claimed optimal.

An infinite finite-support factor has unbounded C clock. An eventual uniform
epoch bound would make its exact 330-layer relative-head/age/parser state
finite. A reachable state cycle lifts along the original actual exterior by
exact current-context induction. A zero-C cycle violates finite fuel; a
zero-drift cycle repeats a center; a positive-C translating cycle contradicts
center simplicity and the tangent-turn bound obtained by closing repeated
periods with a uniformly bounded-crossing auxiliary bridge. Hence an infinite
U-entry factor cannot have the displayed uniform epoch bound. Contradiction.

This argument does not assume a bound on historical component count, original
U births, frontier width, or total black mass along the trajectory.

## 3. Removing the U-entry restriction

Suppose an infinite complete-eight factor existed. If it never used a U-family
packet, its expansion would be an infinite raw D/C/V tail, excluded by the
already independently checked finite-support D/C/V theorem. Otherwise take
its first U-family entry. Only finitely many physical steps precede this cut,
so its complete black board is still finite. Its suffix contradicts Section 2.

No original-root funding labels are reset in this inference. It simply starts
the qualified finite-support theorem at an actual later physical cut.

## 4. Removing the complete-packet boundary restriction

The signed arbitrary-DCUV boundary lemma applies to every feasible finite raw
D/C/V/U factor, without a premise about its later behavior. It discards at
most 203 reads: at most 60 leading C reads, an optional initial DCCC of 55
reads, and a final boundary piece of at most 88 reads. The retained factor
uses only the recurrent-eight packets and has real endpoint markers. The
DCC followed by terminal D exception is repaired by removing the last C,
not by inventing a marker. The needed DCC-U and UCCC-M11 conflicts are
literal finite checks.

If an infinite raw DCUV tail existed, arbitrarily long finite prefixes would
therefore contain arbitrarily long complete-eight factors whose start cuts
lie among the first 116 physical time cuts. One start cut supports unbounded
lengths. The finitely branching tree of its actual complete-eight parsings
has an infinite branch, contradicting Section 3. This compactness step does
not assume a uniform duration bound over initial boards or over U counts.

## 5. Exact relationship to defect reductions

The independently checked six-label UNION theorem uses

    S = {RRRLLRLLRLR, LLRLRLLRRLL, RRRRLRLLRLL,
         RRRLRRRRLRL, RRRLRRRRLRR, LRRLRRRRLRR}.

For finite-support ants, standard104 capture is equivalent to finitely many
adjacent-bad-ten events K carrying labels outside S. That theorem's second
recurrent component uses a DIFFERENT eight-packet alphabet
C,V,V+,U,X30,U+,Q64,B74, with its inherited finite graph. It must not be
identified with the recurrent-eight alphabet of this digest.

The larger still-unresolved literal language D,C,V,U,F,Q,B has a separate
214-vertex,993-edge coalesced quotient. The new theorem excludes any physical
infinite tail in that language with only finitely many F/Q/B occurrences.
Together with its earlier no-D, C-free and D/C/F/Q/B exclusions, a hypothetical
finite-support infinite seven-word tail must have infinitely many D, C,
F/Q/B, and at least one of U or V. The previously open D/C/U/V-only recurrent
support is now removed.

This is not a deletion of all D/C/U/V edges from the larger graph. Such edges
may occur arbitrarily often between other blocks; their resources may be
replenished across those blocks. No theorem here makes outside-S defects
finite, covers arbitrary dense defect clusters, or makes an arbitrary ant
enter the seven-word language. Universal finite-seed capture remains open.

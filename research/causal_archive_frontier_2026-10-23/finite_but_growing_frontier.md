## 1. Effective frontier size without a bounded-token premise

Fix a finite actual complete-eight factor beginning U/UC/UCC. Its center path
is simple. Write m for entry black mass, r0=m-5 for entry residual identities,
and j for cumulative C count at a current full-packet boundary. Let L=max(0,j-294).
Keep the marked five-cell core, every current produced identity whose full
source-entry clock a obeys a>=L, and every untouched entry identity still
needed before its spatial expiration horizon. Remove older produced identities
from this reduced representation, not from the true board.

Let N_a be the number of completed packets with full-entry C clock a. The
signed zero-C fuel and fixed-heading projection argument gives

    N_a <= r0*(2a+10).

All packets at this clock share the same entry heading. Their start progress
strictly increases, while each distinguished consumed input is an epoch-entry
identity within the signed original-root boxes. This bounds packet count;
it does not assume a bound on current total residual mass.

Every retained-eight packet has at most15 outputs. Therefore the retained
produced-ID count is at most

    15*r0*sum_(a=L)^j (2a+10).                           (1)

For j<=293 the sum is(j+1)(j+10). For j>=294 it is295(2j-284).
Thus after the initial transient the active produced ledger has a provable
O(r0*j) cardinality bound, not an assumed uniform constant.

There are at most r0 untouched entry identities. If all entry residual cells
satisfy ||q||_infinity<=R about the normalized initial head, no entry identity
is consumed at a later actual complete packet whose entry clock exceeds
20R+211, by the signed linear nearby-return theorem and input-slot radius5.
Beyond that horizon the untouched entry identities may also be omitted from
this CONDITIONAL representation of the continuing actual factor.

Current produced cells lie in the signed union of original-root boxes of
radius floor((2j+13)/3). Thus the retained state occupies an explicit finite
spatial domain, with known original root coordinates, finite ages0..294,
current head/heading, and the exact literal identity remove/install rule.
The coarse size is at most five plus (1) plus r0 before entry expiration.
Neither the domain nor the number of young cells is uniform in j.

The2323-C lifetime would already have given this kind of growing-frontier

the cardinality argument is not advertised as an independent global resource.

## 2. Conditional trace agreement is NOT a transition-complete simulation

On any fixed actual continuation staying in this same simple complete factor,
the omitted old produced IDs cannot be touched by its later complete contexts.
The retained ledger therefore suffices to replay that GIVEN continuing trace.
Likewise any actual language-valid next packet remains compatible with the
reduced field: its true complete context never reads an omitted old cell.

The converse is NOT established. A candidate packet compatible with the reduced
board can require WHITE at a location holding a discarded old black identity
on the true board. In that event the true ant can leave the retained language
there. The294 theorem cannot forbid this exit, because the candidate was never
an actual complete packet in the same factor. An entry identity omitted using
the spatial horizon has the same limitation.

Consequently the reduced packet system is at best a sound language-continuation
OVERAPPROXIMATION unless it retains old-archive occupancy guards. It is not a
physical bisimulation or an autonomous algorithm deciding whether the true
factor continues. A real occupied-fence no-read certificate, independent of a
hypothetical candidate's language validity, can justify stronger omission in
its protected region. Identity age by itself does not provide that certificate
for arbitrary future ant motion.

This is the exact additional obligation for an effective complete frontier:
control old-archive WHITE guard failures, or prove those guards irrelevant by
genuine causal shielding. The295-layer count alone does not solve it.

## 3. The remaining post-entry-expiration question

The next note supplies an actual all-N family showing that bounded C age ALONE
permits arbitrarily many spatially remote produced cells that really are used
later. That family's entry mass and radius grow with N and entry roots still
fund its return. It therefore does not disprove a uniform frontier theorem
restricted to clocks AFTER20R+211.

We have no actual post-expiration unbounded-frontier counterexample and no
uniform bound there. Inferring either conclusion from the early-clock family
would be unsupported. Conversely, after expiration all consumed identities
being produced in-factor does not make them independent new roots, bound their
branching, or bound the number of zero-C packets in future epochs.

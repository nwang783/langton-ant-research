# No pure ninefold, scale-three ant inflation

Status (22 October 2026, 20:32 UTC): the complete finite exclusion is independently
reproduced using two different constraint encodings. All 1,280 explicit CNFs also
have separately checked monotone-RUP proofs through the final empty clause.
There are 102,992 checked additions in total, at most 828 per case, zero RAT
steps, zero deletions, and zero UNKNOWN outcomes. The final hash-bound manifest
is k9_rup/manifest.json. A second complete audit has independently reconstructed
every original CNF and verified every RUP addition with a different propagation
implementation, so the result does not depend on either SAT solver or a shared
checker implementation.

## Exact theorem and scope

There is no ant turn sequence on **any initial coloring** of the square lattice,
finite or infinite, satisfying, with initial position zero and heading north,

    p(9n) = 3 p(n),  h(9n) = h(n)  for all n >= 0.

Already the first 729 turns cannot satisfy the identities through n=81.
This closes the sole remaining scale in the earlier finite-seed pure-inflation
classification through k=9. Even k is impossible directly from heading parity,
so the combined finite-seed exclusion actually covers every integer 2 <= k <= 10.
The theorem does not address position-only inflation, eventual or affine-corrected
inflation, or larger odd scales. It is not a proof of the full highway conjecture.

## Finite reduction

A block of nine turns starting at time 9n has displacement three times the single
coarse turn at time n, and its final heading is the corresponding coarse heading.
It must also be internally physically feasible. Direct enumeration of the 512
nine-letter words gives exactly nine eligible R images and nine eligible L images,
listed in free_inflations_k9.json and independently regenerated by the auditor.

Enumerate the first nine image choices in chronological order. The coarse letter
at index n is already present in the accumulated fine prefix (except n=0, where
both roots are considered and the first image must begin with its own root).
Prune exactly when two consecutive reads of the same physical cell have the same
turn letter. Initial first-read colors are completely unconstrained.

The complete tree has 12,697 internal search nodes and exactly 1,280 leaves at
nine images, i.e. 81 turns. These 1,280 fixed coarse words and fixed first-nine
image choices are stored in free_nine_coarse81_prefixes.csv. The set, not just its
cardinality, was independently regenerated and compared. Every hypothetical
infinite inflation has one of these prefixes.

For each leaf, ask whether the fixed 81-turn word can be refined to 81 images,
i.e. 729 turns, with its first nine image choices fixed to the leaf. No such
refinement exists in any of the 1,280 cases.

## Exact Boolean encoding

Each coarse index i chooses exactly one of its nine eligible images. For each
physical fine cell, list in chronological macro order every candidate macro
that could read it. Put a Boolean color before the first such macro and after
each such macro. The initial Boolean is free.

For a selected image, its read subsequence at this cell is either empty or an
internally alternating finite word. If empty, before and after colors agree.
Otherwise the before color is determined by its first turn (L black, R white),
and the after color is that color XOR the parity of the number of reads.
These implications, shared across all cells through the same one-hot choices,
are necessary and sufficient for literal ant feasibility of the concatenation.
They are not an over- or under-approximation and impose no bound on initial mass.

The direct implementation is smt_inflation_refinement.py. The independent audit
uses integer image choices and XOR color-toggle chains, with no imported source
geometry, in audits/check_k9_refinement_independent.py. Both report all 1,280
cases UNSAT, no UNKNOWN results. Twelve independently replayed feasible positive
controls are SAT under the audit encoding.

## Proof traces rather than bare solver statuses

cnf_inflation_refinement.py translates the same implications into explicit CNF:

* a nonempty read adds two binary guarded color clauses;
* an empty read adds the two ternary clauses for guarded equality;
* choices have one at-least-one clause and all pairwise at-most-one clauses;
* the first nine choices are fixed by unit clauses.

The final proof path writes those explicit clauses directly to DIMACS, without
any solver variable remapping. The untrusted producer prove_cnf_rup.cpp is a plain
first-UIP CDCL search engine with no preprocessing, clause minimization, or
deletion. It emits every learned clause and the final empty clause. Its source
need not be trusted for proof soundness: every addition is checked separately.

Each retained addition C is checked by reverse unit propagation: start from the
input CNF and preceding checked additions, assume every literal of C false, and
perform Boolean unit propagation. A contradiction proves that the previous formula
entails C. Induction therefore proves the final empty clause from the input CNF.
No learned clause is accepted merely because the solver supplied it.

check_drat.cpp contains the independent watched-literal checker. The final batch
requires every accepted addition to be RUP, with **zero RAT steps and zero
deletions**. Thus its more general unused RAT code is not part of the proof path.
certify_all_k9_plain_rup.py saves compressed CNF and RUP files, SHA256 hashes of
the uncompressed files, all initial choices, and per-case checker counts in
k9_rup/manifest.json. The manifest status is
ALL_1280_MONOTONE_RUP_CERTIFICATES_VERIFIED. Generation and checking took about
414.5 seconds. All 1,280 case records report valid=true, RAT=0, deletions=0.
Earlier native Z3 proof traces failed the standalone check, including some with
preprocessing and minimization disabled. Naive unit propagation independently
confirmed those failures. Those traces are explicitly unverified negative
controls and are not used by the theorem certificate; no claim about their
producer failure mechanism is needed.

## Negative search controls

The direct unrestricted DFS hit 100 million nodes without finishing, and a
future-cell frontier cache hit its ten-million-node cap. Those outcomes were
UNKNOWN, never exclusions. The exact SAT constraint system resolves the same
first coarse leaf in a fraction of a second. No positive infinite model was
inferred from the large feasible finite-prefix tree.

# A literal last-L obstruction forces an entire reverse V rail

## Statement, with all integer lengths retained

For integers r>=0 and 0<=j<=r+1, consider the actual fine-word prefix

    V^r, VCC, V^j

with initial heading N. The final j plain V atoms head S. The next actual zero-C complete-eight base cannot be D or U. Thus an old-eight continuation, before a foreign exit, must use at least r+2 reverse plain Vs before its first D/U. This is a literal initial-color obstruction. It needs no output-age law, periodic profile, finite-state quotient, ghost ancestry or claimed global grammar.

This theorem does not say a foreign cannot occur sooner. Its separate endpoint corollary below addresses exactly the three existing C3 return targets. It also does not extend to a mixed V/U forward rail or to D-separated V blocks without reconstructing their physical reads.

## 1. A finite affine interface proves every r and j

The forward plain V entries are (i,3i,N), i=0,...,r-1. VCC begins at (r,3r,N) and exits at (r,3r+4,S). After j reverse Vs the head is

    (r-j, 3r+4-3j, S).

    g_D=(n,3n+3),

at its zero-based context tick 11. This is marker-owned: the next actual word's RAW tick 1/read 2, not a physical read inside D RAW. An attempted U requires BLACK at

    g_U=(n,3n+2),

at its own RAW tick 12/read 13. The canonical formatter records both ownership modes. A complete old D with a genuine next word supplies the actual required prefix; no extra marker is executed.

Both cells are instead WHITE, by an actual earlier L and no intervening visit.

For a forward plain V at index i, either cell has local form (d,3d+c), where d=n-i and c=3 or 2. The checker obtains exact possible integer d from the literal RAW x bounds, not an assumed search box. Every actual line contact is:

- c=3: only d=-1, an L at RAW tick 1.
- c=2: d=0 gives R at tick 12; d=-1 gives the later L at tick 2.

The full VCC footprint is checked separately. Since its relative d is -j, only d<=0 matters. At j=0 its last reads of the two cells are L at ticks 19 and 20, in its own C1. At j=1 they are L at ticks 1 and 2. At j>=2 it does not read either cell.

For j>=1, the last deletion is therefore in forward packet index n+1, which exists between 0 and r. At n+1=r that packet is VCC; otherwise it is plain V. The edge case n=-1 is the initial packet's actual core L, not an invented negative-index producer. In every case the final deletion times in the whole rail are

    g_D: 18*(r-j)+19;
    g_U: 18*(r-j)+20.

A prior reverse V with index t<j would see either cell at local coordinate

    (d,3d+4-c), where d=j-t>=1.

The exact plain-V RAW footprint has NO such contact. Hence all reverse Vs already executed miss the deleted cell. The proposed D/U's listed L is also its first read of that cell. The repeated L with no intervening R is an immutable whole-board contradiction, for every r and j in the stated range.

Sixteen small prescribed regressions check the affine formulas against literal traces, but the universal statement is proved by the finite footprint/line identities above, not by extrapolating the regressions.

## 3. An all-length C3 endpoint corollary

Use one of the signed three C3 raw-C return targets, normalized to the old C at (0,0,N). Restrict only the W stage to

    D^a, V^r, VCC, with a>=1 and r>=0.

All displayed D/V atoms are plain. VCC supplies the two actual remaining Cs; the S stage is empty. The E start is exactly

    (-a-3r-4, 1+a+r, E).

Let the entire E stage have plain counts d,v,u. Direct endpoint arithmetic gives:

- F(2,-5,E): v+2u=r and d+v=a+r+6.
- Q(4,-5,E): v+2u=r+1 and d+v=a+r+6.
- B(5,-4,E): v+2u=r+2 and d+v=a+r+5.

Each equation forces d>0: the lower bounds are a+6, a+5 and a+3 respectively. Thus the E word necessarily has a first D/U. The literal rail lemma forces at least r+2 earlier Vs before that first non-V atom. For F/Q this already exceeds the total allowed E weight. For B it uses the whole E weight, leaving no U anywhere; but the signed actual complete-eight/foreign pair law requires the last old packet before B to be U. This is also impossible.

## Statement

foreign F, Q or B with its actual M11. The only possible local pair types are

    V -> F or Q;
    U -> F, Q or B.

Every such predecessor is plain: D and every C-tail packet DC, VC, VCC, UC,
UCC are impossible. In particular, the atom immediately before a FIRST foreign
following any uninterrupted complete-eight interval is never C.

This last conclusion concerns the actual full-packet decomposition before that

a foreign or in a longer post-foreign C run outside the stipulated complete-
eight prefix; such a history is not silently recoded as a legal old packet.

large-age first-foreign branches arose from the genuine C-to-F full-turn wrap.
The present local contract sets b=0 at the first foreign after a complete-eight
prefix, rather than dropping the wrap algebraically or assuming positivity.

The scope is FIRST foreign relative to the stipulated uninterrupted old-eight
interval. Reusing the same conclusion after arbitrary foreign excursions would
require a separately established new complete-eight suffix and actual full

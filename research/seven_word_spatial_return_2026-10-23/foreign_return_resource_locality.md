## 6. What remains unresolved

The new DCUV theorem gives a legitimate effective spatial return operator,
not a common decreasing resource for its iterations. Mass grows; the locality
radius is recalculated from that new mass; old exterior support persists and
can matter at later returns. The inherited214-state F/Q/B-only subgraph is acyclic, but
mixed DCUV sections can return repeatedly through its full strongly connected
component. This graph restriction is not a theorem about every physically
feasible raw seven-word factor: the separate port-interface work retains
physical QF and BF controls absent from that inherited graph.
Neither recurrence nor finite port count supplies a physical complete-state
cycle. The missing global step is a resource or causal transport restriction
across these growing return boards, or a genuine finite-seed countercontrol.

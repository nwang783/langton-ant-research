# Conditional reflex-loop geometry at the remaining DC/C1 interface

This note supplies exact geometry and a witness target, NOT an impossibility
of enclosing a finite prefix. No arbitrary U-entry is assumed to belong to a
global bi-exterior section. The coupled-stream cap-four or bi-infinite
translation hypotheses are not imported.

## Actual path and doubled coordinates

Assume an actual U-entry complete-eight factor, its first complete F/Q/B,
and the immediate returned RAW C. Suppose the returned chord's chronological
FIRST new contact is an old C1 of DC in the three surviving C3 residues.
Normalize that old C to head(0,0,N), at corrected doubled center q=(-1,-1).

The creating D starts at(-1,-1,N) in head coordinates. Its corrected chord

The returned C begins at head(0,-1,W), corrected center(1,-3), and runs NW
to q. Its endpoint is the old C's start and creating D's end; outgoing-old-
vertex ownership correctly assigns the contact to C, not to incoming D.

The actual foreign pieces, in these same doubled coordinates, are:

- Q(4,-5,E): (7,-9) to(5,-3) to(1,-3).
- B(5,-4,E): (9,-7) to(7,-1) to(1,-3).

core or a separately owned packet. Intervening old D/V/U/C atoms use the
usual actual corrected-center chords.

Let Gamma be the path from q along the old C, all later old atoms, those
repaired foreign pieces and returned C back to q. The signed first-foreign

## Positive orientation and the reflex vertex

The source-inclusive old C clock to foreign is3. Exactly one foreign is
present; its actual predecessor is plain V or U, not C. The signed repaired
open-turn formula gives5*pi/2 from the old C's outgoing NE tangent to the
returned C's incoming NW tangent. Closing NW to NE contributes -pi/2.
Gamma therefore has positive total turn2*pi.

At q the outgoing ray is NE and the preceding boundary ray points SE toward
(1,-3). The positive Jordan interior occupies the REFLEX270-degree sector,
the complement of the open east-facing90-degree wedge. The incoming creating
D, approached backward from q toward(-3,-3), lies strictly in that interior
sector. This is not a boundary/collinearity ambiguity: the old C only occupies
the NE ray, not its backward SW extension.

The earlier prefix from the factor's U entry through creating D is connected
and disjoint from Gamma except at q, by old simplicity and the first-contact
premise. Since a terminal portion of that prefix lies inside Gamma, its entire
earlier prefix lies in the bounded Jordan component.

## What would actually yield an obstruction

Enclosing a finite initial prefix is NOT itself contradictory. The prepared
finite DC-entry controls show why a source-free claim is unsafe. One needs an
independently justified continuous exterior witness attached to an actual
point of that prefix, reaching outside Gamma while avoiding its whole boundary.
Such a witness might come from a current physical component or inherited
exterior path, but its edges must actually remain available through this
first foreign and returned C. Initial core edges, retired identity edges,
ghost historical connectors and bare section membership do not qualify.

No such witness is proved in this note. In particular existing stream/global
bi-exterior analyses report that their bi-infinite-translation or specialized
section hypotheses do not hold automatically at a generic U-entry. The only
proved conclusion here is the conditional interior-prefix geometry.

## Endpoint predecessor consequences

Actual complete-eight adjacency permits only last V/U before F/Q, and only U
before B. Since at least one E packet remains, remaining E weight is at least1
for F/Q and at least2 for B. Hence every spent portal has T>=3 for F and
T>=2 for Q/B. The earliest possible V portal, T=1 after any number of D bases,
can never be spent in these residues. The earliest U portal, T=2, is excluded
for F only; Q/B remain genuinely open.

This is an actual endpoint-aware restriction, unlike the withdrawn inference
that a plain source must own C,C. It does not exclude later portals, bound
the unbounded D gaps, remove inactive physical archive sites, or close the
three returned-C cases.

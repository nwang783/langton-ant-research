## 1. The earlier prefix really is inside this loop

In doubled corrected-center coordinates, the creating D runs from (-1,-1) to q=(1,1), and old C1 then runs from q to (3,3). The returned C runs from (3,-1) back to q.

The signed preforeign U-entry simplicity and first-foreign extension make the old path simple. The exact one-foreign clock is five quarter turns along the open old-C-to-returned-C arc. At q, the closing NW-to-NE principal turn is minus one quarter turn. The loop consequently has total turn four quarter turns and is counterclockwise. Its q corner is reflex, with 270-degree interior. The 90-degree excluded wedge points east between NE and SE.

The earlier prefix without q is connected. Jordan separation therefore puts that entire prefix in the bounded component, not merely a prepared artificial coloring or an inferred historical wire.

Enclosing a finite prefix is not by itself a contradiction. The useful consequence here is its strict height inequality. No attached path to infinity or bi-exterior normalization is assumed.

## 2. The WHOLE loop's minimum, not just the foreign endpoints

For physical head y:

- W D/V/U steps change y by 1,1,0. Thus every W head has y at least 2.
- The W-to-S C changes y by zero.
- S D/V/U steps change y by -1,-3,-4.
- The S-to-E C changes y by -1.
- E D/V/U steps change y by -1,-1,0.

All S and E progress is therefore nonincreasing in y. Every E head has y at least the foreign's y; every S head has y at least the foreign's y plus one. These are all-length cone statements, not bounded count enumeration.

## 3. A bounded preceding N run follows

Take the maximal zero-C N-oriented run immediately before the selected D entry. If a preceding C exists, its N-oriented exit is this run's start. If its atom counts are d,v,u, its vertical weight is m=d+3v+4u; its start has doubled corrected-center y=-1-2m in the selected-D frame.

That point is in the STRICT bounded component established above. It has y strictly greater than the loop minimum. Therefore m is at most two for F/Q, or at most one for B. V has weight three and U has weight four, so the run consists solely of k plain D packets, with k at most 2/2/1.

If there were no preceding C in the factor, the creating U base at the factor entry would itself contribute vertical displacement four before the selected D. Its earlier entry corrected-center y would be at most -9, contradicting either strict height bound. This includes all U-family starts: UC/UCC already have a preceding own C; a genuinely initial N run can only occur before the first C. Thus an actual preceding E-to-N C is forced.

That C must be the final C of its actual completed full packet, because the next atoms up to the selected D have no Cs. Its possible full parents are DC/VC/UC or the second C of VCC/UCC. A C1 of VCC/UCC cannot be used as if its mandatory C2 vanished.

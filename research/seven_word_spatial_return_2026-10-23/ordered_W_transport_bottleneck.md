# The indirect W-to-E identity-transport bottleneck

## Scope

Keep exactly the arbitrary-length first-foreign old-C returned-RAW-C setting of
ordered_W_archive_matching.md. The old C is at (0,0,N), W starts at (0,1,W) at
clock 1, S has clock 2, E has clock 3, and the foreign is F(2,-5,E), Q(4,-5,E)
or B(5,-4,E) at clock 3. The immediately returned RAW C starts at (0,-1,W),
with its actual M11 continuation. The existing matching/first-D theorem is a
dependency, not silently presumed independently of its own scope.

There is no bound on the lengths of these stages. A stage's final fullpacket
can own its next C, or the W fullpacket can own both C tails and skip S.
Every E packet is plain D, V or U: the foreign is the first later non-eight
word, and no additional old C occurs before it. All producer-to-consumer
exclusions below are applied in the actual old complete-eight factor BEFORE
the first foreign, never to a returned consumer after it.

The conclusion concerns actual black residual-identity ancestry, not every
counterfactual color influence or failed-WHITE guard. All dormant ports and the
whole board remain retained.

## Completed sources cannot fund at full-source age zero or one

Enumerate every full output of all eight fullpackets against every input of
all eight full consumers. Use the actual source-inclusive, consumer-exclusive
C clock and relative heading -age modulo 4. At age zero the 1,564 alignments
split into 760 sector-inactive, 770 signed COUNT5, and 34 whose putative source
has not yet completed. At age one they split into 804, 748, and 12 respectively.

The final class in each case consists only of VCC/UCC sources, which need two
Cs to complete. Their outputs cannot supply a full consumer entered before
that completion. This qualification is essential: one must not identify an
internal C output's physical birth clock with its full source-entry clock.

Thus no actual input can be funded by a completed fullproducer output at
full-source-entry age zero or one. In this setting:

- W cannot fund later W or S inputs.
- S cannot fund later S or E inputs.
- E cannot fund later E inputs.

These are input/output identity statements. For example a current W output
can still obstruct an S packet's WHITE guard; no such functional influence is
being discarded.

## E reproductive packets spend additional W-entry identities

For an E V packet choose its actual distinguished input (1,2). For an E U
packet choose its actual distinguished LOWER input (0,3). This U port is NOT
the upper input (-1,4) chosen for the separate W ordered archive matching.

An E distinguished input newly produced since W entry could come only from W
at full source age two: S and E supplies were just excluded. Among all 136 W
full-output to these two E input-port alignments, 64 are sector-inactive and
68 are signed COUNT5 exclusions. Exactly four remain:

- VCC original base A=(0,1) into V or U distinguished input;
- UCC recycled q=(-1,2) into V or U distinguished input.

The signed V coefficient-one port theorem excludes both A cases at all ages,
including these actual parallel/antiparallel roles. The signed genuine-U q
consumer theorem excludes both q cases: q can retire only into the V/VC lower
port (0,3), not V's distinguished (1,2), and not any U port. These laws are
used before the first foreign on the actual simple old factor.

Consequently every E V/U distinguished input is an actual residual identity
already current at W entry. It has not previously been spent. These E inputs
are distinct from one another and from all chosen inputs used by the W
archive matching. No new root reset or entry-ID relabeling is involved.

## A direct W-to-E-D relay cannot create a future-visible output

Plain D has just one full output, its fresh common (2,0). At heading E, if its
actual input (0,1) is a W output identity at physical site q, its new output
is at q+(-1,-2). An output meeting the actual future context, whose minimum
x is -3, therefore requires q.x>=-2.

For a W head (-A,1+B), every full output has physical x=-A-local_y with
local_y>=0. Hence A<=2. The only possible earlier W prefixes are empty, D
and DD, with heads (0,1), (-1,2), (-2,3). The first W base is D-family by the
previous matching/first-D theorem. These three heads cover every W length,
not just words of depth two.

The checker enumerates every full output at these heads and every actual
combined future context. Exactly 24 proposed E-D outputs would be visible.
Every corresponding W-source-relative D pose is sector-inactive, at source
age two. Thus none is an actual relay. An E D that consumes a W output can
exist elsewhere, but its only fresh output misses this future context.

## Conserved boundary resource and the remaining opening

The chosen W archive inputs and E V/U distinguished inputs form disjoint
once-spent subcohorts of the SAME W-entry residual. The W archive's common
outputs remain current, ordered and unread through this first foreign return.
An actual W-output ancestry edge directly into an E producer with a
future-visible output must target V or U, and that packet additionally spends
one of the older W-entry distinguished identities just described. A chain
through S or through another E producer is excluded by the exact young-age
input inventory, while an E-D direct relay is invisible.

This is not a contraction: a V/U packet can have several outputs, and the
unchosen inputs and outputs are still present. It isolates a genuine
additional older-port cost for the only direct W-to-E active identity channel.
Arbitrary S/E geometry, indirect color constraints, and the three raw-C contact
residues remain open. No second-foreign iteration or returned-eight maturity
law follows.

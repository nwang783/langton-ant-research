Signed complete-eight/foreign adjacency leaves only plain V or U immediately

At an old internal vertex assign the contact to its outgoing old atom, with
old parameter 0<=s<1. A later foreign contact cannot occur at the old terminal

The chronological first-contact Jordan premise is therefore available without

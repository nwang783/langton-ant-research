# A contact-or-persistence lemma for a foreign-missed genuine U star

Status: author proof, pending independent review. This is a coupled conditional lemma, not a new global simplicity theorem.

## Exact interface

Let an actual U-entry complete-eight prefix contain a genuine U/UC/UCC creator, normalized to U-base entry `(0,0,N)`. Its original star consists of source-R identities at `(0,0),(0,1),(1,0),(1,1)`. Let A be the prefix's FIRST complete F/Q/B. Assume A's **complete RAW plus actual following M11 context misses all four cells**. Append any finite actual ordinary RAW tail over D/C/V/U, with the actual following-word M11 at each considered cut. There is no second foreign.

CURRENT at an ordinary RAW cut means physically BLACK, the SAME original source-R identity, and outside that cut's actual canonical entry K. An alive original in K has already lost CURRENT status, even though its physical L occurs later. No old-eight age law is used after A. There is no fictitious core at the internal F56 cut of Q/B.

**Conditional conclusion.** If the whole expanded corrected-center proxy is simple through any completed ordinary tail prefix, then the same original LEFT pair `(0,0),(0,1)` is CURRENT at its terminal RAW cut. Equivalently, a first loss of either original LEFT identity forces a proxy contact by completion of its loss atom. That contact may involve any earlier proxy segment, not necessarily the creating U chord. It is not automatically a CURRENT physical barrier.

Consequently, at the **entry** of any global first new proxy-contact atom, LEFT is still current, provided A missed all four originals. Nothing is asserted through that contact atom.

## 1. Only one no-creating-chord first-loss gate

Before a first LEFT loss, both originals are current. For an ordinary atom b, a compatible current BLACK cell outside entry K either misses its complete RAW+next-M11 context or is a genuine true input. A RAW L physically deletes the identity; an exit-core capture counts the earlier ordered loss. This includes marker-owned reads and does not require postforeign maturity.

The checker exhaustively aligns either LEFT cell to every TRUE requirement of each of the four literal atomic contexts, in all four headings, retaining the active sector and the true entry-core restriction. Reject any before-WHITE conflict with the held pair. If the atom's chord misses the creating U segment, the **only** first-loss gate is

`U at (1,-1,N)`.

It consumes both LEFT originals in own U RAW (ticks14 and13). It requires original-cell `(1,0)` to be WHITE before entry and `(1,1)` to be BLACK. The latter may be an original or a replacement; the proof does not assume which. Every other first-loss role either conflicts with held BLACK/core requirements or contacts the old creating U chord. Such a contact already gives the lemma's allowed alternative.

## 2. Held-edge Jordan clock, with actual source-tail interfaces

Suppose for contradiction that a first LEFT loss occurs while the expanded proxy remains simple. The previous paragraph forces U at `(1,-1,N)`, whose doubled chord is `(1,-3)` to `(1,5)`.

The original `w=(0,1)` edge is

`B=(-1,3) to E=(1,1)`.

B is the creating U chord's strict midpoint. Follow the actual proxy from B, through the remainder of that U, its real source C tails, the old prefix, exactly one repaired foreign, and the ordinary tail to E, the strict midpoint of the consuming U chord. Close by the same original w edge from E back to B.

- The source remainder meets w only at B. Literal UC/UCC tails never reread w, and their actual chords miss it.
- Until the consuming base, w is the same current identity. The signed ordinary and repaired-foreign current-edge barriers keep all intervening proxy pieces disjoint from it.
- The final consuming U first half meets w only at E, checked exactly.
- The assumed simple proxy makes its B-to-E subarc simple. Thus this closure is a genuine Jordan curve, not a loop made from historical wires.

Let n be the actual source-U-entry-inclusive, consumer-U-entry-exclusive C count, including any creator C tails. The signed complete-eight→foreign literal adjacency table says the atom immediately before A is not C. The exact repaired-proxy tangent lift therefore makes the open turn `(n+2) pi/2`. Both endpoint tangents are N. The bridge adds `+pi/4` at E and `-pi/4` at B, net zero. Jordan turning gives

`n+2 = +4 or -4`.

Since n is nonnegative, n=2. The negative alternative n=-6 is impossible. No sampled upper clock is used.

## 3. The genuine deleted-to-WHITE prerequisite cannot be funded

At consuming U entry, `(1,0)` must be WHITE. Its original source-R identity was BLACK and CURRENT just after A because A's complete context missed all four star cells. Hence it had an earlier first loss in an ordinary atom. Choose that earliest loss, counting an exit-core capture as a loss at the earlier atom rather than hiding it until next-word read2.

LEFT is still held before this earlier atom. Both LEFT cells must MISS its complete context. Its chord cannot contact the creating U chord under the assumed simple whole proxy. The checker exhaustively leaves exactly four first-loss ports for `(1,0)`:

| Atom | Entry | Exit | Physical first L |
|---|---|---|---|
| D | `(1,-1,N)` | `(2,0,N)` | next actual word tick1/read2 |
| V | `(1,-3,N)` | `(2,0,N)` | next actual word tick1/read2 |
| U | `(2,-2,N)` | `(2,2,N)` | own U tick13/read14 |
| U | `(2,-4,N)` | `(2,0,N)` | next actual word tick1/read2 |

All are N-heading zero-C atoms. With exactly one foreign already executed, an N heading means their source-inclusive C clock m satisfies `m+2 = 0 mod4`. Because `0 <= m <= n=2`, m=2. Thus no C remains between completion of this earlier loss atom and consuming U entry.

Every actual following N-heading zero-C atom is D, V, or U, with nonnegative x and y head increments. From an exit with `x>=2,y>=0` it cannot reach consuming U entry `(1,-1,N)`. This is only the actual following zero-C run, not a bound on unrelated older clocks. It contradicts the needed WHITE cell.

The contradiction proves the conditional lemma.

## Scope and use

- The arbitrary length ordinary tail is covered by choosing its first LEFT loss, not by iterating a finite corner-state abstraction.
- The foreign complete-context-MISS assumption is essential to the proof. It is **not** inferred from geometry. Signed first-foreign effect bound 9fdb supplies it when the true creator-entry-to-A C age is at most4. That corollary uses only preforeign ages.
- If A actually erodes a star identity, this note makes no claim. A broader all-star erosion/contact implication remains open.
- The actual prepared 29-cell F,D,UCC,V,D,D control from c5a00c92 is consistent: A misses all four labels, but its second U already contacts the hypothetical old U chord when the LEFT pair first fails. Its labels are prepared initial BLACK, not a genuine creating-U ancestry.
- A proxy contact is an explicit permitted outcome. This note does not rule out any contact, prove later-return simplicity, or establish an unconditional old-star permanence theorem across a foreign word.

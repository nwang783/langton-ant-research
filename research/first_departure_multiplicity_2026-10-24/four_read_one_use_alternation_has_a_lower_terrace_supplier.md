# Four-read one-use alternation has an actual lower-terrace supplier

This is the remaining mode after the three-read staircase collision;
it is not claimed to be impossible or physically realized as a front.
Complete WHITE histories, actual GLOBAL-M selected one-use b subpath

singleton. No criticality or generic age bound is assumed.

## 1. An explicit odd-reader alternative is retained

The cap-free L3 transport lemma58fb5427 gives each shared LRL reader
SSSN (four reads), SSNNS or SSSNN (five reads). Thus either a shared
reader is already a five-read odd WHITE output, or both are SSSN.
This is a local alternative, not a distinct-root/output injection.
A complete cap-four front uses the latter alternative automatically.
No uncapped five-read escape is silently pruned.

Normalize the even-reader alternative to

    A=(0,-1):WWEW; r=(0,0):N; B=(1,0):WWEW;
    s=(1,1):N; C=(2,1):WWEW;
    U=(1,-1):SSSN; V=(2,0):SSSN.

The outer reader E=(2,-1) has complete Ein1,Nout1. The cap-free exact
WHITE alphabet is EWW/EWWW/WWE/WWEW: equal R heading counts are one
each, no L arrives E, and the total length is three or four. No imposed
reader cap is needed.

## 2. Actual time orders force the low O/flow-three profiles

The unique N output of E feeds V's only N input, its L3. V.L3 feeds
B's third W input, its L3; B.L3 feeds U's third S input, its R2. Hence

    time(E.Nout)=T_3(V)-1=T_2(U)-3.

E's unique E input is supplied by U's only E output, its L1, so its time
is T_1(U)+1. Since T_2(U)-T_1(U)>=4, the N output cannot be earlier than
the E input. They cannot be the same read, since no allowed word has an
L_E. Therefore EWW/EWWW survive; WWE/WWEW have a positive-clock conflict.

These surviving E words have R0 heading E and R2 heading W. Exact ranks
give the two actual supplier channels

    B.R2 -> U.L1 -> E.R0,
    E.R2 -> V.L3 -> B.L3.

Consequently

    T_0(E)=T_2(B)+2,
    T_3(B)=T_2(E)+2,
    T_3(B)-T_2(B)=(T_2(E)-T_0(E))+4>=12.

The lower reader's whole R0-to-R2 interval lies strictly inside B's
R2-to-L3 stock interval. It is a genuine chronological supplier, not a
flow-only association, assumed external age, or first/last ownership map.
All optional E.L3 reads stay present when E=EWWW.

## 3. The terrace is lower, not another max-level curve

U and V have two S R reads and no N R. Their NW sectors are M, so their
opposite SE sectors are M-2. These are E's two C sectors. Thus E is an
O root (EWW) or flow-three root (EWWW) on C level M-2, NOT a max-C root.
Its L levels are M-3 on its N side and M-4 or M-5 on its S side.
This low stock may share a valley/odd root with other suppliers; no
per-site root or fuel payment is inferred.

The two complete25/26-event local interfaces have exact physical ports,
GLOBAL-C equations and injective event-disjoint component calendars.
They are still only necessary constraint controls. Exterior readers,
full voltages, a physical prepared factor, or a complete front are not
supplied by those calendars.

LEFT4,RIGHT1. Write A_i=(i+1,i), R_i=(i+1,i+1), D_i=(i+2,i):SSSN,
and E_i=(i+2,i-1):EWW/EWWW. All copies are physical lifts. If n is the
number of LEFT full-quotient classes then the b lift shifts by -d after
n indices, so d=(-n,-n) in this normalization.

    E_i.R2 -> D_i.L3 -> A_i.L3 -> D_(i-1).R2 -> A_(i-1).L1

    -> A_(i-1).R2 -> D_(i-2).L1 -> E_(i-1).R0.

Every arrow is a matched physical ordinal, not a repaired edge. Family
x-y offsets distinguish A,R,D,E even in short quotients. Across all i,

i traverses ONE full drift d and yields

    P=10*n+sum_i(T_2(E_i)-T_0(E_i)).

Each span<P follows from positive terms; P>=18*n is an arithmetic
consequence only. This is not added to overlapping age/current budgets,
and the circle itself is NOT asserted to exist. No standard or general

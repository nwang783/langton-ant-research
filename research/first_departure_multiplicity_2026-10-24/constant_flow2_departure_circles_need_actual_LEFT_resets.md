## 1. Statement and complete local data

Let gamma be an actual first-R departure b-cycle, every selected physical
edge having TOTAL multiplicity two. The selected C level is constant by
the signed first-R rule. Its complete local alphabet, after normalization,
is therefore

    RIGHT, cycle incoming E/outgoing S: EEWW or EWWE;
    LEFT,  cycle incoming E/outgoing N: WEE, WEEW or WWEE.

Write O=WEE, U=WEEW, V=WWEE at LEFT contacts. The three-read O is retained.
Both R headings are opposite at LEFT corners. No global maximum level,
K1, output-free assumption, or bound on return spans is imposed here.

THEOREM. At least one LEFT contact of gamma is O or U. Equivalently, an
all-V flow-two b-circle is impossible, regardless of its turn word.

## 3. Chords and arbitrary exterior histories cannot bypass the partition

Every physical vertex off gamma lies in one of its two complementary
components; give all its read events that component's label. A grid edge
with neither endpoint on gamma cannot cross gamma. An unused cycle-chord
edge joining two nonconsecutive gamma vertices has its entire open
interior in ONE complementary component; hence the local exterior-port
labels at BOTH endpoints agree. If only one endpoint is on gamma, its
exterior port agrees with the off-curve endpoint's component as well.

Thus nonlocal adjacency, passive/current contacts, arbitrary multiplicity
in the exterior, and every possible chord are accounted for. They cannot
carry an actual event from RIGHT to LEFT when every LEFT contact is V.
There is no geometric crossing assumption about individual repaired
arcs: this uses only the embedded physical b-circle and the full actual
port lists at its vertices.

The labels are invariant under the actual full drift. Both sets are
nonempty already at each cycle edge. They therefore give a nontrivial
successor-invariant partition of the actual P-event quotient. But its
next-event map is one P-cycle. This contradiction proves the theorem.

## 4. The exact surviving reset is source-L1/target-R2

The excluded wall does NOT justify imposing one-use behavior. At LEFT O
or U, the cycle incoming ranks are(1,2), outgoing ranks(0,1). The actual
read-level side table is exactly

    R0: exterior RIGHT -> cycle first RIGHT;
    L1: cycle first RIGHT -> cycle second LEFT;
    R2: cycle second LEFT -> exterior RIGHT;
    L3, when present: exterior RIGHT -> exterior RIGHT.

Hence these are genuine actual crossing/reset contacts, with L1 entering
LEFT and its later R2 exiting LEFT. The optional L3 is explicitly retained.
No claim is made that these resets are fresh fuel, have bounded age, are

The theorem concerns closed constant-flow-two b-circles. Finite segments
can communicate around their endpoints; multiplicity changes at RIGHT

# Auxiliary-corner scope clarification

24 October 2026. Addendum to 84e543c3, immutable original unchanged.

The phrase "off-curve B" in the singleton-LEFT four-L note is descriptive
shorthand, not a hypothesis or a tested constraint. The 240-case cover
imposes NO condition that B is absent from gamma. It retains all complete
WHITE one-to-four-read E/W-axis histories at B, regardless of whether a
distant part of gamma also visits that physical cell or its full-drift
class. The code imposes only the stated physical ports, rank clocks,
next-read condition, and prescribed four-L return.

Accordingly read B as the AUXILIARY square corner, with its gamma
membership unspecified. The four surviving open profiles and the forced
adjacent RIGHT Y/Y histories remain a valid necessary overapproximation.
The global-M exclusion of a singleton U four-step return uses those
adjacent RIGHT profiles and the signed five-contact staircase relation,
not any off-curve placement of B.

This clarification adds no exclusion, side budget, fuel charge or new
canonical-node identification. It only removes an unsupported placement
implication from the wording.

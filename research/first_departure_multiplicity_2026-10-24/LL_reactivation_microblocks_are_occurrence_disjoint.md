# LL reactivation microblocks are occurrence-disjoint

24 October 2026. Author dependent global chronology lemma pending review.
Assume the exact main scope and six cyclic reactivation inventories of
7a4d0c56: complete WHITE cap-four, actual FULL P,d, critical GLOBAL-M
b-circle Gamma, no one-use edge. This counts only GENUINE LL-first O/U
roots; passive VV bridges have no assigned reactivation charge.

## 1. A microscopic loop, not a deletable ant loop

For each genuine LL root r let X be its following flat RIGHT X. Its
actual same-pose interval X.R2->X.L3 is exactly one of

    P: RRRR, length4;
    N: RRLLLLRR, length8.

Every middle/future read is the exact one in7a4d0c56, including T/U L3
stock in N. Put I_X=[T2(X),T3(X)) and enlarge it to

    B_X=[T2(X),T3(X)+1),

so the block includes the actual endpoint L3 departure as well. Its
length is5 or9. It stays entirely LEFT in one long critical owner return.
The microscopic loop has zero displacement, but deleting it would
change colors and later reads. No ant splice or canonical-node identity
is asserted.

## 2. No other LL-post-X R2 can start inside one block

Every LL-post-X has a complete AABB heading pattern (EEWW or a rotation)
and starts its reactivation with TWO consecutive actual R reads.
Inspect the complete event list of either microscopic loop:

- The interior Y.R2 has ABBA, not AABB, so cannot be another post-X.
- In P, D is at R0, not R2.
- In N, T/U/Y interior L reads are not R2. D.R2 has either a three-read
  history or ABBA, never a four-read AABB post-X history.
- R.R2 is the only other possible four-read AABB R2. Its IMMEDIATE
  successor is the original X.L3, an L read. Thus it cannot start either
  reactivation word, both of which start RR.
- The endpoint is L3, not another R2.

This uses physical read ranks and complete histories, so drift cannot
relabel an interior R0/L3 as another R2 or rotate AABB into ABBA. If two
actual B_X blocks overlapped, the later one's R2 start would be one of
these prohibited events in the earlier block. Equal starts of different
physical events are impossible for one cursor. Hence the blocks are
pairwise disjoint in physical time, INCLUDING all full-drift translates.

The map from a genuine LL-root class to its following X class is
injective: cyclic predecessor geometry at a given RIGHT X fixes its
immediately preceding V and LL-first root, with all headings retained.
A full-drift identification of two Xs identifies those two root units as
well. There is no per-root assignment to a distinct long OWNER; several
blocks may lie in one long return.

## 3. Safe additive time reservation

Let nP,nN be the numbers of P/N genuine LL-root quotient classes.
Their original four-L [L1,R2) returns occupy4(nP+nN) LEFT read slots per P.
The blocks B_X are disjoint from all those four-L returns: a reactivation
block belongs to a single LEFT owner, contains R reads, and hence cannot
be owned by any four-L LL root. Their endpoint L3 departure remains LEFT.
Thus, writing L_RIGHT for the actual total RIGHT edge-time per P,

    P >= L_RIGHT + 4(nP+nN) + 5nP + 9nN
      = L_RIGHT + 9nP + 13nN.

This is one occurrence-level reservation inside the signed side-current
time partition, not a sum of overlapping clock lower bounds. It must not
be added to old physical-backbone/time budgets without checking their
occurrences separately. Other lower bounds may safely be combined by MAX.

There is no original-fuel, odd-output or fresh-root payment. A long owner
may service many blocks and may be a translated occurrence of a previously
used owner class. The reservation gives no upper bound on nP,nN,P, the
number of passive VV bridges or strip length, and is vacuous if no genuine
LL crossing exists. No complete-front exclusion or prepared factor follows.

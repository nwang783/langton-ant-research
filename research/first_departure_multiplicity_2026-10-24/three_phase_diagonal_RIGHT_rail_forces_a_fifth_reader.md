## 1. The two adjacent helpers have complete port counts

Let L0=(0,1), L2=(-1,0), Q=(-1,1). Their incoming axes are EW,EW,NS.

    incoming W=0, outgoing S=2.

The first count is the zero W departure of S=(1,1), and the second is the
two S arrivals of F. Under a complete WHITE cap-four inventory these
force exactly

    L0=EEE or EEEE.

At L2 the full port counts are

    incoming W=2, outgoing S=0.

The first count is F's two W departures. The second comes from B+d
=(-1,-1), whose complete NNN history has no S arrival. These force

    L2=WEW or WEWE.

Both helper domains allow the even optional last L and the odd output;
no prepared-color assumption is substituted for complete history.

## 2. One more actual reader forces the odd choices and its R0

Q receives N from L2, so its N-in count is 3 or 4. Its E departures supply
all E arrivals of L0, so its E-out count is 3 or 4. Exhausting all complete
WHITE NS histories of at most four reads gives only

    Q=NNNS or NSNN, with N-in=E-out=3.

Indeed N-in4 would require NNNN, whose E-out is only two. With N-in3,
E-out4 is impossible in four reads, while E-out3 has the two stated
profiles. This forces

    L0=EEE, L2=WEW.

In both Q alternatives its first N arrival and first E departure are
the SAME actual R0. The first N departure at L2 and first E arrival/S
departure at L0 are R0 as well.

## 3. Universal actual four-R reversal

Exact physical first ordinals now force

    F.R2 -> L2.R0 -> Q.R0 -> L0.R0 -> F.L1.

not R0. Its first R turns around the other C face; R counts are one

not a completion of the rail or a renewable controller.

If L0 or L2 themselves have more reads, their cap-four domains used above
also need not hold. The safe global conclusion is therefore that at

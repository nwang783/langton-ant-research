# Flat RRLL strips have unbounded open injective chronology

24 October 2026. Author local construction pending independent review.
This is a necessary local port/chronology control, NOT an ant board
factor, completed exterior, translating front or regenerative controller.
Its main role is to prevent treating a long flat cluster as many fresh
resources or assuming reader/rank conflicts accumulate with strip length.

## 1. Exact family, all positive integer lengths

For any integer k>=1, prescribe the complete WHITE histories on a
2 by (2k+2) physical rectangle:

    a_j=(2j,0):    NSSN;   b_j=(2j+1,0):  EWWE;
    u_j=(2j,-1):   WWEE;   v_j=(2j+1,-1): NNSS,
                         0<=j<k;

and the four end cells

    L=(-1,-1): NNN; W=(-1,0): WEW;
    S=(2k,-1): EWE; Z=(2k,0): SSS.

Every a/b is a flat RIGHT Y in its own frame; every u/v is a passive
LEFT V. The high ends are the exact three-use sink/source. The b-path
through the flats has word

    RR, (LL,RR)^(k-1),

with one outside V at either end. Every physical port count, including
zero, agrees internally. There are 16k+12 prescribed read events. Only
the four END histories are odd, independently of k. Those are a FORMAL
odd inventory, not an attained output of a completed ant factor.
The cluster's signed R-minus-L turn is exactly +2 for every k.

## 2. Complete literal channels, not just feasible inequalities

The actual ordinal successor edges inside the rectangle form disjoint
finite literal channels. Write a read as its cell name followed by its
rank. The following list is a topological ORDER of entire channels:

1. v_(k-1),0 -> S0.
2. For j=k-1,...,0:
   v_j1 -> u_j0 -> a_j0 -> b_j0 -> v_j2 -> u_j1;
   and, if j>0, v_(j-1),0 -> u_j2.
3. L0 -> u_0,2; the singleton L1; the singleton S1.
4. Z0 -> b_(k-1),1 -> v_(k-1),3 -> S2;
   singleton Z1; Z2 -> b_(k-1),2;
   a_(k-1),1 -> b_(k-1),3.
5. For j=k-2,...,0:
   a_(j+1),2 -> b_j1 -> v_j3 -> u_(j+1),3 -> a_(j+1),3 -> b_j2;
   followed by a_j1 -> b_j3.
6. a_0,2 -> W0; singleton W1;
   L2 -> u_0,3 -> a_0,3 -> W2.

The formulas apply unchanged at k=1; the empty indexed ranges disappear.
Every read appears exactly ONCE. Every internal physical successor edge
is exactly one arrow in this list. Each channel starts at an unfilled
incoming exterior port and ends at an unfilled outgoing exterior port;
there is no omitted internal edge or silently discarded older read.

The order respects every prescribed cell's ranks 0<1<2<3. Within a
channel any consecutive reads of the same cell are exactly four steps
apart. Separate channels by sufficiently long positive delays. Choose
their starting times in the checker/axis-compatible mod-four phase.
Then every internal edge has duration one, every cell's rank gaps are at
least four, every prescribed event time is DISTINCT, and every physical
cell's times have the same mod-four residue. Thus this is stronger than
a max-plus feasibility check that might allow simultaneous unrelated
reads. The executable gives the exact index construction for all k.

## 3. What this construction does and does not settle

There is no upper bound on strip length from complete local ranks,
internal successor geometry, injective clocks or the number of odd end
histories alone. A per-RR-unit fresh-fuel/odd-output charge would fail on
this necessary family unless an ADDITIONAL genuine exterior theorem is
proved. Its curvature +2 does not shrink with k, so the family is fully
consistent with a possible all-length curvature obstruction.

The positive delays are STILL OPEN EXTERIOR CONNECTOR DEMANDS. No legal
turn word or initial board is supplied for them; they may have geometric,
stock, side-current or later-reader incompatibilities. In particular the
control is not a prepared ant factor and does not establish that all
channels can be concatenated by a real walker. Critical same-contact
returns, full-drift quotient voltage, complete positive-output growth
and a single global event orbit have NOT been imposed. These omissions
are exactly the next composition problem, not reasons to assume the
strip is globally impossible or globally attained.

The four finite k controls in the checker validate the symbolic index
formulas; they are not a length search or evidence for a front.

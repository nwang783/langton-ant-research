## 1. Two genuine packets have exactly one intervening V gap

Consider two consecutive GENUINE packets in the packet order with ONLY
singleton-LEFT gaps between them, and no passive packet or high gap.
Let n be the number of such singleton gaps. The preceding genuine
packet ends with an RR cap; the next genuine packet starts with the
LL-first O/U root.

If n=0, that genuine first-LL root is immediately after RR. The general
RR table requires the next LEFT endpoint to be passive V, contradiction.
Thus n>=1. The FIRST singleton is V because it touches the preceding
RR cap. The LAST singleton is also V: it cannot be singleton U by
c9574fb8, and if it were singleton O its following outside LEFT would
have to be V; that actual outside LEFT is the next genuine O/U root.

If n>=2, the last singleton V has neither neighboring flat in RR. Its
preceding gap is singleton LEFT and its following gap is genuine LL.
Thus both outer LEFTs are present and the signed GLOBAL-M five-corner
window e597889e applies. It forbids a middle passive V between those
flat RIGHTs: the middle LEFT must be O/U. Contradiction. Therefore n=1,
and the sole intervening singleton is V. Every read and older L3 in
that separator remains; this is an exact typed spatial adjacency gate.

## 2. A closed all-genuine staircase cannot service its own reactions

Suppose a complete packet circle had genuine packets but no passive
packet and no high gap. Section1 forces its full packet word to be

    genuine, V-singleton, genuine, V-singleton, ... .

All genuine LL-root LEFT returns are the exact four-L length-four
returns. All singleton LEFTs are passive V, with no crossing event.
There are no WEEE flow-three roots because there is no high gap.
These exhaust every Gamma contact type in the Stage38 packet partition.

But each genuine packet's post-X reactivation channel is wholly LEFT
and has a critical same-contact owner of span at least12 (positive) or
16 (negative), by signed7a4d0c56/09e98e96. An owner must be an actual
Gamma LEFT crossing root. None of the only available length-four roots
can own it, and a passive V has no LEFT entry at all. Contradiction.
No assumption of different owners for different packets is used.

Thus every maximal genuine-packet staircase reaches a PASSIVE packet
or HIGH gap in the finite full-quotient packet circle. This is a
terminal-TYPE statement, not a claim those terminals are distinct,
fresh, paid by original stock, or older in one universal time order.
An arbitrary number of staircase visits can share one terminal type.

## 3. Flat-only boundaries require passive packets

If there is no high gap and no genuine packet either, a circle without
passive packets consists only of singleton RL gaps: it is the entirely
alternating boundary already excluded by signed7637b9c1. Together with
section2, this proves that EVERY flat-only boundary has at least one
PASSIVE RRLL packet. A flat-only boundary containing genuine packets
cannot consist solely of them and passive singleton separators.

This preserves the actual full drift and quotient finiteness; it is
not a strict-spatial-decrease induction on an infinite lift. It does not
exclude flat-only boundaries with passive packets, mixed high launches,
old singleton-O owners or shared cross-curve stock continuations.

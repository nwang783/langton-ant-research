# A GLOBAL-M one-to-two crossing launch has an odd witness

24 October 2026. Author cap-free localized extension, pending review.
This removes the LL portal, its sink and its short return from 5b7a0101.
The only complete local source/root words supplied are

    B=(1,1):SN or SNN; Y=(0,1):EWW or EWWE.

They are consecutive RIGHT,LEFT vertices on an actual essential first-R
b-circle, B->Y. The incoming selected edge to B has physical total use1,
and B->Y has total use2. Y is the displayed O/U crossing profile, not a
passive or arbitrary higher-multiplicity LEFT word. Its two incident C
sectors have GLOBAL maximum height M. No cap is imposed on q=(1,2),
C=(0,2), or any other exterior cell. All histories are complete and WHITE.
No constant-height theorem for arbitrary all-cap b-circles is assumed.

## 1. Reader-independent actual incidence forces a LEFT predecessor

Let q be the physical b-predecessor of B. Since q.R0 supplies B's unique
S arrival, and B's first W output supplies Y's first W arrival,

    q.R0 -> B.R0 -> Y.L1.

q.R0 is also q's first E arrival. Therefore the first E output of C
has time T_1(Y)-3. Y's first N output is R2, so C's first N input has
time T_2(Y)+1. Thus C's first E output precedes every N input. Complete
WHITE alternation forces C to begin SS, with C.L1 that first E output.

If q were RIGHT, its selected incoming heading would be E, requiring
C.R0 to depart E into q. In fact C.R0 departs W. Hence q is LEFT on the
actual b-circle, regardless of either cell's later multiplicity. The
physical first-port argument is unchanged by B's optional third R2.
It uses no unproved side ownership or age bound.

## 2. GLOBAL maximum forces exactly three full supplier profiles

Y's opposite-R complete word makes both C sectors M. The physical sector
with bottom-left coordinate (0,1) is simultaneously Y's NE C sector,
q's SW C sector, and C's SE C sector. q.R0 arrives E and departs S, so
it selects that maximum sector. The signed cap-free output-one endpoint
formula, with this INDEPENDENT maximum premise, forces q to one of
E, EE, EEW, EEWE. The first two have no W input, incompatible with q
being LEFT. Thus q is a flow-one EEW or EEWE vertex.

At C let a=R_N, c=R_S, b=L_N, t=L_S and epsilon=R-L in {0,1}. Put
k=Nin(C)=1 for Y=EWW and2 for Y=EWWE; l=Eout(C)=2 for q=EEW and3 for
q=EEWE. Actual physical port counts and alternation give

    a+b=k, a+t=l, c=k+l-3*a+epsilon.

Since C begins S, c>=1. Its first-R NW sector minus its opposite SE
sector M equals c-a. GLOBAL maximality implies c-a<=0. In each of the
four (k,l) cases these integer equations give:

- (1,2): a=c=1,epsilon=1, and first SS forces C=SSN;
- (1,3): a=c=1,epsilon=0, and first SS forces C=SSNS;
- (2,2): a=c=1,epsilon=0, and first SS forces C=SSNN;
- (2,3): impossible, since c>=1 forces a<=1 and hence c-a>=1.

For example a>=2 in the last case gives c=5-3a+epsilon<=0;
a<=1 makes the height excess positive. No unknown reads are omitted.
These finite lengths are CONCLUSIONS from exact counts and GLOBAL M,
not a multiplicity cutoff or a global first-R-maximum rule.

Thus the full necessary supplier profiles are exactly

    (Y,q,C)=(EWW,EEW,SSN), (EWW,EEWE,SSNS), (EWWE,EEW,SSNN),

for either source word SN or SNN. No outgoing sink, following LEFT,
critical same-contact return, or other portal geometry was supplied.

## 3. Full-quotient odd-output injection

Choose the odd Y=EWW when present; otherwise choose the forced odd
q=EEW. A Y witness has L1 opposite its R0 heading and recovers the source
B=Y+E in this frame. A q witness has L1 equal to its R0 heading and
recovers B=q+S by its first actual departure. The two types are disjoint
under all rotations; each recovery is physical and full-drift equivariant.
Distinct source classes of these specified GLOBAL-M launches therefore
have distinct odd complete-WHITE witnesses.

If n_launch counts these source classes, then N_odd>=n_launch. Do not add
this to the exact-portal reservation: that is a special case of the SAME
injection. Source SNN may itself be odd, but is not reserved additionally.
This is output capacity only, not original BLACK fuel or an attained front.

## 1. A chronological argument forces C to begin SS

Since q.R0 is the unique south departure into B's only S arrival,
q.R0->B.R0. B's first west departure goes to Y's first W arrival,
which is Y.L1 in both EWW and EWWE. Thus

    T_0(q)=T_1(Y)-2.

q's first arrival is E, so C's FIRST east departure supplies that R0:

    time(first E output of C)=T_1(Y)-3.

On the other hand Y's FIRST north departure is Y.R2. It supplies C's
FIRST N arrival, giving

    time(first N input of C)=T_2(Y)+1.

The first E output of C is therefore strictly before its first N input.
Until that first N input, every C arrival is S. Since C begins WHITE,
its first S is R0 and departs W; its second S is L1 and departs E.
Consequently C begins SS, its first E output is exactly C.L1, and

    C.L1->q.R0->B.R0->Y.L1

is an actual three-edge successor channel. No older reads were omitted:
this is about the first of each physical port over the full history.

If q were RIGHT on the supplied b-circle, the selected incoming heading
would be its R0 heading E. Its only possible physical b-predecessor
would be C immediately west. But C.R0 departs W, away from q. This is
impossible independently of C's later multiplicity or q's later reads.
Thus q is LEFT. Quotient identification cannot change a first departure's
physical direction. This is an all-length and reader-independent
incidence obstruction, not a failure of one prepared router.

## 2. GLOBAL maximum gives the odd witness without capping C

Now additionally suppose the displayed portal's two incident C sectors
at Y have GLOBAL maximum height M. They are equal because Y's two R
headings are opposite. The sector southwest of q, and the sector
southeast of C, are that same physical max face (bottom-left coordinate
(0,1)). Hence q.R0 selects an incident GLOBAL maximum C. The independent
maximum qualification of signed61ab7596 applies to this output-one q:
its complete word is E, EE, EEW or EEWE, without imposing a q cap.
Since q is LEFT, the first two words have no selected W incoming edge;
therefore q is EEW or EEWE and is genuinely flow-one.

If Y is EWW it is already a three-read odd witness. Suppose instead
Y=EWWE and q=EEWE. The complete physical counts at C are then

    N_in(C)=2, E_out(C)=3.

Let a=R_N, c=R_S, b=L_N, t=L_S at C, and let epsilon=R-L be zero or
one. Alternation and the two counts give

    a+b=2, a+t=3, c=5-3*a+epsilon.

C begins S, so c>=1. Therefore a cannot be at least two; a<=1 and

    c-a=5-4*a+epsilon>=1.

The cap-free directed stream identity at C says that its first-R
northwest C sector exceeds its opposite southeast sector by c-a.
The latter is M, so the former is at least M+1, contradicting GLOBAL
maximality. This proof retains every possible C read and initial color
is used explicitly only as WHITE.

Thus an even EWWE root forces q=EEW even with UNBOUNDED exterior
multiplicity. The two witness types and full-quotient inverse recovery
from fad3fa8e therefore still give the nonadditive odd-output injection
for these exact GLOBAL-M portals. No all-cap first-R maximum rule or
constant-height theorem for arbitrary b-circles was invoked.

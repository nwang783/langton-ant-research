# Flow-two reset archives retain an exact L3 tail, version 2

24 October 2026. Author analytic composition interface pending review.
This supersedes unpromoted author draft d9f99515; the all-Y section now
explicitly includes a passive single-V gap, not only O and VV gaps.
Complete WHITE cap-four physical histories and the stated signed local
boundary profiles are required. No age upper bound, exterior realization,
initial-fuel payment or physical erasure is asserted. All intervals below
are PHYSICAL times; period translates are summed only afterward.

For any real a,b let I(a,b) be the oriented interval indicator: positive
on[a,b) if a<b, negative on[b,a) if b<a. Thus I(a,b)+I(b,c)=I(a,c), with
no assumption about ordering a,b,c. Put J_p=I(T1(p),T3(p)) at a flat.

## 1. Singleton O with a following Y: a positive old-stock relay

Use the signed global-M critical singleton island: preceding flat Y=P,
odd LEFT root O=r, following flat Y=Q. Write r1=T1(r),r2=T2(r). Exact
physical first/second ordinals give

    T3(Q)=r1+1, T1(P)=r2-1.                            (1)

The two selected internal boundary edges are the actual steps
r.L1->Q.L3 and P.L1->r.R2. Let B_reset be their unit-interval sum and
I_r=I(r1,r2). Then

    J_Q + I_r + J_P
       = I(T1(Q),T3(P)) + B_reset.                    (2)

These THREE positive intervals relay in that order. Adjacent overlaps
are exactly one step each; there is no triple overlap because r2-r1>=4
and each flat age13>=8. In particular an arbitrarily long O return fills
the gap between old Y archives; it is not another independent fresh
payment. Both full Y ages and the original R0/L3 chronology remain.

## 2. Following X: the unresolved late-L3 tail is exact

Now let Q=X instead. The same physical second strand gives

    T1(X)=r1+1, T1(Y)=r2-1.                            (3)

Let B_reset count the actual steps r.L1->X.L1 and Y.L1->r.R2.
Oriented interval telescoping gives

    I_r + J_Y - J_X
       = B_reset + I(T3(X),T3(Y)).                    (4)

The remaining tail explicitly retains BOTH real L3 suppliers. Its sign
is not fixed: a positive tail is a residual archive; a negative tail
is a residual X debt. Their relative order must come from the actual
exterior, not a reset of phases, an assumed age<P, or the desired result.
No source-L3 is deleted by writing this cochain identity.

## 3. The LL-first O/U four-L reset has the SAME tail, not no tail

For the signed double-LEFT root r=O/U, use

    r.L1 -> V.L3 -> X.L1 -> Y.L1 -> r.R2,
    r2=r1+4,
    T1(X)=r1+2, T1(Y)=r1+3.

This is the signed actual four-L cleanup319400b2. Its three selected
boundary edges are r->V, V->X and Y->r. The physical middle X->Y edge
is NOT a b-edge; it must be retained as an exterior chord. Therefore
B_reset is I_r MINUS that one-step chord, not all four steps.
The same exact identity holds:

    I_r + J_Y - J_X
       = B_reset + I(T3(X),T3(Y)).                    (5)

The root's optional later L3 in the U case is not part of I_r and remains
an actual RIGHT-side supplier event. The earlier X.R0 stock obligation
of at least twelve reads remains too. Equations(4)-(5) do not certify a
legal deletion of the four-L excursion: that would change the board.

## 4. General all-Y cluster relay, with O waits included

Combining(2) with the passive RR and LL relations from a8422e3e
and the literal two-edge single-V strand,
ANY admissible all-Y flat cluster has a single positive effective archive
interval, provided its internal flow-two roots are included in the sum.
The internal gaps are exactly

    RR: one-edge overlap;
    R-V-R: two-edge overlap, retaining passive single V;
    R-O-R: two one-edge overlaps with I_O between them;
    R-V-V-R: three-edge overlap.

An LL-first O/U would force the following flat X and is not an all-Y
case. In reverse spatial order all positive Y and O intervals have
increasing starts/ends. Their overlap lengths are1,2 or3; adjacent overlap
lengths sum to at most6 at a Y interval of length>=8, and to2 at an O
interval of length>=4. Hence no triple overlap is hidden. Thus

    sum_allY J_Y + sum_internal_O I_O = I_U + B_internal. (6)

The endpoint archive is U=[T1(last flat),T3(first flat)). Its positive
length may be arbitrarily large. Every selected internal physical edge
is retained as its one-step indicator. Sum ALL P-translates to apply this
to the signed full-boundary cover; all old copies persist.

This does NOT prove a cluster-curvature bound. A formally admissible
all-Y gap word can satisfy this temporal relay and still fail the
complete neighboring-reader equations. The signed exact-three-flat
reader exclusion is such an additional physical condition, not a
consequence of interval algebra alone. Conversely no algebraically
consistent word here is promoted to an attained board factor or front.

The remaining mixed-cluster problem is transport of the explicit L3 tails
in(4)-(5), preserving their sign, physical endpoint roles and full-drift
voltages. An induction that resets a negative tail to a positive archive
would discard the actual unresolved mechanism.

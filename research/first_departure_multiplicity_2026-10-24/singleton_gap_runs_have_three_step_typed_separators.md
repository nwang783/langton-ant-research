# Singleton-gap runs have at most three typed separators

24 October 2026. Author dependent all-length grammar lemma pending
review. Use signed Stage38, including all corrected local gates. Let
P,G,H mean passive packet, genuine packet, and high gap, respectively.
These names do not describe prepared controllers or chronological splices.

## 1. Every singleton run is bracketed by an actual packet

If every flat gap were singleton, there would be no LL/RR packet and
no high gap: Gamma would be the alternating boundary excluded by
7637b9c1. Thus each maximal run of singleton-LEFT gaps is bracketed by
P,G or H in the full cyclic packet word. Short quotients retain their
actual lifted endpoints; no primitive-drift identification is needed.

Each singleton LEFT is O or passive V. Singleton U is excluded by
c9574fb8. A singleton adjacent to an RR cap must be V by the complete
RR endpoint table. If neither flanking RIGHT lies in RR, the actual
five-corner GLOBAL-M windowe597889e applies and its middle singleton
must be O, not V. An O's immediate outside LEFT contacts must both be
V by its signed island rulec9574fb8.

## 2. At most three singleton gaps, all lengths covered

Every INTERIOR singleton gap in a run of length at least three has
singleton gaps on both sides, so neither flanking RIGHT is in RR. It
must therefore be O. A run of length at least FOUR has two adjacent
interior O gaps, violating each O's requirement that its outside LEFT
contacts be V. Hence every maximal singleton run has length at most3.

For a length-three run its exact LEFT types are V,O,V. The first and
last cannot be O because they border the middle O. They are therefore
V; but a singleton V between flat RIGHTs needs a neighboring RR cap,
since otherwise the same five-corner rule forbids it. The first V can
touch such a cap only on the preceding side, and the last only on the
following side. Of P,G,H, only G ENDS in RR and only P STARTS in RR.
Consequently EVERY length-three singleton separator has the typed form

    G, V-singleton, O-singleton, V-singleton, P.

This retains the O output and all its actual return ages. It is a
necessary typed separator, not an assertion that its complete exterior
exists. The special G-to-G case has exactly one V separator by661fa6f7.

## 3. Finite junction language with unrestricted old rails

The all-length geometry is therefore a network of P/G/H packets with
only zero-to-three singleton gaps at each junction, together with the
unrestricted q in every H rail and possible repeated P strips. The
remaining chronological problem is finite JUNCTION TYPES plus their
arbitrary composition and physical exterior ports, not an unbounded
alternating gap string. No arbitrary length cutoff was tested.

This is only a necessary grammar cover: further exact reader, time,
side-current or full-voltage constraints may remove some of its short
junctions. Conversely none of the surviving grammatical transitions is
declared an attained board factor, complete front, reusable controller
or fresh-fuel source. Old ages and many-to-one long ownership remain.

## 1. Actual cyclic turns, not a spatial induction

and LEFT turns. Let nRR and nLL count adjacent equal-turn pairs in its
FULL cyclic word. Then

    nRR - nLL = nR - nL = 0.                          (1)

One way to see this is that the numbers of cyclic RL and LR transitions
are equal, so subtracting those from nR and nL gives (1). RRR/LLL would
close a physical unit square and cannot occur on the essential lift.
Thus RR and LL pairs do not overlap another pair of their own type.
Counts retain lifted incidences in a short quotient; no two physical
contacts are identified by an unproved primitive-drift assumption.

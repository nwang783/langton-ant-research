## 1. Complete reader kernel without side, height, or clock filtering

Let q=(1,2) be the physical b-predecessor of B and C=(0,2) its western
neighbor. Since q.R0 departs S toward B and this edge is used once,
the complete cap-four q words are exactly E, EE, EEW, EEWE. These are
the signed cap-free output-one domains restricted to at most four reads.
For C retain EVERY complete WHITE N/S word of length zero through four.
There are 2*4*31=248 products, with every internal zero/count port and
chronological physical ordinal matched. Exactly 225 fail a port count;
18 have a literal contiguous successor path returning to the wrong next
rank of the SAME physical cell. No positive-clock or stream filter is
needed. The five remaining necessary profiles (Y,q,C) are exactly

    EWW,  EE,   SSN
    EWW,  EEW,  SSN
    EWW,  EEWE, SSNS
    EWWE, EE,   SSNN
    EWWE, EEW,  SSNN.

Every surviving C starts S, so its first R departs W, AWAY from q.
The complete 248-product certificate retains the failed physical paths
and every known successor edge of the five open local profiles.

## 2. q cannot be a RIGHT endpoint, even when the one-use run is empty

If q were geometrically RIGHT on this b-circle, its selected incoming
heading would equal its R0 heading E. The only possible physical
b-predecessor of q would then be its western neighbor C. But every
surviving C first-R departure is W, not E. Thus q has no selected
incoming edge, impossible on a b-circle. A different quotient lift
cannot manufacture a missing physical incoming edge.

This removes not only a last internal RIGHT singleton E, but also the
changed 2->1 RIGHT sinks EE/EEW and the 3->1 RIGHT sink EEWE. No length
premise is used. In particular a zero-internal one-use segment cannot
enter this exact portal directly from such a RIGHT sink.

Therefore q is LEFT. Constant selected C on the actual b-circle makes
LEFT preserve its physical flow, so q is a flow-one LEFT. Its complete
word is EEW or EEWE, and the three necessary profiles are exactly

    (Y,q,C) = (EWW,EEW,SSN), (EWW,EEWE,SSNS), (EWWE,EEW,SSNN).

The maximal incoming one-use segment consequently has a nonempty
internal vertex set and ends in this LEFT q. This strengthens the
previous 3247f7df gate, which only ruled out an internal RIGHT ending.
It does not classify the preceding, arbitrarily long one-use run.

## 3. Distinct portals reserve distinct odd complete-WHITE classes

If Y=EWW, choose Y as the odd output witness. If Y=EWWE, the three-profile
table forces q=EEW, and choose q. Both chosen classes have three reads.
The Y-type has its L1 heading OPPOSITE its R0 heading. The q-type has its
L1 heading the SAME as its R0 heading. These are rotation-invariant,
disjoint complete-history types, so the two witness families cannot alias.

A Y-type witness recovers its portal B from the unique R0 heading E in
this frame: B=Y+E. A q-type witness recovers B by its own first departure:
B=q+S in this frame. Each recovery is physically quarter-rotation and
full-drift equivariant. Hence different full-quotient portal B classes
have different witnesses, including mixed orientations and short quotients.

If n_even is the number of these EXACT two-read-endpoint LL portal
classes on the supplied b-circle, then

    N_odd >= n_even.

This is output capacity, not initial fuel, and it is NOT added to other
reservations that may use the same odd class. The local profiles are
necessary interfaces, not newly attained translating fronts.

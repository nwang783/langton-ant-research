## 1. Four-face zero LRLR already contradicts actual first-R headings

Suppose four consecutive curve faces are LEFT,RIGHT,LEFT,RIGHT, and both
LEFT/RIGHT pairs have selected LEFT-L3 entry bit zero. Normalize the
five selected contacts as

    C=(1,-1), A=(1,0), B=(1,1), D=(1,2), E=(1,3).

The first LEFT/RIGHT pair has A's bit zero, so C's bit is zero by LEFT
transport. The second pair has D's bit zero, so B's bit is zero as well.
Optional RIGHT L3 at every contact remains present where allowed.

For the first pair, the signed two-outward-max launch theorem says that
its upper helper Y=(0,1) has one of

    SSN, SSNS, SSNN, SNNS.

In particular its actual first R arrives S. The first-boundary EEWW
refinement removes SNNS, but is not needed for this contradiction.

For the second pair the signed early-launch theorem requires its lower
q helper to have NSS or NSSN, with actual first R arriving N. That helper
is q'=(0,1), EXACTLY THE SAME PHYSICAL CELL as Y, not merely a same-phase
or translated surrogate. A complete physical history cannot have both
R0 headings S and N. Thus such a zero-state LRLR block is impossible.
This overlap proof does not assume a literal realization of the retained
local launch tables; it uses only their independently signed necessary
whole-history conclusions.

## 2. Constant-zero has no straight faces

Assume the selected LEFT-L3 bit is zero around the whole Gamma.
A strict straight face requires its input bit one, by the signed local
state theorem. An equal-M straight face must now be entered through a
strict state fall, by the signed branchful entrance obstruction, again
requiring an input bit one at the preceding face. Both are impossible.
Therefore every Gamma face bends LEFT or RIGHT.

## 3. No LL plus signed turn zero forces alternation

The new all-K canonical birth-curve theorem excludes consecutive LEFT
turns. On an embedded essential oriented cylinder circle the total
signed turn is zero, as used in the earlier signed K1 bend bookkeeping.
Thus the number of LEFT and RIGHT turns is equal.

In a cyclic all-bend word with no LL, every LEFT is followed by at least
one RIGHT before the next LEFT. Equality of the two counts makes every
such RIGHT run have length exactly one. The whole curve therefore
alternates LEFT/RIGHT, in the actual full quotient and its physical lift.
It contains the forbidden zero LRLR block of section 1, also in small
quotients via lifted occurrences. Contradiction.

# A contractible max-RIGHT middle leaf consumes five old blacks, with a safe count

23 October 2026, 17:07 UTC. New exact restricted identity and occurrence
count, pending independent review. This is NOT a generic gate, packet,
renewal or permanent-root charge.

## 1. Hypotheses

Use a complete WHITE cap-four translating front and its signed repaired
sheet graph G. Let gamma be a CONTRACTIBLE graph-LEAF cycle at sheet
j=M-1. Assume its unique exchange's repaired transition on gamma is a
RIGHT turn around a global max C face. This is the higher-node geometry
of the signed row-5 return if that node is contractible AND a graph leaf.
Neither contractibility nor degree one is inferred for a general renewal.

The signed penultimate orientation and geometric length results give
that gamma is counterclockwise and has edge length ell>=12.

## 2. The canonical leaf is one actual same-cell return

Let v be its unique exchange cell. The signed bridge formula rules out
the outgoing-R2 side for a zero-winding leaf. Hence gamma contains
outgoing L1, the complementary neighbor contains outgoing R2, and

    Delta_v=T2(v)-T1(v)=ell<P.

All actual successors along gamma agree with its canonical successors
except the closing incoming-R2 -> outgoing-L1 repair at v. Thus the
actual trace from v.L1 through its next v.R2 is one contiguous spatial
return: its first ell departures are exactly gamma, and its terminal
R2 read is outside gamma. The terminal R2 is included for color balance.

The two incoming headings at v need not agree: in the row-5 max-RIGHT
case they are opposite. This is NOT a full-pose return or a removable
splice. No future transport is deleted.

## 3. Exact inventory, retaining one-R final L1 reads

Every non-root visit on gamma is unchanged by repair. Since gamma has
only one incident exchange edge, no other visit there can be L1 or R2
of a two-R cell. Since ell<P, no drift-translated copy of the root L1/R2
phase occurs in the interior either. Its possible reads are therefore

    R0; final L1 of a one-R RL cell; final L3 of an RLRL cell.

Let r count those R0 reads, and p count both kinds of final L read.
At v the CANONICAL turn on gamma is RIGHT. The counterclockwise total
canonical turn is four more LEFT than RIGHT, so

    1+r-p=-4, hence p=r+5.                         (1)

The initial actual L1 and terminal actual R2 have opposite color changes
and cancel. Consequently the complete read bracket has EXACT signed
black-count change

    (r+1)-(p+1)=r-p=-5.                            (2)

Equivalently it consumes five more black sites than it creates. Since
ell=1+r+p=2r+6>=12, it has r>=3 fresh R0 sites and p>=8 final-L stocks.
The stock counts refer to distinct physical cells in this bracket.

These final-L sites already are black at the START of the bracket:

- A one-R final L1 has its earlier R0 on a higher sheet, not gamma.
- A final L3 has its earlier R2 event outside this bracket. Any interior
  two-R read would touch another exchange edge; the only allowed root
  R2 phase is the terminal event because ell<P. A drift-translated root
  L3 is NOT discarded, but its own R2 is then before this bracket.

Their required earlier reads cannot occur inside the actual gamma-only
return interval, and precede the final L, so they occur before its start.
Conversely every R0 site is first visited during the bracket and is
white at its start. All final-L stocks are retained as old stock. The
one-R stocks are NOT called mature R2 or rank-3 stock.

Equation (2) is a finite signed field change even for an infinite wake.
It does not identify an original finite-seed root or make the consumed
stock irreplaceable.

## 4. Distinct such leaf brackets have disjoint occurrence sets

For a family of h DISTINCT leaf cycles gamma_i of this type, let A_i be
its canonical edge occurrences plus its terminal outgoing-R2 occurrence.
Canonical node sets are disjoint. The terminal R2 occurrences at their
distinct exchange cells are also distinct.

A terminal R2 cannot lie in another contractible leaf gamma_j. Then the
two leaf nodes would be adjacent, each with degree one. Connectedness
would force G to consist of just those two zero-winding nodes, contrary
to the full total homology one. Thus all A_i are pairwise disjoint.
This is occurrence disjointness, not an assumption that their physical
cells or original seed-root ancestors are disjoint.

Each A_i has ell_i+1 reads and signed turn/color sum -5. A complete WHITE
front has global R-L equal to its number of odd-length complete cell
histories, hence nonnegative. The remaining P-sum(ell_i+1) reads must
therefore have R-L at least 5h, requiring at least 5h remaining reads.
It follows that

    P >= sum ell_i + 6h >= 18h.                    (3)

This reserves capacity only for DISTINCT contractible max-RIGHT middle
LEAVES. Multiple renewal sites can use one nonleaf cycle, and a cycle
with multiple exchanges need not satisfy the actual-bracket identity.
No bound on the number of renewals follows.

## 5. Scope and countercontrols retained

The prepared-star four-L old-stock leaves have canonical root LEFT, not
the max-RIGHT hypothesis here; their net bracket change is -3, not -5.
The prepared black-fuel leaves fail the WHITE premise. Both controls
remain valid. General deep packets can have positive black change and
are not classified as these graph leaves.

Do not add (3) to overlapping top-face, A/B corridor or chronological
bounds without a separate combined register. The complement in its
proof may already contain those budgets. This is no K>=2, finite-seed
capture or permanent-fuel theorem.

## 2. These classes cannot touch an L face at height <=M-3

Here is the exact unshifted calculation, including equality cases.
At a horizontal-input corner with diagonal C sectors NE/SW, let r_E,r_W
be its R input counts and l_E,l_W its L counts. For an additive a,
complete incident face equations give

    H_NW=1-l_E+a, H_NE=r_W+a,
    H_SE=1-l_W+a, H_SW=r_E+a,

where H(L)=h(L)+1 and H(C)=h(C). Rotations give the other axis.

At a three-read flat corner with opposite R headings and both C faces
at M, r_E=r_W=1, l_E+l_W=1 and a=M-1. The two UNSHIFTED incident L
heights are therefore exactly M-1 and M-2. This covers both the positive
x and the negative d reservations; their different chronological order
does not affect these local levels.

At a singleton corner selecting C height M, the other C height is M-1.
Take r_E=1,r_W=0,a=M-1 and l_E=l_W=0 after normalization. Both incident
UNSHIFTED L heights are M-1. Thus the reserved singleton also cannot
touch any L face at height <=M-3.

Consequently none of the n_A reserved outputs can be an odd-output
contact of a lower minimum plateau at level <=M-3. This is why the
unused same-R t family was not silently subtracted: same-R three-read
contacts can have an L height M-3 and would require a separate argument.

## 3. Available lower-root capacity is at most Nodd-n_A

The signed band theorem gives r-1 spatial bands between the ordered
maximum curves. Each contains an original L minimum of height <=M-3.
It either touches odd output strictly inside that band or supplies the
qualified reverse cut there. Distinct rooted bands use distinct physical
odd-output classes. By section2 those roots all avoid the n_A already
reserved top classes. Therefore at most Nodd-n_A bands can be rooted,
and at least

    max(0, r-1-(Nodd-n_A))

bands have the disjoint reverse counterflow pairs. The same physical
partition as6376fdc5 has baseline6D and at least12D per unrooted band,
which gives the displayed bound.

This is a height-stratified output/current coupling, not a fuel charge.
Different early packages can share lower minima; the proof counts only
distinct spatial bands, never one lower cut per branch. It does not
exclude cross-Gamma transfer, general K1 fronts or arbitrary finite
seeds. No odd class is counted twice in the root capacity.

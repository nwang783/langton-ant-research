# Two disjoint C/L birth cuts give a genuine three-current budget

23 October 2026. Exact conditional flow lemma. It does not add overlapping chronological clocks. The construction uses an original C-face birth curve and a time-reversed L-face birth curve, with disjoint physical cuts and their relative spatial order explicitly retained.

## 1. Hypotheses and sign convention

Let the original complete translating checker flow have actual full P,d, nonzero d, stream f(e)=h(C_right)-h(L_left)>=0, and exterior constants c_R=c_L+1. Suppose:

- Gamma_C is an embedded proper essential C-contact curve through faces of constant height lambda_C, oriented with full winding+1 in original drift orientation;
- Gamma_L is an embedded proper essential L-contact curve through faces of constant height m, oriented with full winding+1 in REVERSE drift orientation;
- the curves are geometrically disjoint, and Gamma_L lies strictly LEFT of Gamma_C when the latter is oriented in original drift direction;
- their selected cursor constructions give K_C=lambda_C-c_R>=1 and K_L=c_L-m>=1.

Both curves and all side currents use the actual full quotient. No primitive time period is substituted. The L curve may be supplied by the prepared reverse plateau lemma only when its required WHITE selected contacts or odd-output-free collar have actually been established.

## 2. Exact spatial stream partition

The curves cut the cylinder into far-LEFT, middle, and far-RIGHT bands. Define three face potentials, taking the continuous agreeing values on cut faces:

Far-LEFT potential:

    h-m on the far-LEFT side of Gamma_L; zero elsewhere.

Far-RIGHT potential:

    h-lambda_C on the far-RIGHT side of Gamma_C; zero elsewhere.

Middle potential:

    m on the far-LEFT band;
    h in the middle band;
    lambda_C on the far-RIGHT band.

Their sum is h everywhere. On an L cut face h=m, and on a C cut face h=lambda_C, so the definitions match at the cuts.

Each potential induces a balanced integer checker current by C-minus-L across each directed physical edge. These currents are NONNEGATIVE and partition f, not just signed formal streams. Away from the cut faces, both incident faces of a physical edge lie in the same band, giving either its full original flow or zero. At a cut L face, its height is exactly m; at a cut C face, its height is exactly lambda_C. The neighboring band's difference is again the original physical edge flow, with the other currents zero. Thus every physical edge's full multiplicity belongs to exactly one band current. If adjacent faces lie on the two different cuts, their intervening edge belongs to the middle current with flow lambda_C-m. No diagonal contact edge is being subtracted as a physical current.

Balance follows from the stream corner telescoping, including every selected cut vertex. The currents need not individually be one cursor orbit. Their edge-occurrence families are disjoint by the explicit physical partition.

## 3. Homologies and the additive length bound

With the retained oriented exterior-jump convention, their original-drift homologies are

    far LEFT: 0-(c_L-m) = -K_L,
    middle: lambda_C-m = K_C+K_L+1,
    far RIGHT: c_R-lambda_C = -K_C.

They sum to1, the full current's homology. A balanced checker current has equal numbers of horizontal and vertical edge occurrences. If its homology is k, its length is at least2 ||d||_infinity |k|. Applying that lower bound to the THREE DISJOINT currents gives

    P >= 2 ||d||_infinity [2(K_C+K_L)+1].

This is a genuinely additive spatial register. It may be stronger than the maximum of the separate C-curve and reverse-L-curve bounds. It is NOT added to A/B corridors, supplier-return clocks or any other overlapping time budget.

## 4. Scoped K1 application

If Gamma_C is the original K1 maximum curve, K_C=1. If a qualified odd-output-free lower L minimum supplies a LEFT reverse curve with K_L>=3, then

    P >= 18 ||d||_infinity.

The depth-three strict late-D4 case is a candidate source of that L minimum, but the original minimum component's odd-output collar condition AND its LEFT location must separately be proved. The mere local face height m<=c_L-3 does not by itself provide the reverse curve or its location. This note deliberately keeps those qualifications rather than claiming an unconditional D4 or K1 exclusion.

The result is not original K>=2, a new finite-seed fuel theorem, or absence of lower essential cycles. It describes exactly how such a retained lower essential cycle can coexist with the upper curve only by paying the middle band's physical flow as well as both exterior counterflows.

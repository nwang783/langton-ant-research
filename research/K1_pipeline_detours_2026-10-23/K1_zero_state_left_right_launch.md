# A zero-state left/right bend pair needs an older outward max launch

23 October 2026, 15:05 UTC. New local analytic necessity submitted for
independent review. Equal-M escape histories are RETAINED. No whole
zero-state mode or general K=1 exclusion is claimed.

## 1. Exact oriented three-face configuration

Use the signed complete white cap-four K=1 first-L curve, with its
LEFT-L3 bit s. Suppose F_(i-1) is a LEFT turn immediately followed by
RIGHT turn F_i. Normalize selected contacts

    C=v_(i-2)=(1,-1), A=v_(i-1)=(1,0), B=v_i=(1,1).

Their selected RLR incoming prefixes are respectively E,E,W; N,N,S;
E,E,W. The parent C faces have lower-left coordinates

    F_(i-2):(0,-2), F_(i-1):(1,-1), F_i:(0,0),

all height M. The first right-turn LEFT helper is Z=(0,0), the second
Y=(0,1). Let G be the SW C face at Z, lower-left(-1,-1), at height<=M.
Put q=(0,-1), south of Z and west of C. Its SE C face is F_(i-2)=M and
its NW C face is G.

Assume s_A=0. The preceding left turn preserves the bit, so s_C=0.
Thus q has exactly TWO E outputs into C, at its R0 and L1, even if C has
an optional RIGHT L3. In particular

    time(second E output of q)=T_1(C)-1.           (1)

A likewise has exactly two W outputs, L1 and R2, into Z. Signed K=1
chronology gives T_1(C)<T_1(A), with arbitrarily many actual detours
retained between them.

## 2. A strict-lower G is impossible

If G<M, all Rs at Z arrive W and select F_i. Exactly two W inputs from
A permit either

    Z: RL with headings W,W,

or the two-R alternatives W,E,W and W,E,W,E. The latter emit respectively
three or four N outputs into Y. If Y's other C face is strict, its first
N input and E output are its R0, giving the consecutive first-port chain

    R0(Z)->R0(Y)->R0(B)->R2(A)->R2(Z).

It would place R2(Z) only4 steps after R0(Z), impossible with two local
returns. If Y's other C face equals M, opposite R headings with three N
inputs allow at most one E output, while B needs at least two. Four N
inputs are incompatible with opposite R headings. Thus the two-R Z
alternatives are impossible even with an equal-M branch at Y.

Consequently Z must be RL(W,W), h(G)=M-1, and its ONE S output is L1,
supplied by R2(A) one step earlier. The first S input at q is therefore
T_2(A)+2. At q the C-height difference is1, so it has just one R, arriving
N. To emit E twice it must subsequently read L incoming S. Its second
E output cannot precede that first S input. This contradicts(1), since
T_1(C)<T_1(A)<T_2(A). Therefore

    h(G)=M.                                       (2)

## 3. The equal-M escape is specific, not an exclusion

At Z, equal C heights force opposite R headings, and its exactly two W
inputs imply exactly TWO S outputs. For clarity, all possible headings
with this W-input count are

    W,W,E; E,W,W;
    W,W,E,E; W,E,E,W; E,W,W,E; E,E,W,W.

Every such history has a final S output at a W arrival from A (or later
than one), hence its last S output is no earlier than T_1(A)+1. Thus q's
SECOND S input is no earlier than T_1(A)+2, strictly after its second E
output in(1).

Now q lies between equal M faces and has two S inputs and two E outputs.
Complete white cap-four flat histories permit a second E output before
the second S input ONLY in the histories

    q: RLR or RLRL, incoming N,S,S[,N].             (3)

(The other possibilities SSN[,N],SNNS,NNSS place E2 at or after S2.)
In(3), the second E output is exactly the first S read, L1. It is supplied
by the FIRST S output from Z. To occur before T_1(A), that output cannot
be after Z's first W input from A. Thus it must be Z's R0 arriving E,
turning S around the outward max face G. Exact matching gives

    T_0(Z)+1=T_1(q)=T_1(C)-1,
    T_0(Z)=T_1(C)-2.                               (4)

This is the retained early launch from an equal-M branch. A blanket
claim that every zero-state left/right pair is impossible would be FALSE
at the necessary local-history level: the N,S,S escape in(3) is real
and must not be dropped. No literal exterior connector is asserted.

## 4. Canonical max-face birth must move further outward

At Z, R0 already selects G, so Z cannot be G's negative first-port birth
corner. At q, (3) has R0 around F_(i-2), then L1 incoming S, which uses
G's input BEFORE its first output R2. Thus q is a positive, not negative,
flat contact for G and cannot be its birth corner either.

The signed max-face theorem requires G's canonical birth at a negative
flat contact with another max C face. Neither RIGHT corner Z nor q can
supply it. Hence its birth predecessor must be a C face across one of
G's two LEFT corners, with lower-left coordinate

    (-2,-2) or (-2,0),

and height M. Its birth is strictly earlier than B(G), by at least5.
This forces a further outward max supplier, not a disposable finite fuel
bud. Its subsequent path could bend or return to older Gamma faces;
no infinite regress or new essential cycle is inferred here.

The useful necessary geometry is therefore: a zero-state adjacent
LEFT/RIGHT pair needs an early max launch exactly two steps before the
older selected L1, AND the launched max face needs another max supplier
through one of its LEFT corners. This is a chronological supplier
obligation, not a paid gate count, K>=2 theorem or global exclusion.

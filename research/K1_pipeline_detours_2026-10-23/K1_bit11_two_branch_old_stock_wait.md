## 1. First branch: new at +5, or a genuine older LEFT birth

    r=(-1,-1), e=(-1,0), u=(-1,1), t=(-1,2).

The earlier fixed prefix reaches r with incoming W at alpha+7; the
first J-output at Z is Z.L1 at alpha+5. Its four corners are r,q,Z,e.

feeds Z's only E input, R2. Its S inputs come from u. Every u in the
signed seven-profile inventory has at most ONE S output. A negative

# A short L3 return must use at least the third physical ear entry

23 October 2026, 18:08 UTC. Conditional occurrence-level corollary of the
bounded LEFT ear entry interface, submitted for review. No general
no-fold theorem or realization of the compatible boundary inventory is
asserted.

Let the exact hypotheses of K1_bounded_left_ear_entry_interface.md hold,
with at least two selected contacts v_j,...,v_k on the bounded LEFT ear.
Suppose ALL crosscut entries use a single directed physical edge, whose
complete occurrence times are

    tau_1 < tau_2 < ... < tau_f.

Suppose an actual supplier path from its r-th entry reaches the first
boundary contact's L3 at time

    T3(v_j)=tau_r+c, c<4.

Same-cell spacing and K=1 give

    T0(v_(j+1)) < T2(v_j) <= tau_r+c-4 < tau_r.

The two-entry demand requires at least TWO crosscut entries before
T0(v_(j+1)). At most r-1 entries can precede tau_r, so necessarily

    r>=3 and f>=3.

More strongly SOME entry must occur before R0_j and SOME later entry
must occur between R0_j and R0_(j+1). They need not be tau_1/tau_2 for
general r, because extra early entry/exit pairs may occur. For r=3 they
are precisely tau_1 and tau_2, since only those two entries can precede
tau_r. Merely counting three entries is not a sufficiency theorem. This is an ordinal gate retaining the
actual L supplier and all preceding R reads, not a paid source budget.

In the exact same-Gamma row-5 return, the entry edge is O->E, with
occurrences O.R0, O.R2, O.L3. The actual supplier reaches v_j.L3 from
O.R2 in two reads. Thus r=2,c=2 and the ordinal gate fails.

By contrast a strict straight main face can have its helper Z's third
upward output Z.L3 feed a selected contact B.L3 one read later. On a
suitable LEFT ear this would use r=3,c=1, so this NECESSARY ordinal gate
alone would not exclude it. Its preceding first/second upward outputs
and all other boundary histories must still meet the exact deadlines;
no complete factor, front, clean connector, or genuine K1 realization
is supplied by that boundary observation.

This explicitly separates the excluded second-occurrence old-L3 return
from the still-open third-occurrence straight-gate suppliers. General
K>=2 crossing permutations need not have the K1 two-entry deadline.

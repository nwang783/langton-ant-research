# Extra outward leaves reduce the unpaid duplicator capacity

23 October 2026. Complete WHITE cap-four front, actual full quotient. This combines the new outward-Q1 terminal leaf theorem with the signed finite canonical flag identity and existing output reservations. It is output capacity only, not initial fuel, a distinct original-root count or a realized-front claim.

## 1. Global SSNS outward sources reserve output capacity

Let D_4 count all actual positive canonical duplicators whose associated opposite face I has the all-four profile. Let n_T count any family of distinct actual late canonical routes D -> J -> P0 -> I covered by the new outward theorem, with g = SSNS and f = EEEW. The local route need not lie on a selected cycle for this paragraph.

Each such route supplies a distinct flag-one leaf Q1. The signed injection D -> Q1 is physical and equivariant. Its whole history has no positive flat contact, whereas every D-associated I in the seven-profile theorem has the mandatory positive flat Y = NSSS corner. Therefore these n_T leaves are disjoint from ALL D-associated I faces, not just those on one Gamma.

Every one of the D_4 sources has its own distinct flag-one I leaf. Hence the number of flag-one leaves is at least D_4 + n_T. The global flag identity gives

    N_O + N_D >= D_4 + n_T.

Partition actual positive duplicators as in the signed output theorem:

    N_D = D_C + D_H + D_S + D_4.

That theorem reserves distinct odd output with

    N_odd >= N_O + D_C + max(D_H, n_SB) + D_S + n_Sanc,

when the two optional K = 1 families n_SB and n_Sanc are included; set them to zero without those families. It follows that

    N_odd >= n_T + n_Sanc.

This is an aggregate output-capacity inequality. It is not a literal map from each T source to a particular odd output, and it is not additive with other overlapping output counts. In particular the simpler global inequality N_odd >= n_T needs no K = 1 cycle assumption.

If the signed n_A distinct early terminal I leaves are also retained, their union with the D_4 opposite leaves has size at least max(D_4, n_A). All T leaves are disjoint from both families. Thus

    N_odd >= n_T + max(0, n_A - D_4) + n_Sanc.

The safe combination with the existing rise/strict-fall reservation is by maximum:

    N_odd >= n_Sanc + max(n_up + n_SB,
                         n_T + max(0, n_A - D_4)).

No assertion that the two displayed right-hand reservations are disjoint is made.

## 2. A selected-cycle improvement covering BOTH outward birth modes

Fix Gamma. Let n_L count selected-Gamma actual duplicators having the exact late all-four route D -> J -> P0 -> I, including both the strict-pit and outward-M alternatives. Let n_Lout count its outward-M subset. These sources are D_4 sources, so n_L <= D_4. Let n_A retain the signed early selected-Gamma sources, a disjoint source family.

The association D -> I is injective. Therefore the n_A + n_L source faces have distinct flag-one I leaves. Every outward source supplies another distinct Q1 leaf. In the SSNS rows it is disjoint from every I. In the NSSS rows a coincidence Q1(D) = I(D') forces D' = P0(D), strictly off Gamma. It therefore cannot equal an I attached to any selected-Gamma source. Consequently

    number of flag-one leaves >= n_A + n_L + n_Lout,
    N_O + N_D >= n_A + n_L + n_Lout.

The output theorem gives N_odd + D_4 >= N_O + N_D + n_Sanc. Combining yields

    N_odd + (D_4 - n_L) >= n_A + n_Lout + n_Sanc.

Thus these exact late Gamma duplicators are not free capacity for the earlier terminal leaves: their own distinct I leaves already consume their capacity. An outward route also requires the extra Q1 leaf. The remaining D_4 - n_L duplicators, including off-Gamma sources, are explicitly retained and may supply that capacity.

This is not a recursion theorem. In particular a NSSS/EWEE coincidence at P0 does not establish that P0 is an actual positive duplicator, that its own route repeats, or that it consumes a new root. The strict-pit alternative retains its separate odd-output-contact OR three-current bound. No original seed budget, general K = 1 exclusion or D_4 exclusion follows.

faces bend and every LEFT-L3 rise needs a branched right turn, but
neither is a paid resource or a global exclusion. No K>=2 theorem,
front classification, all-finite-seed capture, or new universal speed
constant follows. The useful next target is to couple the six bend
families and retained branch geometry with real helper-source chronology,
not to extend the finite scan bounds.

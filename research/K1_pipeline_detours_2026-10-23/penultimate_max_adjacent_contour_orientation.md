# A max-adjacent penultimate contour is essential or a low-hole boundary

23 October 2026, 16:54 UTC. Analytic geometric extension of the signed
sheet repair, pending independent review. A low-hole boundary is not a
fresh-stock or finite-root resource.

## 1. Statement and precise smoothing

Use a complete WHITE cap-four translating front with global maximum C
height M. Consider the repaired canonical sheet j=M-1. If one of its
cycles gamma has a physical edge with the right C face at height M,
then gamma is either essential or, if contractible, COUNTERCLOCKWISE.
Equivalently a contractible such cycle bounds a LOW hole on its LEFT,
not a bounded high island on its RIGHT.

In K=1 this is the sole carrying sheet j=c_right. The result also makes
sense without K=1. It does not apply to the top-sheet M face cycles,
which are clockwise and may separate C faces touching at a corner.

Use the signed repaired pairing to draw each canonical sheet cycle in
thin physical edge corridors and disjoint rounded vertex arcs. The
sheet has indicator stream 1{h>=j}; put the high region on each oriented
arc's RIGHT. Faces with h>=j belong to that high region. At a four-port
vertex the repaired two-R or two-L pairing fixes the small central
patch, so the high-side regions are unambiguous after smoothing.

## 2. All max/max contacts connect their high C sectors at j=M-1

Both incident C faces have height M. The signed positive-port theorem
makes the corner active. Its complete cap-four history is flat, with
one R around each max face, and is RLR or RLRL.

There are exactly these threshold geometries, up to rotation:

- RLR: the two L heights are M-1 and M-2. The L face at M-1 is filled
  high and directly joins the two max C sectors.
- RLRL with the two L headings equal: the L heights are M-1 and M-3.
  Again the high L face joins the max sectors.
- RLRL with opposite L headings: both L heights are M-2. All four
  threshold ports are active, and the repaired j=M-1 pairings are the
  two L turns. Their high-side central patch joins the two opposite
  max C sectors, while separating the two low L sectors.

These are the same complete ordinal cases used by the signed sheet
exchange. In EVERY case the max C sectors are joined on the high side.
The last case is exactly where one must not use the top-sheet two-R
smoothing by mistake.

Thus every corner-connected global-max C component lies in ONE connected
high-side region of the penultimate sheet. Its signed first-L birth
curve can be routed through those high patches. It remains a positive
essential curve in the full-drift cylinder; no primitive reduction is
made.

## 3. A clockwise contractible max-adjacent cycle is impossible

Suppose gamma were clockwise and contractible. Its high RIGHT side is
inside its bounded contractible disk. Its adjacent max C face F lies in
that high-side component. Section 2 puts all of F's max component, and
an essential birth curve in that component, in the same high-side region.
This region cannot cross gamma, so that essential curve would lie inside
a contractible disk. This is impossible.

Hence a contractible gamma with a right max neighbor must instead be
counterclockwise. Its right high region is outside; its bounded left
region is a low hole. No statement is made that all such holes are
four-edge loops or have only one exchange.

## 4. Exact consequence for the row-5 stock alternative

In the signed row-5 near return, the E=S,N,S,S cell's outgoing L1 edge
has label M-1 and right C face equal to the return max H. Therefore its
higher endpoint node A in the exchange graph is either essential or,
if contractible, a counterclockwise lower-stock-hole boundary.

The earlier renewal trichotomy allows an extra middle-sheet cycle.
This result classifies the max-adjacent higher node; it does NOT say
that an arbitrary other middle node on the bridge's lower side has the
same orientation, nor that a counterclockwise hole has a fresh root.
It may be an old-stock escape and must remain in a causal argument.

No new K>=2 theorem, finite fuel budget, additive handle charge, or
complete white connector is inferred.

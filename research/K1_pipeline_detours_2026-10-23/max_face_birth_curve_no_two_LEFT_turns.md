# A canonical maximum-face birth curve has no two consecutive LEFT turns

23 October 2026, 19:52 UTC. New first-port theorem for complete WHITE
cap-four fronts. It does NOT require K=1, leftmostness, absent branches,
or any original-fuel bound.

## 1. Exact lifted four-face geometry

Use the signed canonical first-L maximum-face cycle Gamma, oriented in
the actual parent-to-child direction, with actual full P,d. Across every
selected contact v the incoming prefix is RLR with R0/L1 sharing heading;
B(child)=T1(v), while the parent birth precedes it by at least five.

Suppose two consecutive curve faces turn LEFT. Rotate the entire physical
lift so the three incoming/outgoing edge directions are NW,SW,SE. Their
four consecutive maximum C faces have lower-left corners

    F0=(0,0), F1=(-1,1), F2=(-2,0), F3=(-1,-1).

The three selected contacts are

    a=(0,1): NNS,
    b=(-1,1): WWE,
    c=(-1,0): SSN.

Every prefix retains optional LEFT or RIGHT L3. The unused fourth
contact is w=(0,0), joining maximum C faces F0(NE) and F3(SW).
The signed positive-max-contact theorem therefore makes w active and
flat: both R reads are present, with opposite incoming E/W headings.
All its L headings remain E or W.

## 2. Two required outputs precede the first required input

The complete physical N edge w->a supplies a.R0 and a.L1 first, and
possibly a.LEFT-L3 later. Thus

    time(w's SECOND N output)=T1(a)-1.              (1)

The complete physical E edge c->w is supplied first by c.L1, then c.R2,
and possibly c.LEFT-L3. Its first E input at w is therefore

    time(w's FIRST E input)=T1(c)+1.                (2)

Canonical birth chronology in the actual physical lift gives

    T1(a)=B(F1)<B(F2)<B(F3)=T1(c).

Consequently (1) is strictly BEFORE (2). No time is reduced modulo P,
so this remains true in folded curves and short actual full quotients.

## 3. The complete flat-cell inventory forbids that order

At a complete WHITE cap-four flat E/W cell, at most ONE N output can
occur before its first E input:

- If R0 arrives E, the first E input has already happened.
- If R0 arrives W, it emits the first N output. An L1(W) emits S;
  R2 must then arrive E before any second N output is possible.
- If that L1 instead arrives E and emits N, its input is already the
  FIRST E input. Optional L3 cannot restore a second earlier output.

This exhausts the complete flat histories and keeps equality at the
first-E visit. It contradicts the strict inequality from (1)-(2).
Therefore TWO CONSECUTIVE LEFT TURNS ARE IMPOSSIBLE on Gamma.

This is an all-period geometric constraint on the canonical birth
curve, not a classification of arbitrary maximum-face paths, all-cap
fronts, finite seeds, or a general K1 exclusion. Extra maximum branches
and arbitrary older L suppliers were never discarded.

# The row-5 stock renewal cannot keep the one-root sheet star

23 October 2026, 16:48 UTC. Exact necessary topology from the signed
row-5 return and signed bridge formula. Pending independent review.
No handle, cycle or extra component is charged as a fresh resource.

## 1. The particular downward exchange

In the conditional same-Gamma row-5 return, the second supplied cell E
has full incoming history S,N,S,S. Its higher C face is the return max
face at M, and the other C face is b=M-2. Exact chronological ordinals
give

    outgoing L1 level M-1=c_right;
    outgoing R2 level M-2=c_left;
    final L3 level M-2.

Thus its cycle-graph edge e points from the outgoing-L1 MIDDLE node to
the outgoing-R2 LOWER node. This is the equality old-stock source, not
an omitted singleton-black-L input. Let A be the higher endpoint node.

## 2. Exhaustive graph alternative

Use the signed connected adjacent-sheet graph G, with even cycle excess
N2-C+1. There are exactly three possibilities, with overlapping stronger
geometry allowed:

1. e is NOT a bridge. Then G has positive cycle excess, hence by parity
   its excess is at least TWO.

2. e is a bridge, and its outgoing-R2 component contains a node at sheet
   M-1. That node is necessarily DIFFERENT from A, which is in the
   opposite component. So there is an additional middle-sheet canonical
   cycle. It may be contractible: no nonzero winding is asserted here.

3. e is a bridge, and its outgoing-R2 component contains NO node at
   sheet M-1. Since its starting node has level M-2 and every graph edge
   joins adjacent levels, every node in this component is at level at
   most M-2. The signed bridge formula makes this component's total
   winding 1+m>=1. Hence at least one of its canonical sheet cycles has
   positive winding, necessarily +1 by embeddedness. That cycle is on a
   sheet j<=M-2, whose TOTAL winding in the entire graph is zero. There
   must therefore also be a negative essential cycle on the SAME sheet.
   The compensating cycle is distinct, but is not asserted outside the
   bridge component.

No other case is possible. If a lower-side path ever reached a level
above M-2, its first such node would have level M-1 and fall under case 2.

## 3. What this rules out, and what it does not

The existing prepared W20 star has one middle node, every other node
contractible, and zero cycle excess. It satisfies NONE of the three
alternatives. Therefore that simple graph type cannot be maintained if
its missing fuel is replaced by the conditional row-5 old-L3 return.
The completion must acquire at least one of the precise additional
canonical geometries listed above.

This is a necessary change in the actual repaired-sheet interface,
not a new global maximum, K>=2 theorem, or white-front construction.
Several renewal sites can share the SAME extra middle node or the SAME
graph cycle. No additive payment follows. An additional middle node
need not be an additional max component. Zero-total lower sheets may
contain both essential orientations, as explicitly retained in case 3.

The local row-5 supplier remains possible at the necessary-history
level. Its global connectors and preceding raw histories have still
not been constructed. This criterion does not discard the known
four-L cleanup or either physical side current.

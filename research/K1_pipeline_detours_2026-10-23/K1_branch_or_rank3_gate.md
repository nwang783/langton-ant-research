## Hypotheses and branch-free reduction

Retain the complete white cap-four K=1 first-L curve Gamma, actual P,d,
contacts v_i and LEFT-L3 bits s_i. Suppose there is NO equal-M other-C
neighbor at ANY LEFT intermediate corner of Gamma: at each straight face
its single LEFT other-C neighbor is strict, and at each right turn BOTH
LEFT other-C neighbors are strict. Left turns have no intermediate LEFT
corner. RIGHT-side max neighbors and arbitrary side detours remain.

The signed state-gate lemma makes every rise0->1 impossible under these
hypotheses, so the cyclic bit is constant. If it were0, strict straight
faces would be impossible. Every parent face would bend. All bends
cannot have the same sign: four consecutive identical90degree turns in
the C-contact lattice close a contractible square in the PLANE, whereas
Gamma is an embedded essential proper periodic line. Hence its cyclic
bend sequence contains adjacent LEFT then RIGHT turns. The strict-G
part of K1_zero_state_left_right_launch.md excludes that pair in state0.
Therefore the branch-free mode has

    s_i=1 for EVERY selected contact i.             (1)

All selected contacts have their L3 on LEFT. This does not erase RIGHT
L3 from other, unselected helpers or from the general branched modes.

## Actual L3 corridors versus true gates

Under(1), the signed local tables give only the following possibilities.
Write A=v_(i-1), B=v_i for a parent face, with corners genuinely rotated
into the earlier normalizations.

- LEFT turn: A3 -> B3 is one ACTUAL physical step.
- RIGHT turn, signed strict-left row2 or5: A3 -> Z2 -> Y2 -> B3 is an
  uninterrupted ACTUAL three-step physical path. Both helper corners
  have strict other-C neighbors and are not selected Gamma contacts.
- STRAIGHT: A3 -> z2 -> B1. This is a genuine rank3-to-rank1 gate,
  not an L3-to-L3 corridor. B3 is later supplied separately by z3.
- RIGHT turn, signed strict-left row4 (both helpers locked):
  A3 -> Z2 -> Y1 is a genuine gate. Later Z3 -> Y2 -> B1, and a separate
  Y3 -> B3 supplies the selected L3. All those L suppliers are retained.

The row numbers refer to strict_left_K1_right_turn_table.md's six-row
numbering. No row is asserted to have an exterior connector merely from
its necessary table.

## A closed uninterrupted L3 corridor cannot be the whole front

Suppose every parent face were one of the first two corridor types.
Concatenate the actual consecutive paths around one full Gamma lift.
They carry the actual ant from L3(v_0) to L3(v_ell), whose time is
T_3(v_0)+P and position v_0+d. Thus they cover EVERY actual time in a
full period, not a subtracted nonconsecutive flow.

Every read in that full interval would be either a selected L3 or a
strict-other-C helper R2. None is a selected R0, L1 or R2 phase. Helper
vertices cannot coincide with a selected contact even after a full-drift
translation, because a selected contact has BOTH C heights M while each
of these helpers has a strict lower C neighbor. Yet every selected
contact must also have its complete R0,L1,R2 events in the same full
period quotient. This is impossible.

Consequently at least one true gate of the third or fourth type occurs.
Combining with the initial branch alternative:

    EVERY K=1 curve has either an equal-M LEFT branch, OR every selected
    L3 is on LEFT and at least one straight or locked-right-turn
    rank3-to-rank1 gate is present.

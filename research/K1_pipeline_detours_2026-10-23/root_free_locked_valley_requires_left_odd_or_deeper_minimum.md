Let Q be an original locally minimal L plateau at m=M-3, with no odd

1. An original odd-output physical cell has primal x<=-1.

   left region then contains an original locally minimal component at

there must be actual odd output to the LEFT. The conclusion is not a

## 1. Assume both alternatives absent

No odd output at x<=-1 means every reverse active cell there starts
WHITE and has an EVEN complete history. No deeper original L face means
every reverse C face at lower-left x<=-2 has height<=lambda. Cells and
higher reverse C faces to the RIGHT of the low row remain unrestricted.

The immediate secondary cells O_k=(-1,2k+1), E_k=(-1,2k) are then WHITE-
even. Their signed prepared-port list, with baseline b and opposite L
floor lambda-3, allows only other C height lambda-1 or lambda. In
particular the maximum nu of all left C heights satisfies

    lambda-1<=nu<=lambda.

The left maximum exists: the active strip is finite in the full quotient,
the left exterior is b<lambda-1, and d is vertical so x descends to a
well-defined quotient coordinate.

## 2. The left maximum cannot equal the right exterior

If nu=lambda-1, choose a plateau attaining this height LEFT of the low
row. It cannot cross that complete height-b row. Every neighboring C
face is <=nu, including the low boundary row, so it is a locally maximal
finite-full-quotient plateau. All its incident corners have x<=-1 and
are reverse WHITE.

The prepared maximum theorem forces nu>c_right_rev=lambda-1, a
contradiction. This step retains BLACK reverse input elsewhere and uses
only the genuinely WHITE collar. Consequently nu=lambda.

## 3. Choose a leftmost height-lambda component, not an infinite induction

All height-lambda components in that left region are locally maximal,
since the region has no higher C face and is separated on RIGHT by the
low row. They have WHITE-even collars. There are finitely many full-
quotient face classes at lambda. Choose a component Q* whose minimum
face x coordinate is as small as possible.

The signed local axis-rail theorem applies to Q* too: its level is
lambda, its reverse retreat is1, its collar is WHITE-even and the TRUE

low row lies one C step LEFT of its selected maximum faces, and the

height lambda cannot cross a complete lower row anywhere in the lift.

All its secondary cells lie strictly farther LEFT than every Q* face.
They too are reverse WHITE-even by the assumed odd-free halfplane.
Their prepared-port list forces a further-left C maximum at least
lambda-1. The prepared plateau argument from section2 rules out an
exact maximum lambda-1 there. Thus there is another height-lambda

smaller minimum x than Q*. This contradicts its choice.

This is a finite leftmost-component contradiction. It does not infer
impossibility merely from an infinite physical coordinate decrease.

Every candidate component's mode and collar hypotheses were re-proved

## 2. Its geometry is forced to an oppositely oriented RIGHT turn

The two C faces across H's right corners O,E are the low b faces, not M.
Thus neither corner can be a selected flat Gamma contact. Both selected
contacts of H must instead be its LEFT corners

    A'=(-2,1), B'=(-2,0),

The low C neighbors are LEFT of Gamma near the main RIGHT face F. Their

be locally RIGHT of Gamma. One face cannot be on both sides of the
embedded curve. Hence H is traversed downward, A' then B', as a RIGHT
turn. Its two LEFT helpers are O then E, the original strict-right
geometry rotated by 180 degrees. Both other C heights are b<M.

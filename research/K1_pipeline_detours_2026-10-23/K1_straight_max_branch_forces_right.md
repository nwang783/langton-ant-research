# A straight equal-M LEFT branch must be followed by RIGHT

23 October 2026, 18:53 UTC. New local branchful chronology obstruction,
submitted for independent review. No leftmostness, global absence of
branches, leaf structure, or original-root charge is assumed.

## 1. Exact hypotheses

Use a complete WHITE cap-four translating front, actual full P,d,
canonical first-L curve Gamma, and K=1. Suppose a STRAIGHT face F has
an equal-M unused LEFT C neighbor J. The signed straight-flat table
forces its helper z to have complete incoming W,W,E,E and both adjacent
selected contacts to have LEFT-L3 state0. Optional RIGHT L3 is retained.
The already signed branch-to-bend theorem excludes a following S face.
We now exclude a following LEFT face, so only RIGHT remains.

Normalize

    F lower-left(0,0), J lower-left(-1,-1);
    A=(1,0), B=(0,1), z=(0,0).

A and B have prefix N,N,S, with no LEFT L3. Any L3 at either has incoming
S on RIGHT. The full helper history is z:W,W,E,E, with N outputs R0,L3;
S outputs L1,R2; W inputs R0,L1; E inputs R2,L3. In particular

    T3(z)+1=T1(B).                                   (1)

## 2. Assume the next face is LEFT

Its C face G has lower-left(-1,1), entry B, exit O=(-1,1). Its next C
face H has lower-left(-2,0), height M. The one-edge LEFT face transmits
LEFT-L3 state, so O also has state0. Its complete prefix is W,W,E, and
its only optional L3 has incoming E on RIGHT.

Thus O's W inputs are R0,L1; S outputs are L1,R2; R0 and optional RIGHT
L3 depart N. The exact first S output is supplied by B.R2:

    T1(O)=T2(B)+1.                                   (2)

Put e=(-1,0). Its incident C faces are H at NW and J at SE, BOTH M.
Its E outputs feed z's two E inputs. Its S inputs are exactly O's two
S outputs; any O.RIGHT-L3 output goes N, not S. Therefore

    time(second E output at e)=T3(z)-1=T1(B)-2;
    time(first S input at e)=T1(O)+1=T2(B)+2.         (3)

The second E output is strictly EARLIER than the first S input, with
separation T2(B)-T1(B)+4>=8. These are actual physical occurrence times,
not residue phases; all earlier suppliers of e remain part of its
complete white history.

## 3. The flat-cell obstruction

At a white cap-four active cell with equal incident C heights, both R
reads are present and have opposite headings N/S. Before its FIRST S
input it can have at most ONE E output:

- if R0 arrives S, there is no output before the first S input;
- if R0 arrives N, it emits the first E output. An intervening L1(N)
  emits W, while R2 must arrive S before any later E output is possible;
  alternatively an L1(S) already is the first S input.

Optional L3 and all N/S source alternatives are included. This was the
same exact local lemma used in the signed straight-branch-to-S argument.
It contradicts(3). Hence the next face is not LEFT.

Combined with the already signed no-following-S theorem,

    a STRAIGHT face with an equal-M LEFT branch is followed by RIGHT.

## 4. What this does not prove

This is a real branchful successor restriction. It does not say that
the branch is a leaf, that its parent is a different max component,
that its RIGHT successor has a strict LEFT collar, or that a 0-to-1 L3
rise is automatically realized there. Constant-zero and constant-one
modes, further LEFT max neighbors and arbitrary older suppliers remain
explicit until their own inventories are reconstructed.

In particular the signed TOP max-face leaf LRRR control is not removed
or charged as permanent fuel. Its first three helper R0 reads cannot
silently supply e's SECOND E output; that output must still have the
real whole-history provenance in(3). No K>=2 or general K1 exclusion
follows from this one successor rule.

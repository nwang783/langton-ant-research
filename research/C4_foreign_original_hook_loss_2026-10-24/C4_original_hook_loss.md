The next foreign RAW or its supplied M11 cannot be the first physical deletion of:

- either SAME source R46/R47 identity for source F;
- any of the four SAME original hook identities for source Q or B.

An original may already have died in the ordinary S4 section. This theorem does NOT say all hooks remain CURRENT through C4 unconditionally. It requires a completed marked next foreign, including its actual supplied M11. A partially executed candidate may delete a tag and then fail later. There is no U-entry, earlier-history, global-first-contact, simplicity or epoch-length premise.

# The retained waves are outside the seven-atom language

## Exact result

The retained 1564 and 4352 literal wave words are NOT cycles in the seven RAW atoms D,C,V,U,F,Q,B, at any phase or period multiple.

- 1564 has 21 cyclic M11 first returns: 7 are C and 14 are outside the seven atoms.
- 4352 has 45 cyclic M11 first returns: 14 are C, 3 are D, and 28 are outside the seven atoms.
- Neither periodic word contains even a bare RAW V,U,F,Q,B occurrence. In particular neither contains a complete U/UC/UCC entry with its actual next M11.

This is a phase-independent literal statement. It does not use or alter the complete prepared black fields, their unread classes, or their finite XOR caps. It does not assert that the prepared waves arise from finite seeds forever; the signed finite N-period realizations retain their original scope.

## Why first-return parsing is complete

The actual common marker is M11=LLLLRLRRRRL. For each of the seven frozen RAW words W, the only occurrences of M11 in W followed by its actual next M11 start at offsets 0 and |W|. Thus every atom is an indivisible marker first return.

In any hypothetical seven-atom tiling of a cyclic wave, every atom boundary would therefore be an actual M11 occurrence, and no actual marker could lie inside an atom. Consequently every consecutive cyclic M11 interval would have to be ONE of the seven exact words. The checker computes all such intervals without a parsing heuristic and retains each literal word, exact offset, length, and hash.

The first nonwrapping outside interval is already decisive in each wave:

- 1564: marker tick30 to tick151, length121;
- 4352: marker tick107 to tick190, length83.

Each exceeds the maximum seven-atom RAW length74, and neither contains an internal M11. Their complete offending words are stored in the JSON, together with every other outside return. A different cut, hidden split, physical proper rotation, or larger period multiple cannot repair the mismatch.

## RAW versus complete context

Bare RAW matching is recorded separately. D's length-ten word is a common prefix of EVERY M11 marker, so the 21/45 bare D occurrences do not supply 21/45 complete D packets. The complete D counts are 0/3. Similarly C has 9/16 bare occurrences but only 7/14 followed by actual M11.

U, UC and UCC have zero bare and zero complete cyclic occurrences. Therefore no genuine U-entry ordinary-eight subfactor lies inside either stationary wave body. A U entry earlier in a finite producer or launch is not erased from history; its restricted-language scope has already ended through other return words before it could cover the entire wave.

## Consequence for the geometric work

The wave families are important adversarial controls against mass-only budgets and unsupported regenerative claims, but they are not evidence for or against arbitrary ordinary-tail simplicity within the present seven-atom language. There is no seven-atom proxy to continue through the outside return words without defining and proving new geometry and actual complete contexts for those words.

Conversely, proving that this restricted language terminates would not prove global finite-seed attraction. A separate coverage/transition theorem would still have to connect arbitrary ant behavior to that language. Neither a phase-independent parser nor an arbitrarily long finite wave episode supplies that missing bridge.

No generic trajectory search, new finite episode, proxy self-contact, renewal, or infinite controller is claimed here.

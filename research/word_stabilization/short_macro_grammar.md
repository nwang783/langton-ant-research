# Exact short-word grammar and counting bounds

This is a necessary grammar, not a sufficient physical-realizability criterion. It applies to arbitrary physically feasible concatenations of the five before-marker words C,U,V,Q,B.

Literal same-cell read-parity checks give the following allowed lengths k for A C^k B, with A,B non-C and 0<=k<=3:

| A / B | U | V | Q | B |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| U | 0 | 0,1,2 | 0 | 0 |
| V | 0 | 0,1,2 | 0 | none |
| Q | 0 | 0,1,2 | none | none |
| B | 0 | 0,1,2 | 0 | none |

For initial C runs, C^k U permits only k=0; C^k V permits k=0,1,2,3; C^k Q and C^k B permit k=0,1. Every non-C block followed by C^k permits only k=0,1,2,3. Pure C^4 is feasible but C^5 is not. Consequently k>=4 cannot produce an omitted valid gap.

All permitted entries have explicit finite first-L seeds and separate literal replay. All forbidden entries have a same-cell same-letter conflict. The exact data is `macro_gap_certificate.json`, regenerated by `verify_macro_gap_grammar.py`.

## Counting C blocks

Every internal run of C blocks before another non-C block must end at V and has length at most two. An initial run can exceed this allowance by at most one; a terminal run after a non-C has length at most three. Pure C words have length at most four. Thus universally

n_C <= 2 n_V + 4.

## Counting Q/B blocks

Delete all C blocks. Between consecutive U/V blocks, the remaining Q/B segment is a prefix of BQ. After a V it is at most Q; after a U it is at most BQ. The initial Q/B segment is also at most BQ. Therefore

n_Q+n_B <= 2 n_U+n_V+2.

Combining these bounds,

TOTAL <= 3 n_U + 4 n_V + 6.

Thus proving only that finitely many U and V blocks can occur under finite initial fuel would exclude every infinite five-macro concatenation. No positive-fuel statement for individual Q or B blocks is required.

## Sharper C/V/B consequence

In a C/V/B word, B can occur at most once and only at the beginning of the non-C subsequence. More precisely, its only possible prefix is B or CB: V C^k B and B C^k B are impossible for every k, and CCB is impossible.

Combining n_B<=1, n_C<=2 n_V+4, and the independently certified fresh-V-tip theorem gives

TOTAL <= 3 n_V+5 <= 3m+5

when the entry board has m black cells. A turn-count bound is 48m+134. These sharpen the earlier 8m+8 and 182m+179 bounds. The finite-support exclusion itself remains the same intermediate theorem, and does not address U/Q coset changes or arbitrary recurrent ten-window defects.

# Physical V-tip reuse after adding D

These are exact finite negative controls, not highway counterexamples. They show why the V-tip freshness conjecture from the older five-macro language cannot simply be extended to D/C/V.

DCCCV, followed by actual M11, has a six-cell minimal seed. Its successive macro fuel counts are5,1,0,0,0. The final V tip is (2,0), read R at time8 and then L,R at68,72. This particular DCCC packet is exceptional at the beginning of a marked factor, so it alone does not refute a theorem with initial exceptions.

The second marked factor DDCDCDCDV avoids DCCC entirely. Its exact seed, last-read chronology and one-macro marked extensions are saved in dcv_vtip_reuse_controls.json. Thus excluding the initial DCCC packet does not restore V-tip freshness.

The C++ search is only a discovery tool. replay_dcv_tip_controls.py rebuilds every turn, the minimum first-L seed, and the actual right-marker feasibility directly. No claim of infinite extension is made.

A third control DCVCCVV, also with actual M11, has zero new fuel in its final V despite having no CCC run. Thus even the disjunction that at least one first-L input of every V is globally unread is false in the larger D/C/V language. Its complete seed and marked continuation tests are included in the same JSON.

# C-free six-word fuel, including the diagonal D10 block

Status: independently audited on 2026-10-22, including all weighted raw marks and the terminal-input extension. No seven-word or global capture claim.

## Domain

Let the six raw words be D10=LLLLRLRRRR, V18, U28, F56, Q64, B74, as literally bound in d_cfree_raw_port_quotient.json. F56 is

    LLLLRLRRRRLRLLRRLLLLRRLLRRRRLLRRLRLLRLLRLRLLRRRRLLRLRRRR.

Consider an arbitrary physically feasible factor A1...An, n>=1, over these six words, followed by the actual eleven-letter marker M11=LLLLRLRRRRL. No port-graph premise is imposed. Every right-infinite six-word concatenation supplies that marker at every boundary: all words longer than D share this prefix, while a D boundary obtains its eleventh letter from the next word's initial L. The marker may also be the prefix of a terminal C15.

The heading changes are all0 or2 and the lengths all even. Consequently, after normalizing the first pose to(0,0,N), every macro-entry heading is N or S and every entry cell has even checkerboard parity. This restricted reader set is essential; allowing impossible E/W intermediate macro poses makes the corresponding overapproximation inconclusive.

## Fuel marks and bounds

For each raw type, all marked sites in d_cfree_fuel_marks.json are FIRST read L inside that raw block and are unread in every earlier raw block. Their respective counts are

    D:1, V:2, U:3, F:10, Q:11, B:14.

In particular (-1,0), phase1, is fresh for every type. The four common startup first-L sites (0,0),(-1,-1),(0,-1),(1,0) in the first block are distinct from all counted sites. No later fresh mark can coincide with any of them. Thus, if m is the complete-board black mass at factor entry,

    4+d+2v+3u+10f+11q+14b <= m.

All charges concern entry-black cells in this factor, not cells globally unread before the ant's earlier history. Comparing each raw length to ten times its weight gives

    raw turns <= 10(m-4).

Comparing net mass gain to twice its weight gives exit mass<=3m-8. Pure D powers attain the turn bound. Therefore no finite-support ant can have an infinite tail entirely in this six-word language.

## Fresh terminal boundary input

The same certificate marks phase len(A)+1 of every target A+M11. The marked physical points, in A's entry frame, are respectively

    D:(0,1), V:(0,3), U:(-1,4),
    F:(-3,-2), Q:(-3,-4), B:(-2,-5).

Each is the FIRST read at that point in A+M11. Together with the forbidden pairs, this proves that the first-L phase1 input of the next macro or terminal C is unread throughout the preceding C-free factor. This is not freshness across any earlier C block.

# Bounded sweep bags are real; a finite geometry quotient is another theorem

## Exact old-U window

Start at a genuine creating U, normalized to (0,0,N), inside an uninterrupted complete-eight prefix. Include its FIRST foreign A, then an ordinary suffix through the RAW atom making the global first new contact with this old U chord. The actual final M11 prefix is supplied once, as a real following physical prefix.

The signed first-contact compression gives total source-to-contact-ENTRY C count K<=4. The terminal contact C, if present, belongs to the final entry-heading epoch; its exit rotation does not add another ordinary epoch within this RAW window. There are at most K+2<=6 ordinary constant-heading epochs, counting the additional split at the foreign half-turn.

No bound on epoch length, initial black mass, dormant boundary ports, or transverse distance is assumed.

## A sweep coordinate with strictly monotone epochs

Use s=2x+y. The actual ordinary head drifts have these s increments in headings N,E,S,W:

| Atom | N | E | S | W |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| D | 3 | 1 | -3 | -1 |
| C | 1 | 2 | -1 | -2 |
| V | 5 | 5 | -5 | -5 |
| U | 4 | 8 | -4 | -8 |

Every N/E epoch is strictly increasing, every S/W epoch strictly decreasing, with minimum absolute step one. This includes a terminal C in its actual incoming heading.

Reconstruct each atom's complete fine vertices, including its actual exit, not just its proxy chord. Exact maximum |s(vertex)-s(entry head)| is8 for ordinary RAWs,14 for a foreign RAW, and5 for the final actual M11. The checker retains all28 atom/heading intervals. It never uses unsafe whole Q/B proxy chords to estimate literal support.

## The actual read-constraint graph has a bounded path decomposition

Make one graph vertex per ACTUAL fine read occurrence. Add:

1. program-order edges between consecutive physical reads;
2. color edges between consecutive visits to the SAME physical cell.

An atom block's interval I is the minimum/maximum s of its fine vertices, including entry and exit. At each integer sweep level l, put ALL read occurrences of every block with l in I into its bag. Add the one foreign block and final-prefix block according to their own intervals.

A read vertex appears on one contiguous bag interval. A same-cell edge is covered at l=s(cell), since both blocks contain that read vertex geometrically. A program-order edge inside a block is immediate. Across a true atom boundary, both block intervals contain the exact physical cut head, so the two read occurrences share a bag there too. Including the exit vertex is essential for the length-ten D boundary.

Any ordinary block meeting level l has its entry head s in [l-8,l+8]. There are at most17 such heads per monotone epoch and at most6 epochs. Therefore every bag has at most

    6*17 = 102 ordinary atoms,
    104 total blocks after adding A and the final actual prefix,
    102*28 + 74 + 11 = 2941 actual read occurrences.

The embedded read-constraint graph thus has pathwidth at most2940. This is an exact cardinality statement independent of unbounded run lengths.

Chronology is not guessed from geometric left-to-right order. The epoch's chronological index comes first. Within N/E, increasing s is forward; within S/W it is backward. The actual RAW tick then orders individual reads. The foreign and final prefix retain their actual slots. No copy of M11 is executed after every atom.

If an initial board is fixed, its actual first-read bits must be supplied and checked. Unread black cells stay unchanged; they are not deleted by the decomposition. For existence of some finite initial board, a finite consistent word has the usual exact first-L seed, but that is a separate existence use, not a substitution for a prescribed complete field.

Complete finite physical regressions U,F,D^n plus actual M11 at n=1,10,100 are replayed from their full minimum boards. Every program-order and same-cell edge is checked against the interval bags. These regressions are not claimed to contact the old U chord or to prove an all-n word family.

## What is finite, and what still carries unbounded integers

Once a CORRECT embedded/coincidence certificate is given, the active cell equality partition, color bits and chronological local order have bounded finite size. That establishes a finite color-consistency part.

It does NOT automatically establish a finite-state GENERATOR/VERIFIER for the geometry of all possible epoch words. Relative positions perpendicular to s may be unbounded. Setting t=x-2y exposes an explicit missing register.

Let X=D^5 and Y=V^3, both N-oriented. Their actual head drifts satisfy

    X: (5,5),  delta s=15, delta t=-5;
    Y: (3,9),  delta s=15, delta t=-15.

Consider two monotone geometric tracks starting together. A block A gives track1 X and track2 Y; a block B swaps them. After A^n B^m, both tracks have exactly the same s advance, but their transverse endpoint gap is

    10(n-m).

Endpoint coincidence is equivalent to n=m. A finite-state machine reading these finite spatial block labels and checking that endpoint identification would recognize {A^n B^n}, which is not regular: prefixes A^n have pairwise distinguishable right contexts B^n. Yet the number of active tracks remains two and their local step types are fixed.

This is a geometric-certificate obstruction to the inference “bounded bag cardinality implies finite geometry state.” It is NOT an actual Langton-ant color history, not a genuine U-entry countercontrol, and not proof against some stronger ant-specific quotient. A complete current-U invariant might remove precisely such variable-gap configurations. That extra theorem has not been proved here.

The honest current exact representation is a bounded finite color/order frontier PLUS boundedly many potentially unbounded transverse-potential differences. A finite quotient would have to justify when those differences may be forgotten or collapsed. Bounded bag size alone supplies no such justification and no general decidability claim.

## Smaller tractable windows

With only two unbounded epoch tracks, one relative transverse gap may suffice after fixed endpoint anchors and bounded connector blocks are retained. That suggests a one-counter construction, not a zero-counter DFA. Adjacent-heading track pairs have a pointed combined drift cone, so bounded endpoint displacement can sometimes bound both lengths outright; opposite-heading pairs can cancel and need the gap variable.

These are structural observations, not a completed automaton theorem for every two-track U window. The next task is a specific actual-history template with both fixed endpoint/core conditions and every cell-color/order obligation. Earlier D/C/V-source ancestry is not assumed covered by the old-U decomposition.

# A finite prior-run horizon for the two open D-source contacts

Author exact geometry and topological proof, pending independent audit. This is a conditional ancestry restriction, not an exclusion or attainment of either residue.

## Hypotheses and notation

An actual finite complete-eight factor starts with U/UC/UCC, then executes its FIRST complete foreign word. The signed one-first-foreign theorem says the old atomic corrected-center path plus the repaired foreign proxy is simple. The immediately returned ordinary atom has a chronological FIRST new contact with the old path at the selected outgoing D source. Normalize that D to head (0,0,N), doubled corrected center (-1,-1), source clock zero. Its containing full packet may be D or DC. The signed necessary catalogue leaves exactly:

- row15: F(2,-5,E), old clock3, returned C(0,-1,W), whose endpoint q=(-1,-1) is the old D START;
- row30: F(3,-4,E), old clock3, returned V(1,0,W), whose first-contact midpoint q=(0,0) is the old D midpoint.

The actual foreign is F, so its repaired proxy is its single actual F56 piece. The signed returned-atom local interface guarantees no contact with this foreign piece except the true adjacent starting endpoint. No returned simplicity beyond the FIRST-contact segment is assumed.

## The simple loop, including source-vertex ownership

Take the old arc beginning at q, running forward along the selected D (or its latter half), all later old atoms and F, then the returned straight segment up to its first contact q. It is a Jordan loop. The old-plus-F arc is simple. Before its terminal q, the returned segment has no old contact by the FIRST-contact hypothesis and no extra foreign contact by the signed local interface. Its true starting adjacency is counted only once. A straight returned segment has no internal self-intersection.

In row15, q is an old vertex. The incoming previous atom also ends at q, but this is the SAME contact point, owned by outgoing D in the catalogue. It is not discarded as a forbidden second contact, and that incoming segment is not part of the loop boundary. Its points strictly before q must be placed by a local-side argument below. In row30 q is in the strict interior of D, so old simplicity rules out any additional old segment at q.

The signed principal-tangent lift, with three old C atoms and exactly one foreign whose preceding atom is NOT C, is

5*pi/2 + alpha_return - alpha_D,

from the D tangent to the returned tangent. Here alpha_D=alpha_C=pi/4 and alpha_V=atan(3), strictly between pi/4 and pi/2. The final principal corner from returned tangent back to the outgoing D tangent is -pi/4-alpha_return. Therefore the loop's total principal turn is exactly +2*pi, and it is counterclockwise. This uses the existing repaired-proxy lift including actual endpoint conventions; it does not suppress a C-to-foreign wrap. That wrap is absent here because the signed literal pair table permits only plain V/U immediately before the first foreign.

## Every earlier old point lies strictly inside

For row15 the outgoing D ray has angle pi/4; the reverse of the incoming returned-C ray has angle7*pi/4. The counterclockwise loop has the open reflex interior sector (pi/4,7*pi/4) at q. Exhaust ALL actual possible incoming old atoms at the selected D head:

- a preceding D oriented N has reverse ray5*pi/4;
- a preceding V oriented N has reverse ray pi+atan(3);
- a preceding U oriented N has reverse ray3*pi/2;
- a preceding actual C oriented E has reverse ray3*pi/4.

Every ray is STRICTLY inside that sector. The C case includes an actual internal first/second tail of its containing full producer; no fictitious C marker is introduced. Thus a sufficiently small terminal portion of the REAL incoming old segment lies inside the loop. The prior old prefix is connected and has no other point on the loop: old-plus-F simplicity excludes intersections with the forward old/F boundary, while the FIRST-contact hypothesis excludes intersections with the returned boundary except the same q. It cannot change from the bounded interior to the exterior without another boundary contact. Hence its entire prefix, with q deleted, lies strictly inside. This includes its initial U-entry endpoint.

For row30 the reverse ray of the earlier half of selected D is5*pi/4. It lies strictly inside the counterclockwise reflex sector from outgoing pi/4 to the reversed incoming returned-V ray3*pi/2+atan(3). Therefore a small earlier D subsegment is inside. Its connection through the old D start to the whole earlier prefix has no boundary contact, by the same simple-prefix and FIRST-contact hypotheses. The whole strictly earlier old prefix is inside. No claim about a prefix is inferred merely from the tangent of an unrelated historical edge.

## A lower support bound for the ENTIRE loop

Let Y be the foreign head y coordinate: -5 in row15 and -4 in row30. Every point of the loop has doubled y at least L=2Y+1.

This is proved for the entire intervening old path, not assumed from its endpoints. Before the first C, N heads have y>=0. After the first C, W heads have y>=2: a C at N increases head y by one, and W zero-C D/V moves increase y while U leaves it unchanged. The W-to-S C leaves head y unchanged. From its S exit through the S stage and then the E stage, head y is nonincreasing: S D/V/U decrease it; S-to-E C decreases it by one; E D/V decrease it and U preserves it. Consequently every E head before F has y>=Y, and every preceding S head has y>=Y+1. S and E corrected-center offsets in doubled y are both+1. N/W corrected-center offset is-1. Thus all these vertices, and hence every straight ordinary atom chord between them, have doubled y>=2Y+1. This also covers all actual internal C-tail endpoints and the complete source DC tail.

F in heading E has corrected displacement(-2,+6), so its minimum is its start y=2Y+1. The returned C/V segments rise in doubled y. Their segment up to q also stays above L. Therefore the WHOLE Jordan boundary is in the closed upper halfplane y>=L.

The bounded Jordan interior lies in the STRICT upper halfplane y>L. The open lower halfplane connects to infinity without touching the boundary; and a point on the support line cannot be interior because every neighborhood contains points below it.

Combining confinement with support, every actual old point strictly before selected D has doubled y greater than -9 (row15) or -7 (row30). This geometric containment is not a bound on earlier clocks or source ages.

## Translate only to the actual last zero-C N run

Consider the real maximal run of plain zero-C full D/V/U packets immediately BEFORE the selected D. Every member has heading N. If it has counts d,v,u, its vertical displacement is H=d+3v+4u, and its horizontal displacement is d+v. Its initial head is (-(d+v),-H), with doubled center y=-2H-1. When this run is nonempty that actual earlier start lies STRICTLY inside, so -2H-1>L. Thus:

- row15: H<=3, and the exhaustive possible words are empty, D, DD, DDD, V;
- row30: H<=2, and the exhaustive possible words are empty, D, DD.

No U can occur in either run. Row30 has no V. These are derived bounds, not an arbitrary predecessor depth cutoff. They say nothing comparable about an earlier run at another heading or older C clock.

There MUST be an actual positive-C full packet immediately before this maximal N run. Otherwise the run would extend back to the factor's initial U family. With no earlier C, that family must be plain U heading N, adding vertical displacement four, which violates either bound. Hence the preceding boundary packet is one of DC, VC, VCC, UC, UCC, ending at the run's initial N cut. The selected D's OWN possible C tail occurs after it and is not confused with this earlier boundary.

The checker enumerates exactly5*(5+3)=40 boundary interfaces: last positive-C packet plus the finite allowable run. It records the true packet entry and its actual final E-to-N C entry. The history BEFORE that packet remains arbitrary. These 40 cases are an exhaustive interface for applying genuine prior-cohort/core guards; they are not an exhaustive list of whole prefixes or proof that all 40 are feasible.

## Exact limit

The two underlying residues remain necessary-only. This theorem supplies a finite backward run horizon and genuine boundary packet, with incoming endpoint touches and C-tail ownership explicit. It neither preserves an original bud through F's active-core rewrite nor claims returned simplicity, all earlier-clock boundedness, or universal seven-word termination.

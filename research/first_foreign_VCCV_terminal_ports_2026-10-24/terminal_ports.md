# A terminal marked interface for the genuine erosion morphology

Author certificate, pending independent review. This is not arbitrary-tail simplicity and does not say that arbitrary output erosion is terminal.

## Statement

Take an actual complete-eight interval followed by its FIRST complete F/Q/B, then the four ordinary RAW atoms V,C,C,V. Retain the actual next M11, as in the signed erosion controls. This precise interface cannot be extended by any next complete seven-atom D/C/V/U/F/Q/B RAW plus its actual next M11.

This statement is about completed marked continuations. In some rejected targets, the added D or C RAW really completes and the contradiction occurs only in its following actual M11. Neither RAW completion nor a future marker read is silently identified with a completed marked packet.

## Physical port field, not output permanence

Normalize the first returned V to (0,0,N). At the next atom entry after V,C,C,V:

- Its original A=(0,1) is still physically alive in active CORE, not a CURRENT residual output. A real next-word read2 may delete it, as in the signed genuine UQ/UB positive controls.
- Its entry-core cells (-1,0) and (-1,-1), physically deleted by that V's L1/L2, remain WHITE. The C,C,V portion never rereads them.
- Certain genuine foreign R-born shadows remain BLACK. No old-eight age law is used to preserve them; their actual word trace contains no intervening read.

The exact first demanded color and physical owner is reconstructed in every case. All CORE writes/removals count, not only residual exports.

## Twenty-one complete next-word cases

For each F/Q/B and next D/C/V/U/F/Q/B, execute the exact whole literal word plus actual M11. Of these 21 prepared next-word targets, exactly one is feasible: B,V,C,C,V,V.

The uniform deleted-hole cases are:

- Next D needs the first V's L1-deleted hole at its marker-owned L11. This is actual following-word tick1/read2, not D RAW.
- Next U/F/Q/B needs the first V's L2-deleted hole at its own RAW L12/read13.

Next C and next V instead hit exact still-BLACK foreign shadows with a required first R/WHITE read. For F and Q this closes every remaining target. Source R ownership is retained and no physical lifetime is reset at a marked boundary.

## The sole prepared B escape needs a missing earlier port

The complete prepared B,V,C,C,V,V control is real and has a simple repaired proxy. It consumes B's R67 shadow, via alive-CORE absorption at the last V RAW exit and physical next-word tick1/read2 death. This is not erosion of the separate four-tag B hook. Its complete finite board and every read are preserved as a countercontrol to treating this particular B shadow as unconditionally persistent.

But an actual FIRST B immediately after a complete-eight interval must follow plain U. All eight full-G8 predecessor types are explicitly checked, in agreement with the signed adjacency theorem. The resulting fixed word U,B,V,C,C,V,V has an immutable repeated-L conflict: U's L1 deleted physical (-1,0), and the last V requires BLACK there at its own RAW tick13/read14. No intervening read repairs that old preforeign U core hole. An arbitrarily larger earlier history cannot change this conflict between two reads of the fixed substring.

Thus the prepared B escape has no genuine stipulated ancestry. The two positive genuine UQ/UB erosion controls are real, but this exact morphology cannot seed an unbounded seven-atom continuation. No analogous claim is made for another postforeign pattern, a later foreign with another predecessor class, or an arbitrary erosion state.

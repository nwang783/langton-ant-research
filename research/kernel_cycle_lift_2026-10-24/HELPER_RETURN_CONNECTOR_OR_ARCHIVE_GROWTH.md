# An actual helper return must retire old connectors or grow the archive

24 October 2026. A scoped spatial application of the RETAINED axial charge,
not a new invariant. This focuses on the helper RETURN LEG [d,j], so its
cap hypothesis is already inside the parent episode. It does not assume a
bound back to the helper's birth, or a stack order, or a simple route.

## 1. Actual lifetimes and the charged alternative

At cut d the parent's marked lifetime e has just been left dormant. Its
next read is the actual L at c. An old helper f is already black at d,
at a remote cell y, and is consumed at update j<c, with d<=j. It may be
an initially dormant root; if R-born and previously active it also has the
earlier departure s<d described in the chronology note. Put

    L = ||y-p_d||_infinity.

Let K be the number of DISTINCT cut-d black lifetimes ACTUALLY consumed
during updates [d,j), BEFORE the helper L. Their coordinates may later be
recolored, but retirement of that lifetime is counted once. These are
additional old-at-departure inputs: neither f nor e can be among them,
because both survive until j or later. 'Older' here means present at d,
NOT necessarily born before the helper's own birth. In particular a recent
preparation-R lifetime can be one of these connectors.

The complete boards satisfy the exact spatial alternative

    |B_j| - |B_d| + 2K >= 2(L-1),                         (1)

with the harmless right side replaced by0 if L=0. The endpoint is the
PRE-read cut j: the selected helper is still black there. If the complete
black mass does not increase during this return leg, then

    K >= L-1.                                             (2)

Thus a materially neutral long-range return must retire linearly many
ADDITIONAL old-at-departure lifetimes before reaching its selected helper.
Conversely, with no such retirement, the complete board must grow by at
least2(L-1) cells. Unproductive reattachments, cycle changes, and all legal
deletions/rebirths of newer material are allowed.

## 2. Exact linewise proof, inherited charge stated explicitly

Choose a coordinate attaining L. Let J consist of its L-1 strict separating
integer lines between p_d and y. The retained axial charge, applied to the
actual interval [d,j], forces at least two bit discrepancies between B_d
and B_j on EVERY line in J, regardless of endpoint headings. This is the
strict-line result in `global_capture_bridge_2026-10-23/
live_cursor_transport_debt.md`, with comparison board B_d and zero height.

Write A=B_j\B_d and O=B_d\B_j. Summing disjoint lines gives

    |A|+|O| >= 2(L-1).

Every O cell has retired its original cut-d black lifetime, hence |O|<=K.
But |B_j|-|B_d|=|A|-|O|. Therefore

    |B_j|-|B_d|+2K >= |A|+|O| >= 2(L-1),

which proves(1). If an old cell is consumed and reborn black by j, it is
included in K but not O; this only makes the inequality stronger. No
current component is cropped and no birth is relabeled as an initial cell.

There is a sharper spatially resolved form. Let K_r count old cut-d
lifetimes retired on one strict line r, and let Delta_r be the change in
the number of black cells ON THAT LINE between d and j. Then

    Delta_r + 2K_r >= 2.                                  (3)

In particular if no older lifetime is consumed on that line, its current
black mass increases by at least2. This charges actual spatial placement,
not only a multiset of archive types. Summing(3) uses only older inputs
on the separating lines; the global form(1) safely permits losses elsewhere
by charging ALL old retirements K.

## 3. What a bounded active component forces into dormant output

Suppose every COMPLETE active w component at cuts d,...,j has at most
M edges, M>=2. Let P_t=|B_t|-|C_t| be the complete dormant black mass.
Because |C_j|-|C_d|<=M, equation(1) implies

    P_j-P_d >= 2(L-1)-2K-M.                               (4)

So low additional old-input use forces an actual increase in the FULL
dormant archive mass during the return. This is not mass descent.

Let F be the current black lifetimes at cut j born by R during [d,j),
and N_new the number of COMPLETE dormant components containing them.
The line charge gives |F|>=2(L-1)-K. Every dormant component containing
a still-live post-d R birth was active at that birth, and was last released
by an actual bridge L under cap M. Its FULL size is therefore at most
M-1 and remains exactly frozen from that last departure to j. At most M
fresh cells are active. Hence

    N_new >= ceil( (2(L-1)-K-M)_+ / (M-1) ).                (5)

All arbitrary untouched exterior fields and all prior archives are kept.
The passive components counted here can contain old cells as well as new,
but their complete size, not only their fresh subset, is bounded by the
last-departure argument. The opposite active graph is not assumed bounded.

## 5. Exact remaining global gap

This is a conditional connector-or-archive-growth requirement for a real
return, not an impossibility theorem for arbitrarily many returns. Fresh
output may be stored in many dormant components while active size stays
fixed. It may later be consumed, merged or regenerated; then that later
interval has its own old cohort and its own retirement charge. No rule
here injects those charges into original seed cells or prevents the same
spatial stock from supporting overlapping episodes.

Thus the missing organization step is precisely whether those compulsory
spatially distributed fresh archives can be made repeatedly USABLE at the
needed ports without a net loss elsewhere. Equations(1)--(5) survive legal
regeneration but do not make its cost additive across overlapping legs.
They prove neither eventual translation nor universal standard104 capture.

The retained20-cell feedback,184-cell preparation,42-step non-LIFO control,
and standard growing T12 output remain valid controls. Nothing here
reinterprets unread topological support as actual consumed connector fuel.

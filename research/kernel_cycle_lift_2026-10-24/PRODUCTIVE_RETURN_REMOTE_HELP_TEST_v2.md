# Remote old-cohort help: an unproved route test and a proved erasure reduction

24 October 2026. Version2 preserves the earlier unreviewed draft. The16/4
route statement is CONJECTURAL: no motivation proving those constants, linear
bound or universal truth is supplied. The proved content is a marked-component
localization and one-time old-cohort erasure lemma. Intermediate unproductive
reattachments and legal new-field regeneration are explicitly allowed.

## 1. A finite, falsifiable route predicate

Keep a complete finite-board trajectory and one marked BLACK lifetime e.
At update d-1 an L bridge leaves its complete w component D dormant. It is
eventually reimported, may activate/depart arbitrarily many times, and is
ACTUALLY consumed at its next read, L at c. A rebirth at the same coordinate
would be a different lifetime and is not substituted for e.

Put p=p_d and let M=max |C_t| over ALL fine cuts d-1<=t<=c. Let R be the
maximum infinity distance of the physical head from p during d<=t<=c.
An old remote consumption is an L at a cell outside p+[-4M,4M]^2 consuming
a lifetime already BLACK at cut d. First consumption, not mere coordinate
membership after a rebirth, is what retires this old-cohort resource.

**RH16/4 testing hypothesis, NOT a theorem:** if R>16M then at least one old
remote consumption occurs before c. The explicit constants are a test target,
not a derived inequality. A counterexample is one finite trace with R>16M
and no such old-cohort L. It would exhibit compact-source, self-made far
feedback at a small active-w cap; it need not be a noncapturing orbit.

Even if true, the claim would not automatically be a decreasing global rank:
future episodes may regenerate new lifetimes. It would charge one OLD
spatially placed lifetime and retain its irreversible retirement, not reset
credits when its coordinate is recolored later.

## 2. Proved marked-component spatial localization

At d-1 every D edge was active, so its midpoint is within M of the old head
by the signed metric lemma. The departure move adds at most1. Thus D and
the marked cell x lie within M+1<=2M of p.

Whenever e is ACTIVE, the head is within M of x and every active edge is
within M of that head. The ENTIRE complete active component containing e
is therefore inside p+[-(3M+1),3M+1]^2, hence inside the4M box. Whenever e
is dormant, its complete component is exactly frozen until its next R
activation. At a later bridge departure that frozen component is a subset
of the preceding active field. Starting with D, this proves that the FULL
component containing e stays inside the4M box at EVERY cut through c,
despite arbitrary intervening legal reattachments.

This is a fixed box around a particular marked lifetime, not a moving window
around the head or a bound on the complete exterior field. The head can be
arbitrarily far while e is dormant. The opposite active z component is not
assumed bounded.

## 3. Proved one-time old-cohort erasure lemma

Assume there is NO old remote consumption through c. At cut d erase exactly
the cells of B_d outside the4M box ONCE, then continue the ordinary rule.
All subsequent R births, including those outside the box, are kept normally.

Every removed cell was initially black. If it had a first later read before
c, that read would be L consuming its old-cohort lifetime, contrary to the
hypothesis. Hence none is read. First-difference induction gives IDENTICAL
physical head, headings, turns and both cursor positions through c. At each
cut the new black field is the old field minus those never-read old cells.

Both black graphs are subgraphs, so active w mass cannot increase. During
dormant excursions, the OTHER current active component can shrink, lose
imported archives, or change cycle/bridge labels. No claim of complete active
graph equality there is made. The active z graph may change even when e is
active; its size was never bounded.

By Section2, however, NO edge of the marked lifetime's complete w component
at ANY cut was removed. That component stays connected and wholly present;
deletion cannot connect anything new. It is therefore exactly the SAME
complete component in both fields at every cut. In particular the marked
active/dormant itinerary, complete frozen archives, all its actual reimports,
the final productive activation and L consumption are preserved exactly.
The final active w field equality is justified by its fixed radius bound,
not by the fact that erased cells were merely unread.

The original bridge-departure L can also be prepended unchanged: its deleted
cell and complete active field were local and retained. This produces a
genuine compact finite-board witness, not a mid-run cropped simulation.

## 5. Concrete coordinated falsification target

By the erasure lemma, any RH16/4 counterexample can be converted to a compact
initial board supported in the4M departure box, while preserving all later
self-written remote material and the marked module's complete itinerary.
Thus a proposed self-building route/return program need not be padded with
infinite prelaid tracks. Use the retained supplied-word verifier to require
that every needed black cell outside the source box has an earlier actual
R after departure, then check the complete coupled board, M, old cohort and
marked consumption. A valid finite trace is a decisive falsifier; absence
of a trace or resource exhaustion is not a proof.

The184 and20 families must be tested with their ACTUAL active maximum and
departure cohort, not original mass or a cropped component. No first-contact
L is relabeled as a time-zero seed lifetime, no passive component is moved,
and no universal capture or computable waiting deadline is inferred here.

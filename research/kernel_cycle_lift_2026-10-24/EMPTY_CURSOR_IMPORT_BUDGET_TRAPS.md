# Complete old-field placement can saturate an empty cursor's R-star budget

24 October 2026. New physical continuation lemma, independently auditable
without the large cap-six return-profile enumeration. This is not a general
cap-six exit theorem. It filters actual complete-field output states and
supplied return-word classes; it does not infer dynamics from LP failure.

## 1. Exact four-R budget at an empty w cursor

Keep the COMPLETE actual finite black field and its fixed w graph. Let the
active w component be empty at a pre-update cut, with cursor r. All four
incident physical ports are therefore white. The next four updates are R,
with stationary w cursor, and write its four distinct incident edges. No L
can intervene: each next port was white and has not yet been toggled.

Let N(r) be the four neighboring w vertices. Let U_actual be the union of
the COMPLETE old black components that meet N(r), counting their edges once.
At the cut after the four R updates, the complete active component is EXACTLY

    the four new star edges UNION U_actual.                 (1)

All four new cells were initially white, so these edge sets are disjoint.
The stationary-root insertions cannot delete, translate or crop any imported
component. In particular a sustained active cap M requires

    |U_actual| <= M-4.                                    (2)

This is a statement about actual full embedded components, not abstract
passive-type weight or merely the next turn word.

## 2. A budget-saturating NONfirst-port trap

Let F be any specified finite subset of old BLACK edges in the actual field.
Let U_F be the union of the connected components of F that meet N(r). The
components are computed in the graph F, but every one is contained in an
actual complete component, so U_F is a genuine edge subset of U_actual.
Let u be the other endpoint of the FIRST inserted star edge r--u.

**Theorem.** Fix M>=4. At an empty active cursor:

1. If |U_F|>M-4, some complete active component exceeds M within four updates.
2. If |U_F|=M-4 and u is NOT a vertex incident to U_F, some complete active
   component exceeds M within nine updates.

Both conclusions hold for arbitrary unread black fields outside F. Those
fields are not deleted or adjusted between steps.

**Proof.** Part1 follows from (1). For part2, if cap failure occurs during
the first four R updates there is nothing to prove. Otherwise (2), together
with U_F subset U_actual and equal cardinality M-4, forces

    U_actual = U_F.

Thus the first tip u has no old incident edge. In the new four-edge star it
is a leaf, even if other old components join two other star tips or make
cycles behind it. The current physical head has returned to the first R
cell, so the next update is L on r--u. It crosses to isolated u, leaving the
COMPLETE connected archived component

    G = the other three new star edges UNION U_F,
    |G|=3+(M-4)=M-1.                                     (3)

Every component of U_F attaches to one of those other three tips. No further
outside edge can touch G without already having belonged to U_actual. Hence
G is complete, not a cropped mandatory subset. At the new empty cursor u,
the next four R updates must meet the old center r, a neighbor of u. They
therefore import ALL of G. Four new edges plus M-1 old edges have mass M+3>M.
This occurs by the ninth update from the original empty cut. QED.

The wrong-FIRST-port hypothesis is essential. A full old-edge budget present
at the first tip can remain active after the L, rather than force an empty
reset. The theorem deliberately leaves that case open.

## 3. Application to actual supplied empty-to-empty words

Normalize a supplied actual return word to initial head(0,0),N, whose fixed
w sector is1. Its pose/read positions are fixed by the literal R/L turns.
Every cell whose LAST read in the word is R is BLACK at its final cut. Use
these mandatory black cells as F. No colour is inferred from a proxy path.
Assume the word ends at a genuinely empty COMPLETE active w component.

For the twelve words supplied by the cap-six kernel analysis, exact coordinate
calculation gives the following seven traps at M=6:

    word             |U_F|    reason
    RRRRL               3     exceeds old-edge budget2
    RRRRLL              3     exceeds old-edge budget2
    RRRRLRRL            5     exceeds old-edge budget2
    RRRRLLRL            4     exceeds old-edge budget2
    RRRRLLRRL           5     exceeds old-edge budget2
    RRRRLLLLRL          2     budget saturated away from first tip
    RRRRLRLRRL          2     budget saturated away from first tip

Thus EVERY actual empty-to-empty execution of one of these seven words must
exceed active mass6 within nine further updates, regardless the full unread
field and regardless which local import-profile realizes that word.

The five supplied words not settled by this lemma are

    RRRRLRL, RRRRLRLL, RRRRLRRLL, RRRRLRLRL, RRRRLRRLRL.

The table's forced imported masses are respectively1,1,2,1,1; in the mass2
case the first tip IS occupied, so part2 does not apply. These are unresolved
word classes here, not claimed actual eternal mechanisms. Exhaustiveness of
the twelve-word kernel grammar is a separate dependency owned by that analysis;
this note proves the physical trap for each supplied listed word without
loading or treating the enumeration as a trajectory compiler.

## 4. Concrete non-reset writer and arbitrary-routing obstruction

The resource-relaxed cap-six witness index21795 has literal word

    W=RRRRLRLRRL,
    drift(-1,3), heading restored.

It is PHYSICALLY realized from the complete two-cell seed

    {(-1,1),(-1,2)}, pose(0,0),N.

Every fine-cut active w component has at most6 edges through W. At cut10 its
pose is(-1,3),N, its active w cursor r=(-1,5) in doubled coordinates is empty,
and its complete output consists of:

    corner D={(-2,1),(-2,2)};
    singleton {(-1,0)};
    star {(0,-1),(1,-1),(1,0)}.

The output corner is the same abstract two-edge type as the input, and the
net type count creates a singleton and star. That does NOT make it reusable.
The next four R edges touch D at their FOURTH port. Its vertices are
(-5,1),(-3,3),(-5,5), and the four other star endpoints are

    (-3,7), (1,7), (1,3), (-3,3).

If D is the exact complete two-edge component, it uses the entire old-edge
budget. The forced L then leaves first tip(-3,7) empty and archives the
five-edge corner-plus-three-new-edges field. Its next empty-root R sweep
must reimport that full field and exceed6. This excludes ANY cap-six routing
continuation from this exact output state, not merely identical W repetition.
If a larger complete component contains D, the first sweep already exceeds6.

More generally, W itself writes both D cells at slots7 and8 and never reads
them again. Hence the trap applies to EVERY actual W return profile, not
just witness21795. No mid-run cells, pose reset, generic inverse theorem,
graph-role swap, or freely movable component is used.

    z=p+r_h(-1/2,-1/2),
    w=p+r_h( 1/2,-1/2).

They lie in the opposite checkerboard diagonal lattices, and

    w-z=r_h(1,0).                                        (1)

Each physical cell q is simultaneously the midpoint of a primal even edge
and its PERPENDICULAR dual odd edge. Both are occupied exactly when q is
black. These are two separate planar embeddings, not two crossing occupied
edges in one planar graph.

At the currently inspected cell p the endpoints are

    primal: z, z+r_h(1,1),
    dual:   w, w+r_h(-1,1).

The ordinary physical update is EXACTLY

    R: add both corresponding occupied edges;
       z traverses the primal edge, w STAYS fixed.
    L: delete both corresponding occupied edges;
       w traverses the dual edge, z STAYS fixed.           (2)

Heading changes by +1 on R and -1 on L, preserving (1). Thus the two edge
walks cannot be chosen, translated, reset or teleported independently. This
is the physical cursor continuity missing from many graph relaxations.

The signed newer active-star statement is the z half of (2). The retained

## 2. Exact boundary-edge visit/port identities

Fix a set A of primal vertices and its edge cut Q=delta A, represented by
physical cells. In any finite actual interval count R_q and L_q at each
q in Q. These are actual traversals/erasures at the two linked port systems.
For every q,

    b_t(q)-b_s(q)=R_q-L_q.                                (3)

The read letters at q alternate, so an occupied gate cannot carry a primal
R crossing until an actual L deletion has cleared it. Let R_out,R_in be the
primal z crossings out of/into A. Then

    R_out-R_in=1_A(z_s)-1_A(z_t),
    L_Q=R_Q-sum_(q in Q)(b_t(q)-b_s(q)),
    reads_Q=2R_Q-sum_(q in Q)(b_t(q)-b_s(q)).              (4)

All sums are finite over the actually inspected cut cells; an infinite
common background causes no divergent sum. Equation (4) is an interface
transcript constraint, not a bound on how often a gate can be erased and
reinstalled. The side/port of each R is the actual z endpoint; the side/port
of each L is the actual w endpoint.

## 3. A closed current fence gives sound temporary archive forgetting

    1_inside(F)(w_t)-1_inside(F)(w_s)

Suppose w_s is outside F. Before the first L read of an F edge:

- w remains outside F;
- no physical cell strictly inside F is read;
- the whole field strictly inside F cannot affect the actual outside trace.

Indeed an inspected dual edge is incident to w. Unless it is an F edge,
that dual edge is disjoint from F and lies entirely on w's side. An interior
cell therefore cannot be inspected while w is outside. Since every F edge

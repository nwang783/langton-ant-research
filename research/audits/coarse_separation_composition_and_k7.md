# Coarse separation under composition and the axis-swapping radix-seven case

Independent analytic audit, 2026-10-23 00:51 UTC.

Suppose a direction substitution has common nonsingular integer endpoint matrix A, and every proper nonempty image prefix displacement avoids A Z². In its second iterate, every sub-block starts at a point of A Z². A point in A² Z² is also in A Z², so it cannot occur internally within a sub-block. At a sub-block boundary it equals A p, where p is an image-prefix displacement. Membership A p in A² Z² implies p in A Z² by injectivity. Thus only the two overall endpoints can lie in A² Z². This proves separation for the second iterate, and the same argument inductively proves it for every iterate.

For odd axis-swapping length-seven images with endpoint matrix 3I, an east image has four vertical and three horizontal moves. Net displacement(3,0) forces all three horizontal moves east and two vertical moves each way. The only proper prefix with x=0 has y=±1, and the only proper prefix with x=3 also has y=±1. The other proper prefixes have x=1 or2. Hence no proper prefix lies in3 Z². The identical argument after rotation handles the other source directions, without assuming quarter-turn covariance of the images.

Under half-turn covariance, the first- and last-letter maps are axis-swapping permutations of the four compass directions, each of order dividing four. The fourth substitution power is therefore rooted on every letter, has time radix7⁴ and endpoint matrix81I, and has internal81 Z² separation by the preceding lemma. Any fixed/root-cycle limit of the original substitution is the corresponding rooted fourth-power limit. Audit FV then forbids finite support: nonzero first-L reads reproduce under scaling, leaving only empty/single-origin minimal seeds, whose time-four return contradicts separation.

This is a finite-support obstruction for this specified length-seven axis-swapping half-turn class. It does not assert ant illegality on arbitrary infinite backgrounds or close other axis-swapping rectangular parameters.

# Four favorable block scatterers: a complete prefix obstruction

Date: 21 October 2026.

Status: an exact parametric first-divergence theorem for one specified
four-scatterer plan, plus three finite all-future escape certificates.
This does not exclude other renewable circuits and does not claim that all
members of this family eventually escape. No priority claim is made.

## 1 The finite seed and the proposed circuit

Use white-right, black-left updates on the square grid. Start at (0,0),
facing north. Let R(x,y)=(y,-x) be a clockwise quarter-turn. For an integer
L>=128 put

    q0=(-127,-107),
    q1=(-151-2L,-82+2L),
    q2=(-126,-58+4L),
    q3=(-102+2L,-83+2L),
    G={(0,-1),(0,0),(1,-1),(1,0)},
    B_L=union over j=0,1,2,3 of (qj+R^j G).

These are four disjoint 2-by-2 black blocks, hence exactly 16 black cells.
The affine checker explicitly verifies that no pair of initial cells can
coincide for any integer L>=128. The planned impact poses are qj with
headings S,W,N,E, respectively. The certified prefix reaches each pose at
the corresponding macro boundary.

The first block is the favorable scattering class a=1,u=-19 at incoming
scale n=64. Its first read occurs at time 15758. From that complete physical
state, simulate 7183 updates: the 7117-update recovery from the scattering
table followed by 66 more standard-tail updates. Call this fixed word F.
Its displacement is (-24,25), and its heading changes from south to west.
The exact 7183-letter word is included in the affine certificate.

Let P be cyclic phase 61 of the standard 104-letter word W. Starting with
the west heading after F, P has displacement (-2,2) and restores that
heading. Therefore the displacement of F followed by L copies of P is

    v(L)=(-24-2L,25+2L).

The four starting positions satisfy q_(j+1)=qj+R^j v(L), with q4=q0.
Four ideal copies of this macro would therefore close geometrically, with
the original position and heading, after 4*(7183+104L) updates. This
geometric closure motivates the candidate. It does not establish equality
of the black field on return.

## 2 Parametric first-divergence theorem

**Theorem.** For every integer L>=128, the actual ant from B_L agrees with
the proposed four-macro word up to time

    t_star(L)=416L+37740.

At that time the ant is at (4,22), facing south. The next planned turn is R,
but the cell is black and the actual turn is L. This is the first difference
between the actual evolution and that prescribed plan.

The obstructing cell was initially white. It was read exactly once, at
time 9500 in the original blank transient, before the first block was
reached. It has not been read again before t_star(L). The return therefore
encounters a genuine retained old black cell.

The theorem specifies the failure of this proposed word. It does not say
that the ant cannot later return by another route, or that every possible
four-scatterer layout fails.

## 3 Exact proof by affine fields

The proof retains the complete initial field and every subsequent net flip.
It does not erase the original core or treat a local channel as isolated.

After the common initial 15758 updates, the field consists of the complete
1390-cell single-block source state together with the other three blocks.
The source state is generated by literal simulation. The other blocks are
affine functions of L. Exact point-incidence guards show that none of these
remote blocks is read during the initial prefix for any L>=128.

Represent a point as

    (a_x L+b_x, a_y L+b_y).

Represent the net flips from N(L) repetitions of a translating period as
an XOR sum of finite rays

    {b(L)+k d : 0<=k<N(L)}.

Here d is one of the four diagonal vectors with coordinates +/-2, and
N(L) is L or L-65. The field after any planned stage is the XOR of its
affine finite points and all previously added ray segments. This is an
exact finite-field representation for each finite L.

For a fixed finite word, visits to each cell determine the required color
before the word starts. The checker verifies that repeated visits prescribe
the same initial color after their intervening flips. It then checks each
distinct input-color requirement against the complete affine field. The
word's odd-read cells are added as its exact net flip set. F has 1204
distinct read cells and 633 net flips.

For a translating period, its 48 distinct read cells likewise specify
colors at q(L)+k d for every epoch k in the allowed range. Earlier completed
periods contribute through the period's 38 net flip cells. A same-period
net flip at offset m d contributes from epoch m onward precisely when m is
a positive integer; intra-period visit parity was already included in the
required initial color.

Each older field contribution to a target read ray is either empty, a
singleton epoch, or an interval of epochs with affine endpoints. Parallel
rays give intervals. Nonparallel rays give a single intersection, whose
epoch coordinates are solved exactly. All divisions use rational numbers.
The checker rejects any case that would require an unhandled residue class
or a change of order above L=128.

Clipping these contributions to the target epoch range yields affine
integer intervals [a(L),b(L)]. The parity function of such an interval has
boundaries at a(L) and b(L)+1. XOR all interval boundaries, together with
the required black-color interval. The checker verifies exact symbolic
cancellation of the resulting affine boundary expressions. Empty boundary
parity means the read-color requirement holds for every epoch and every
integer L>=128.

All inequality guards are proved uniformly. For example, an affine
quantity aL+b is nonnegative for every L>=128 when a>=0 and 128a+b>=0.
Possible isolated point or line incidences are solved exactly and excluded
from the range L>=128. Thus this proof does not extrapolate a few evaluated
parameter values.

The certified prescribed prefix is

    (F P^L)^3 F P^(L-65) P[0:10].

All four copies are placed with the corresponding rotated starting pose.
The checker verifies 4823 fixed read-color constraints and 192 periodic
read-ray constraints. Its 6182 affine range/order guards are all valid
for L>=128.

The resulting head position is exactly (4,22), with heading south. The next
letter P[10] is R. The complete symbolic field is black there. Independently
of this color calculation, affine incidence checks show that no part of
the new four-corner path reads this cell. Literal replay of the common
initial prefix identifies its sole earlier read as time 9500. The next
actual turn is therefore L, proving the claimed first difference.

The elapsed time is

    15758 + 4*7183 + 3*104L + 104*(L-65) + 10
      = 416L+37740.

The planned geometric return would have occurred 6750 updates later.

## 4 Exact outcomes for three complete finite seeds

The following are the only three complete-loop seeds simulated in this
branch. Each uses its entire 16-cell input throughout, and each final tail
has an exact supplied-word infinite certificate against the entire field.

| L | First divergence | Planned return | Certified standard tail | Final black mass |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 128 | 90988 | 97738 | 109671 | 10585 |
| 192 | 117612 | 124362 | 142951 | 14425 |
| 256 | 144236 | 150986 | 176231 | 18265 |

All three certified tails have period 104 and drift (-2,2). At their planned
return times the actual pose is (-113,129),N, rather than q0,S. Exact seeds,
planned and actual stage poses, complete-field hashes, and tail certificates
are retained in the JSON results. The coincidence of these finite outcomes
is not used to claim an all-L escape theorem.

## 5 Files and reproduction

- `four_scatterer_loop.py`: constructs the three exact seeds and verifies
  their complete-field standard tails.
- `four_scatterer_loop_results.json`: exact finite inputs and outcomes.
- `audit_four_scatterer_loop.py`: finds the first mismatch of each of those
  three inputs against the prescribed macro word.
- `four_scatterer_loop_first_differences.json`: finite mismatch records.
- `certify_four_scatterer_prefix.py`: proves the prefix and first-divergence
  theorem for every integer L>=128 by affine fields.
- `four_scatterer_affine_prefix_certificate.json`: fixed recovery word,
  exact time formula, guard counts, and old-cell provenance.

The all-parameter prefix proof is reproduced by running

    python3 certify_four_scatterer_prefix.py

The finite tail certificates remain separate. A later deviation from the
specified word cannot be ruled out by an earlier geometric closure plan;
it must be studied with the actual retained field.

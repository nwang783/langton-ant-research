# Strict historical axial records give an exact compact-recurrence target

Independent analytic scope addendum, 24 October 2026, 11:18 UTC. This is a specialization of the signed relevant-radius recurrence bridge, not a new occurrence theorem or progress claim about universal attraction.

Fix one actual ant orbit from a finite complete black board B0. For one of the four fixed axial normals n in {(+1,0),(-1,0),(0,+1),(0,-1)}, call t a strict historical record beyond the initial hull when

n.p_t > max({n.p_s: 0 <= s < t} union {n.q: q in B0}).

These are stronger than records defined only against the CURRENT black field. At such a pre-read cut, every cell q with n.q >= n.p_t is actually WHITE: neither an initial black cell nor an earlier read can lie there. In particular it is a genuine pristine cut, with no retrospective deletion of an archive.

## Exact equivalence for a single orbit

The following are equivalent:

1. The actual future eventually repeats up to a nonzero spatial translation, without yet requiring the canonical104 word.
2. For some FIXED axial normal n and finite orbit-dependent R, infinitely many strict historical records beyond the initial hull satisfy rho(t) <= R, where rho uses the ENTIRE actual future-consumed current black set C*(t).

Proof of 2 -> 1: among these cuts only finitely many normalized pairs (C*(t)-p_t, incoming heading) fit in the radius-R square. Two coincide. The signed whole-future pruning and deterministic translation argument gives an exact periodic translating future. Here its drift is nonzero directly: the later record has strictly larger n.p, so n.d > 0. No separate no-bounded-orbit lemma is needed for this direction.

Proof of 1 -> 2: choose an axial normal with n.d > 0. The periodic translated future has unbounded n.p and therefore infinitely many strict historical records beyond the finite transient and initial hull. The ENTIRE relevant radius is eventually bounded by the signed converse, since the first-future-read-L sets translate phase by phase. The same R applies to all sufficiently late records.

Every finite-seed ant orbit has infinitely many strict historical records for at least one of the four axial normals: the signed elementary no-bounded-orbit argument gives unbounded positions; if all four coordinate extrema were bounded then the positions would lie in a finite rectangle. This last fact gives records, NOT compactness at them.

## Precise missing occurrence statement

An ant-specific geometric theorem proving bounded ENTIRE C* radius at infinitely many strict historical records would therefore establish eventual translation. Unrestricted canonical translating-front uniqueness would still be separate. Conversely, a genuinely nontranslating finite orbit must have rho(t) -> infinity even along all sufficiently late records, by the stronger signed all-cuts divergence statement.

The exact local separators, record guards, fixed-cohort input budgets, finite-language first-contact results and conditional controller covers do not currently prove this occurrence statement. A bounded number of relevant cells, bounded active mass, or repeated local old-side guard patterns cannot replace the radius condition. A far relevant cell at a pristine record may be tangential rather than deep in the backward normal direction; no transverse-to-axial conversion is asserted.

All retained actual examples eventually capture, so none falsifies the orbit-dependent infinitely-often bounded-radius premise. The signed all-parameter cohort-retirement obstruction is across distinct finite inputs and at a different selected event; it does not establish failure at strict historical records or on one noncapturing orbit. Nor does observing finitely many large-radius records refute an orbit-dependent eventual bound.

This gives the record/separator research and the exact rho reduction a common, noncircular geometric target. It does not supply that target, an effective procedure for computing C*, a uniform radius, a global resource decrease, or an estimate of closeness to solving the conjecture.

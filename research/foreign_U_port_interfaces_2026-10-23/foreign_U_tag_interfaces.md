## 6. What remains open

The tables give an exact tagged interface, distinguish foreign RAW readers from successor-marker readers, and separate old-star erosion from signed factor-relative fresh fuel. They do not bound the number of foreign excursions, show all gates reachable, or prove that all eventual losses of old U tags must be paid during the foreign RAW word. A returned-eight interval can require a new causal argument even if the foreign context missed the old tags entirely. The missing ingredient is an invariant carrying genuine creator history through the foreign word and back, not a silent reuse of whole-eight permanence.

# A two-track old-U return template fails on an owned core hole

## Exact template and scope

Normalize a genuine creating UCC to (0,0,N). Consider the prescribed word

    UCC, X, F, Y, U,

where X and Y are arbitrary finite words of plain D/V packets, X has heading S, Y has heading N, and the final attempted U enters at (0,-2,N). Its actual following marker would be supplied if that final U were valid.

There are exactly TWO unbounded constant-heading tracks, X and Y. The UCC prefix and F connector are fixed. The total source-to-final-U-entry C count is2. The attempted U chord has the surviving same-direction collinear old-U contact geometry: its first contact with the original U chord is at old s=0 and new t=1/2. That geometry does not make it an actual U word.

No complete board realizes this template through the final U RAW, for ANY lengths or orders of X,Y. The proof uses actual source deletion, full residual outputs and endpoint cones, not a postforeign age law or a finite prefix cutoff. Rails containing U, other foreign words, extra C connectors and other contact poses are not covered.

## Endpoint equations retain all rail orders

Write dX,vX,dY,vY for the D/V counts. Source UCC exits at (-1,5,S). The actual F entry and exit are

    A = (-1-dX-vX, 5-dX-3vX, S),
    F exit = (3-dX-vX, 7-dX-3vX, N).

The final U entry is therefore

    (3-dX-vX+dY+vY, 7-dX-3vX+dY+3vY, N).

Equality with (0,-2,N) is equivalent to

    dY=dX, vY=vX-3, vX>=3.

These identities do not depend on rail ordering. The pure-V specialization is X=V^n,Y=V^(n-3), n>=3. It is a concrete single-gap/one-counter-style endpoint family; no general one-counter automaton or decidability theorem is asserted.

## The physical source hole

Let g=(-1,0). It is in the genuine creating U entry core, so it is initially BLACK and is physically deleted by U RAW tick1/read2. The source's own two C tails never reread it. At the genuine UCC full exit, g is outside both the current core and the fresh full output O.

If g later becomes a BLACK external residual, some genuinely completed later full packet must export it in O with an actual last R. A hypothetical core write is not a loophole: a later core-to-residual export is itself a full O event. The proof does not assert that g is physically WHITE at every intermediate core cut.

## No S-rail residual rebirth before F

Every X entry has x=-1-dprefix-vprefix<=-1. The exact D output is only (2,0); the exact six V outputs have x>=0.

For a heading-S packet to produce g through output o, its head must be g+o. D would require head x=1, impossible. V can have head x<=-1 only through outputs (0,0) or (0,1), forcing x=-1. But a rail head with x=-1 must be the FIRST X entry, whose y is5 rather than the required y0 or1.

Thus none of the seven actual D/V output roles can install g as a residual. At F entry g is outside K, so it is physically WHITE there.

## F does not repair the hole

The cell pulled into the actual F entry frame is

    (-dX-vX, 5-dX-3vX).

Its y is at most-4 because vX>=3. The actual F RAW has minimum local read y=-2, so it never reads g. Relative to the F exit, g has y=dX+3vX-7>=2, outside the actual N entry K. Hence the complete foreign execution cannot repair or hide g in its exit core.

## No N-rail residual rebirth before the target

For a heading-N D/V source to export g, its head must be g-o. D's sole output yields full exit y=1. Every V output has local y<=2 while V's head displacement has y=3, so every possible full V producer of g also exits at y>=1.

The final target U enters at y=-2. Every remaining zero-C N D/V atom raises y strictly. Therefore none of those candidate full output producers can occur and complete before that target. All seven true output slots are retained; raw core writes are not mistaken for residual outputs.

At the attempted final U entry, g is outside K and cannot be BLACK. Yet it is precisely the U input at local(-1,2), first read at actual U RAW tick13/read14, which requires BLACK. Contradiction.

## Actual finite prefix controls, not a completed-contact claim

Five complete finite boards in the JSON realize the ENTIRE UCC,X,F,Y prefix and target-U RAW ticks0 through12. They include pure-V n=3,4,10 and the genuinely mixed pairs X=VDVV,Y=D and X=VVDVV,Y=DV.

Each reaches g at (-1,0,W) with that cell WHITE. The real next turn is therefore R, not the intended U tick13 L. All earlier physical reads and full initial black sets are replayed. At that current marker, C/V already disagree at tick12, D lacks its next M11, and U/F/Q/B all need L at tick13. The actual R therefore excludes EVERY seven-atom continuation from that marker.

These are actual finite U-entry, firstforeign, two-track PREFIX controls. They do not realize a completed final U or a proxy first contact: no U proxy may be assigned to a word that fails inside its RAW. For pure V the intended immutable L/L conflict is at global tick36n+73 against source tick1. The samples demonstrate nonvacuous prefixes; the all-length, all-order exclusion follows from the exact output and endpoint argument above.

This identifies a real surviving source-core memory obligation in a tractable two-track slice. It does not restore a finite-state geometry quotient, close all two-track windows, bound all ordinary-tail lengths, or prove global attraction.

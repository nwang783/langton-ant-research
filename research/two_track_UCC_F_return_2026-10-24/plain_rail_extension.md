# Separate extension to arbitrary plain D/V/U rails

The signed bcd968da D/V result is unchanged. This addendum covers the SAME exact two-track template

    UCC, X_S, F, Y_N, attempted U(0,-2,N),

but now X and Y may be arbitrary finite plain D/V/U words. There are still no additional C turns in either rail. No other foreign, connector or target geometry is included.

## Endpoints and the foreign miss

Put deltaD=dY-dX, deltaV=vY-vX, deltaU=uY-uX. The exact endpoint condition is

    deltaD=2deltaU, deltaV=-3-2deltaU.

Equivalently, the S rail's total y drop satisfies dropX=9+dropY>=9. Thus the source-deleted g=(-1,0) pulls into F as (-dX-vX,5-dropX), with y<=-4. F RAW's actual minimum local read y is-2, so it still misses g. At F exit g has relative y=dropX-7>=2, outside its actual N core.

The pure-V offset n-3 is therefore only one specialization. No equality of D counts or V-only approximation is imposed once U is admitted.

## Every S residual origin is regenerated

The actual S rail starts at (-1,5,S). A prefix with counts d,v,u has head(-1-d-v,5-d-3v-4u). To install g via fresh full output o, its entry must be g+o.

Across the true D/V/U full output sets there are16 roles:

-11 require an entry strictly right of all S heads;
-2 have no nonnegative prefix-count solution;
-2 have unique prior prefix U, already containing the immutable forbidden word UCC,U;
-1 has unique prior prefix V, already containing forbidden UCC,V,U.

In the last case the prospective U would recycle g through its output(-1,2), but it needs the SAME still-absent g BLACK at U tick13 before the later R21. The whole UCC,V,U prefix fails at global89 versus sourceL1 on g. The UCC,U guard fails at70 versus30 on another genuinely deleted source cell. Both are literal contradictions on every board.

Importantly, V output(0,1) changes its reason when U steps are admitted: x=-1 no longer means the first rail entry. Its possible preceding U is excluded explicitly, not ignored by copying the old D/V proof.

Thus no valid completed S-rail packet supplies the source hole before F.

## Every N residual origin also remains impossible

For all plain D/V/U packets, maximum output y is strictly below its head y drift:0<1,2<3,3<4 respectively. Any genuine full O producer of g would therefore exit at y>=1. Remaining zero-C N motion has strictly positive y drift, whereas the target enters at y=-2. None of the16 output roles can complete in time geometrically.

The residual-to-physical argument is the same as the signed narrower proof: a BLACK external cell after the original L deletion needs a later genuine full O. A core-to-residual export is included, not silently discarded. Physical whiteness at intermediate cores is not assumed. At the target, g is outside K and its actual U tick13/read14 requires BLACK, so the completed target U is impossible.

This proves an all-length, all-order exclusion for one structurally complete two-track template without a general finite-state or decidability claim. It does not close other two-track connectors, other old-source windows, or arbitrary ordinary-tail simplicity.

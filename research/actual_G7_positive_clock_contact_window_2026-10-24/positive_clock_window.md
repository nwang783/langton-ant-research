## 3. Effective source-to-ordinary-contact clock

For an ordinary source, let f count complete foreigners after the source entry and before the target ordinary entry. For a foreign-piece source, f counts complete foreigners strictly AFTER that source's own foreign. Its remaining true kink/half-turn stays in the source correction. Put L=K+2f.

- Ordinary source x: T = L pi/2 + a(x) - a(target).

# Second continuation: five-block fold geometry and exact boundary checks

Date: 22 October 2026. The infinite-fold branch H remains open. Nothing in this directory proves the full Langton's ant highway conjecture. The exact statements below are separated from finite experiments and unsuccessful invariant searches.

## Rigorous geometric results

1. **Three double-C folds on pure V rails are impossible.** With F=CC in the before-marker convention, F V^n F V^m F is physically infeasible for every n,m>=0, on every initial coloring. The proof gives explicit consecutive same-letter visits in all parameter ranges. Hence no finite-support trajectory can have an eventual tail over {V,CC}. The mixed-rail generalization is false: CC V^4 U^2 CC V^3 CC M is feasible from an explicit28-black seed. See `double_c_fold_lemma.md` and its checker.

2. **There is no absolute all-coloring turn bound for H.** The infinite word product over i>=0 of U^(5*2^i) C V is physically feasible on a computable infinite initial coloring, has infinitely many C turns and isolated J10 events, and is aperiodic. An all-parameter separation proof handles the four growing rectangular arms. Its first K arms require exactly 4+25(2^K-1)+2K distinct initially black cells. Thus it does not start from finite support. See `isolated_event_spiral.md`.

3. **The last tested port-dependent height candidate fails after arbitrarily long prescribed H futures.** A75-black base state and5n additional lower-row black cells realize V U^(n+2) while the candidate increases from0 to1 on the first V, for every n>=0. The graph follows actual ports and eventually the U self-loop. This excludes repairing this particular candidate merely by requiring any fixed finite amount of H lookahead. It does not exclude another potential or an argument using finite total fuel. See `arbitrary_lookahead_obstruction.md`.

## Exact global verification method

`verify_maxplus_sft.py` gives an exact finite-field decision procedure for a proposed directional potential

\[
\Phi=\max\{p_y+c_{\mathrm{port},h},\;z_y+w_{P_z(B)}:P_z(B)\ne0\}.
\]

Its zero sublevel is a finite-height column language. A trimmed finite graph quantifies exactly over upper-strip fields with finite horizontal support. A dynamic program imposes all known history colors, performs the macro's XOR updates, and checks every relevant head height. Every counterexample is reconstructed as a finite field and verified by literal execution. The proof of coverage is in `maxplus_sft_protocol.md`.

For the first version the head correction has only four orientation constants. The final extension permits a separate constant for each of the995 actual ports and four orientations, for4,491 effective variables including the511 nonzero pattern weights.

## Targeted fuel-hull tests and their limitation

The remaining conjectured geometric statement is:

> For every nonempty physically feasible finite concatenation of the five before-marker words, the box of all read cells lies within four cells in each cardinal direction of the box of distinct globally first-read-L cells.

If proved, this would confine every finite-support H tail and rule it out using the positive mass increase of every macro. It has not been proved.

The original complete depth13 search covered1,536,076 feasible prefixes, found maximum excess4, and retained the sharp witness CVVVCCVVVVCC. The later targeted replay used adversarial fields generated by the max-plus failures:

No fuel-hull counterexample was found in these finite experiments. The short replay lengths do not imply that the violating upper strips cannot be completed to longer trajectories: unspecified lower cells in the literal witnesses were filled white.

The exact extension test therefore allowed arbitrary lower additions while fixing each upper strip and following the true port graph. Of the20,125 last-candidate strips,6,550 admit no following macro,4,884 admit one but not two, and8,691 admit two. The all-horizon family described above comes from the first surviving V,U,U example.

## Structural issue exposed by the failures

In the all-horizon example, the ant adds a black cell at(-1,0), changing a patch centered at(0,1) from mask390 to391. The tested weights assign these masks−1 and0, so the global pattern maximum rises even though the ant immediately leaves westward. Arbitrarily long additional rail fuel can be supplied entirely below that patch. Thus the failed potential is sensitive to a local reclassification of an existing pattern that is passive relative to the continuing rail.

A successful proof still needs to retain the chronology connecting current rail fuel to earlier folds, or identify a stronger invariant that distinguishes an active boundary from such passive patterns. The pure-V fold lemma cannot simply be applied to the counts of U and V: the explicit mixed-rail counterexample already shows why their order matters.

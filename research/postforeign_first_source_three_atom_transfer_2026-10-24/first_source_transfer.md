# A portable three-atom source transfer, and its exact fourth-atom limit

Status: author checked, pending independent audit.

This certificate uses only literal ordinary RAW words D, C, V, U and actual M11 reads. In particular, it applies when these atoms immediately follow a first foreign F/Q/B. It does not apply any old-eight age theorem after the foreign, rename an unsupported internal C a full producer, or assume a future ordinary tail is simple.

## The new first returned source cannot own a THIRD contact

Normalize the first ordinary RAW atom b1 to (0,0,N). Across all 64 triples b1,b2,b3 over D/C/V/U, the third corrected-center chord meets b1's chord in exactly one geometry:

- C,C,U: the old-source parameter is zero, the third-U parameter is one half, and the contact is the first C entry point (-1,-1).

That whole RAW word is literally impossible: L at combined tick42 repeats L at tick2 at (-1,-1), inside the third U RAW. It cannot be repaired by a different incoming board. Thus no actual legal ordinary triple can have a THIRD-chord contact with its first source. The outgoing ownership of the first source's entry vertex is retained; it is not discarded as a harmless endpoint.

The foreign-piece contacts are deliberately left to the separate archive certificate. This note does not assert global third-step simplicity.

## Every fresh first-source residual identity survives both later atoms

The literal RAW+M11 geometries give D one, C three, V six, and U nine residual outputs. For each output the certificate records the actual final R birth tick in b1. These are new physical identities even when the cell formerly belonged to an input or entry core.

Of the 64 triples, 55 have a consistent complete RAW+actual-M11 context. In every one, each original b1 output cell is outside the COMPLETE before-context of b2 and of b3. There are 270 tagged first-output instances; both complete-context misses are checked for every instance. The same cells are outside the active cores at b2, b3 and b4 entry.

It follows directly from physical unreadness that all SAME original b1 output identities remain BLACK, physically alive, and CURRENT at entry to b3 and to b4. No recolored replacement is counted as an original. No future M11 reads are pretended to have happened: checking their absence only makes the asserted physical cuts safer. This is a finite two-successor transfer, not an arbitrary-tail seal.

## Actual fourth-atom erosion without proxy contact

The prescribed continuation A,V,C,C,V is completely legal from finite prepared boards for A=F,Q,B, requiring respectively 22, 23 and 26 initial black cells. More importantly, U,Q,V,C,C,V and U,B,V,C,C,V are genuine U-entry finite-board executions, requiring 28 and 31 black cells. All unspecified initial cells are WHITE. Every RAW and actual following M11 read is physically replayed. Their full expanded corrected-center proxies, with the true Q/B kink at F56, are simple through the fourth ordinary RAW atom.

In each execution, track the FIRST returned V's original A output, local (0,1), born by its actual RAW R11/read12. The next two C atoms do not read it. At fourth-V entry it is SAME/current. The fourth V puts that still-alive original into its exit core; its local coordinate is (0,3), not (1,2). It has no physical read during that V's RAW18. The actual first physical L is V context tick19, which the canonical formatter assigns to the NEXT actual word's tick1/read2. At the fourth RAW exit the original is therefore physically alive-CORE, not CURRENT; at that following read2 it is physically deleted.

For the genuine U,Q case the birth is global tick103, the fourth V starts at140, its RAW exit cut is158, and physical death is159. For genuine U,B the corresponding ticks are113,150,168,169. The complete executions include 169 and179 fine reads respectively.

These are real erosion controls, not a THIRD-contact counterexample, not old creating-U star loss, and not proof of any infinite continuation. They show that whole-cohort persistence cannot be iterated merely because the first three ordinary atoms preserve it. In particular, physical erosion of a new postforeign V cohort need not be accompanied by ANY proxy contact.

The immediate U,F,V,C,C,V version is separately preserved as a failed lift: its actual final-marker R157 repeats source-U R21 at (-1,2). It is not counted as a genuine complete control.

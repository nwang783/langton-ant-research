## Frame and exact statement

Normalize the ENTRY of ANY actual F/Q/B to (0,0,N). This is the FOREIGN ENTRY frame, not the foreign-EXIT-N chart used by the four-hook certificates. Rotate and translate covariantly when applying it.

Every such foreign freshly leaves the SAME ten source-coordinate output cells

    (-1,-1), (-1,0), (-1,1), (0,0), (0,1),
    (1,0), (1,1), (1,2), (2,0), (2,1).

Their actual last-R ticks are recorded individually. They are source-foreign output identities, not the equal-colored entry identities that may have occupied those cells before the source RAW.

Continue only by actual ordinary RAW D/C/V/U atoms, with actual marker contexts, and no intervening foreign. The initial ordinary heading is S. After zero, one and two actual Cs it is S, E and N respectively.

All ten SAME originals are CURRENT through the S0 and E1 phases. At any real N2 cut of head (X,Y), an original g is guaranteed CURRENT whenever

    g_y > Y,
    or g_y = Y and X is not g_x+1.

CURRENT means the same source-R BLACK identity outside actual marked-cut CORE. For the other original ports no status is inferred. No initial U, earlier-foreign count, globally simple proxy, rail-length bound, or eventual contact owner is assumed.

## Why arbitrary opposite-rail cancellation does not break the guarantee

The three actual source-foreign exits have x<=-3 and y<=-2. Every selected g has x>=-1,y>=-1.

Choose the first CURRENT loss of any one selected identity. The true-input inventory covers physical deletion, recycled inputs and marker-owned exit-CORE capture. All twenty ordinary exit-CORE roles are explicitly checked, so an original physically alive in CORE is counted as a loss rather than ignored.

- In S0, head x never increases, and every input has x<=head x+1<=-2. It cannot touch g.
- The first actual C takes S to E with displacement (0,-1). Thus E heads have y<=-3; they never increase y, and every input has y<=head y+1<=-2. It cannot touch g.
- C has no external true input and cannot be the first CURRENT loss.
- In N2, every possible first-loss RAW exits at or above the tag's row. The only equality cases are D upper, V upper and U upper marker-owned inputs. All have exit (g_x+1,g_y). Every subsequent N base strictly increases y.

A later cut below g's row cannot follow such a loss. A cut on that same row can follow it only at the exact equality exit X=g_x+1, with no later zero-C base. This proves the stated predicate. It permits arbitrary earlier S/E/N rail lengths and arbitrarily large cancellation. It does not assume the source tag is physically WHITE after its first loss, so later replacements do not evade the proof.

## What the frontier does NOT say

This is not a strict ordering of consumption events. A low-row original may remain dormant while another higher-row original is consumed. It is only a one-sided height filter, with the precise equality exception. Every unresolved low/equality port must remain in any later causal representation.

No tag is guaranteed through a later foreign merely because it was current at its entry. No claim is made for three or more Cs, all possible next-foreign poses, a finite-state quotient, bounded field, archive-merger budget, arbitrary-tail simplicity, trajectory coverage, or capture.

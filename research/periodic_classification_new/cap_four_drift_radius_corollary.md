# Cap-four fronts require larger actual drift

23 October 2026, 09:24 UTC. Independently signed strengthened corollary (audits KH/KI/KS/KW/LD/LI) of
the signed supplied-drift exclusions.

translating front with at most four reads per physical cell and actual
full drift d=(a,b)!=0 must satisfy

    |a|+|b| >= 8,          a^2+b^2 >= 40.

graph covers (±3,±3), because all four sign choices are genuine

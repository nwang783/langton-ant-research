# Minimal possible full drift after the unit-axis exclusion

23 October 2026, 05:09 UTC. Independently signed corollary of signed/source
theorems. The finite-seed periodic-suffix bridge is separately signed too.

Let d=(a,b) be a nonzero actual full drift of a complete white-input
translating front. Heading restoration forces an even period; checker
parity therefore gives a+b even. The retained all-cap full-unit-diagonal
exclusion rules out (±1,±1). The independently signed
`axis_negative_drift_extreme_exclusion.md` rules out (±2,0),(0,±2).

Those are all nonzero integer vectors of even coordinate sum with
Manhattan norm below four. Consequently

    |a|+|b| >= 4,             a^2+b^2 >= 8.

Moreover, among vectors with max(|a|,|b|)<=2, only (±2,±2) remain.
They are not excluded: the standard104 front has a full drift in this
family. This does not prove that its period or local history is unique.
The full drift is never replaced by its primitive direction.

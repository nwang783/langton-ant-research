# Mandatory narrow proxy-metadata qualification

The frozen young_ancestry.json field all_proxy_pair_checks.true_adjacent_endpoint recognizes the nonparallel parameter representation only. A false flag does not assert a genuine nonadjacent contact. In particular, consecutive collinear pieces meeting solely at the previous end and next start are recorded by collinear_old_interval=[1,1], not by explicit old_s/new_t. They are legitimate true adjacent endpoints, and the separate exact qualifier lists all such records.

The frozen proof does not assert that these prepared controls are simple, and it never uses that metadata flag for the endpoint, ancestry or identity arguments. All 800 predecessor queries and all physical reads remain unchanged. This qualification changes no original frozen file and makes no new simplicity claim.

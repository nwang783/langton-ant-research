## Theorem

active w component has at least EIGHT edges. Equivalently, no finite seed has
an eventual sustained active-w cap of seven.

This is not a capture theorem, not unbounded active mass, and not a bound for
arbitrary infinite initial backgrounds. The already-standard104 complete

strategy for the highway conjecture.

## Proof

Assume an actual finite-support orbit maintains active w mass<=7 from some
finite cut onward.

1. The independently checked cap-seven kernel and its exact six-state nonempty
core imply every nonempty excursion reaches empty within33 cuts. The kernel
has72,284 states/63,244 transitions; its sole core cannot make the next required
import after one full RRLLLL loop. The bounded-core return automaton therefore
covers every first return and has exactly58 possible turn words. Its154,794,412
abstract component profiles are counted, not expanded.

2. The independently checked mandatory-field filter excludes35 words from a
sustained capped tail:5 read contradictions,2 endpoint contradictions,13
within-word cap violations, and15 forced future-cap exits. Every actual return
in the supposed infinite tail belongs to the remaining23 words.

3. The exact common-field word-pair test considers all529 ordered pairs. Its
78 actual positive pairs and451 arbitrary-exterior negative tests establish
true finite-word existence, not compatibility of one selected profile. Of the
positive pairs,27 have mandatory final fields forcing future cap exit. Hence
the infinite word sequence must follow the remaining51-edge graph.

4. That finite graph has only two recurrent SCCs: the two-word SCC on
S=RRRRLRLRL and T=RRRRLLLLRLRL, with all four ordered edges, and the singleton
SCC on U=RRRRLLLLRL. Any infinite graph path is eventually confined to one of
these SCCs.

5. An infinite U-run is impossible from a finite board. U restores heading and
translates by(1,1). Its phase5 L read is the unique phase on line x-y=-1, so
copy k consumes a distinct cell(-1,0)+k(1,1) never previously read in that run.
Every such cell must already be black at the run's start. A finite board cannot
supply infinitely many. These are black cells at that finite cut, not necessarily
original-seed lifetimes.

6. An arbitrary infinite S/T mixture is excluded by ST_SATURATED_PORT_FUEL.md.
After the first block, the preceding RL ending supplies a real R-born hook.
The hook and current forced reads saturate the first-four-R old-edge budget3,
pinning every complete entry module. Earlier R contacts are determined by
complete boundary ports; the successor's saturated entry pins the only final
unknown import to empty or one actual old singleton. Thus counting all complete
passive components of size one or two gives exact drops SS1, ST2, TS0, TT1.
Two zero TS cases cannot be consecutive. A finite nonnegative count cannot
support the infinite mixture.

Both recurrent possibilities are impossible, contradicting the sustained cap.
All fields stay finite at finite cuts; all passive archives stay on the board.
The final fuel proof applies universally to every realizing exterior, not only
the small minimum seeds used as positive controls.

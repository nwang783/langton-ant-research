## What the method added

A type-only fuel argument genuinely fails on physically legal finite prefixes:
small modules can be split or preserved while writing new small modules. The
failure is not merely numerical LP infeasibility. But these local resource
identities do not automatically compose on a single board. Exact mandatory-output
traps and read-parity full-component constraints remove the incompatible or
finite-exit cases. A positive integer fuel then exists on the surviving physical
profiles, and the final zero-fuel adjacency graph is empty.

This is a concrete geometry-plus-resource proof method, not a generic tape
encoding, and not an undecidability reduction. The exact finite-support compiler
obligation remains cursor/port-compatible transport and regeneration across
arbitrarily many macros while retaining all output archives. The explicit
splitter, reversal and one-shot writer do not satisfy that obligation.

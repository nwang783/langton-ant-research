# The reusable lesson, and the precise remaining global obligation

24 October 2026. This separates general elementary lemmas from the ant-specific
hypotheses that remain open. It does not claim a new global invariant or promise
that cap-by-cap enumeration scales to the highway conjecture.

## 1. Saturated entry determines a complete component, for any cap

Let M>=4. At an empty w root, the next four R updates add its four root-star
edges P. Suppose actual prior outputs and forced unread input colors already
identify a set F of M-4 distinct OLD black edges, and P union F is connected to
the root. If the complete active component remains<=M, it is EXACTLY P union F
at cut4. Every other incident port is white. This holds against arbitrary
finite or infinite exterior; any extra connected edge would exceed M.

This is not an enumeration of component shapes. It is a cardinality equality
plus exact geometry. A literal finite update sequence from this complete field
then determines every split and contact until a genuinely unexposed R port is
reached. Already exposed white ports cannot be assigned convenient later inputs.

If a final RL pair starts with a complete active singleton, deletes that
singleton on L and reaches empty, the surviving R-born hook is the new edge
plus exactly the component imported at R. A successor's saturated entry can
therefore pin that complete output component and determine the previous unknown
import. This two-sided constraint is what made the universal cap-seven S/T
ledger possible; it was not inferred from four minimum-board examples.

## 2. Exact passive-component leakage lemma

Segment a finite-support orbit at empty-active cuts. Give every complete
passive component a finite nonnegative integer weight. Its total weight V is
finite at every finite cut. Across a macro, the exact change is

    V_after - V_before = sum(weights of complete emissions)
                        - sum(weights of complete imports).

R can join only the current active component to one whole dormant component;
L can detach only from the active component. Thus there is no silent passive
merge, and all unchanged archives cancel. Emitted components that are later
reimported must occur in BOTH chronological terms, not be dropped or double-
charged as fresh input.

Suppose a finite control graph covers every hypothetical orbit in a specified
nonterminal regime. Suppose full-state geometry proves, on each allowed control
edge e, that V decreases by at least a nonnegative integer d(e). If the graph
of zero-cost edges is acyclic, every infinite control path would incur infinitely
many positive decreases, contradicting finite nonnegative V. The same conclusion
holds whenever zero-cost runs have a separately proved uniform finite bound.

This is a proof SCHEMA, not a claim that an appropriate graph or weight always
exists. Finite-exit traps may remove transitions only in a regime where their
exit contradicts the hypothesis. A cap exit is not global capture, so one may
not discard such transitions in a proof of the full highway conjecture.

For cap seven, the control graph was the S/T tail, weight1 for each component
of size1 or2 and zero otherwise, and costs SS1/ST2/TS0/TT1. The zero graph had
one edge T->S and no cycle. The full port proof supplied the universal ledger.

## 3. A shape-free bound for unread-support distortion

For any k>=1 let K_k(B) count COMPLETE black components with between1 and k
edges. Adding one black edge changes K_k by a number in[-2,1]: it can create
one new component, enlarge one, or merge at most two. Therefore adding q edges
changes K_k by a number in[-2q,q], independent of k and component geometry.

Now fix one whole literal ant word. Let B_min be its minimum first-read-L initial
board, and let B=B_min union X be any realizing finite board. Every edge of X
is NEVER read by that whole word, so the same X is added to the minimum's field
at BOTH endpoints. Consequently

    | (K_k(B_final)-K_k(B_initial))
       -(K_k(B_min,final)-K_k(B_min,initial)) | <= 3|X|.

This charges a static unread initial edge ONCE across the whole word, not once
per apparent reuse. It is only an endpoint stability bound. It does not prove
any descent on the minimum board, and local extra inputs that are read elsewhere
in the whole word belong to B_min rather than X. Treating every local connector
as globally unread would invalidate the argument.

The one-extra-branch cap-eight T control illustrates the issue: adding an unread
old branch changes the small-component fuel return by+2. A general argument
must charge or exclude such release; the cap-seven zero-slack proof cannot be
reused unchanged.

## 4. Saturation alone is compatible with the real highway

The independently signed standard104 frontier has exact active maximum42 at
phase64. At phase60 its active w root is empty; the next four phases have active
masses39,40,41,42. Thus the genuine highway itself has a saturated four-R gate,
with38 old edges and4 fresh edges. Its finite frontier is regenerated while
leaving permanent output, rather than depleted by a positive leakage cost.

Hence saturation determines a complete local interaction but does NOT guarantee
finite fuel loss. At and above the genuine standard cap, one must classify
admissible resource-neutral recurrence, not prove every trajectory exits.
The signed standard frontier identity K -> (K+d) union T is already one real
spatially composable neutral controller with persistent archived output.

## 5. What is still missing for a global reduction/proof

No argument here shows that arbitrary finite seeds eventually have bounded
active mass, enter saturated ports of a controlled form, or admit a finite
control graph outside the standard highway. No argument classifies all
spatially composable zero-cost module cycles as standard104. Unsaturated old
branches can release small resources, and imported archives can carry unbounded
geometric or chronological information. Those are the genuine remaining gaps.

For a finite-support computational reduction, the equally concrete obligation
is an actual cursor-compatible, indefinitely reusable module circuit with ALL
old outputs retained. One-shot splitting, a type-level resource circulation,
pairwise compatibility and reversal do not establish that circuit. The fixed
conjecture and any input-indexed decision problem must still be distinguished;
no undecidability, unrecognizability or independence result follows here.

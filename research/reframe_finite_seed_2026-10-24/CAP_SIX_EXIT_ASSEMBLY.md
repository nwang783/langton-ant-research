## Theorem

For the standard white-R/black-L ant on any finite black seed, there are
infinitely many cuts whose full active w component has at least SEVEN edges.
Equivalently, no such orbit has an eventual sustained active-w cap of six.

This is NOT eventual standard104 capture, not eventual translation, and not a
uniform bound on global highway formation. The theorem uses finite support for
finite resource weight and is not asserted for arbitrary infinite backgrounds.

## Proof

Suppose that from some finite cut onward the full active component always has
at most six edges. The complete field at every finite cut remains finite.

1. Every update belongs to the complete normalized cap-six local kernel: all
14,116 rooted shapes and all 12,060 cap-preserving complete import/split
transitions are enumerated. Its nonempty-state subgraph has an exact topological
ordering. Thus this infinite path visits the empty-active state infinitely
often. Every first return is one of the 43,527 listed complete profiles.

2. The general empty-root import-budget lemma excludes seven entire turn-word
classes on any sustained cap-six tail, regardless of the exterior. The remaining
29,721 profiles are tested against all chronological full-component boundary
requirements. Exactly 4,657 can be realized even as a single finite-board
prefix. Any actual return must be among them.

3. Every consistent profile has a minimal initial black set consisting of its
required-one bits. Its final black field is a mandatory subset of every actual
realization of that profile. Applying the same arbitrary-exterior budget lemma
to this larger mandatory output excludes another 4,564 profiles: each would
force cap exit within at most nine further updates. Therefore every return in
the supposed sustained tail belongs to the remaining 93 profiles.

4. At an empty-active cut define F as the sum over ALL passive w components of
weights one for singleton edges, one for corner dimers, two for straight dimers,
and zero for all other components. F is a finite nonnegative integer. Exact
whole-component import/emission accounting proves F is nonincreasing on all
93 profiles and strictly decreases on 86. The remaining seven exact zero-drop
profiles are indexed 78, 4554, 4555, 4866, 4895, 5169, 33389.

5. Consequently the supposed infinite return sequence eventually contains only
these seven profiles. But the separately frozen seven-profile full-field lift
certificate checks ALL 49 ordered adjacent pairs. Every pair has a concrete
contradiction between complete-port requirements pulled back through the real
read chronology. Thus no two of these zero-drop profiles can occur consecutively
on any board, even with arbitrary unread exterior. This contradicts an infinite
zero-drop tail.

All archive edges remain in the complete field in steps 2 through 5. The proof
does not replace them by counts when checking geometry. The count potential is
used only after the spatially trapped and inconsistent profiles are excluded.
Pairwise incompatibility suffices here because the compatible graph is EMPTY;
no claim that a compatible cycle would be physically realizable is made.

## Quantitative return count, without a promoted physical-time constant

Starting at an empty cut with fuel F0, there can be at most 2*F0+1 consecutive
completed returns from the 93-profile survivor list: at most F0 strict drops,
with at most one zero-drop return in every gap. A return outside that list either
cannot occur or is followed by cap exit within nine updates. A final suffix
avoiding empty has bounded length by the exact nonempty DAG. These facts imply
an effective finite exit bound, but no optimized numerical physical-time bound
is asserted in this assembly.

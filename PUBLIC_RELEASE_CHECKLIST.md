# Public-release checklist

This snapshot is prepared for private preservation. Public visibility and an open-source license are separate decisions. Making a repository public does not itself grant permission to copy, modify, or redistribute all of its contents. No repository-wide license has been assigned by this snapshot.

Before a public release:

- [ ] Obtain explicit approval for the intended public audience and the exact repository, release assets, and history to be published.
- [ ] Complete a rights/provenance review of original research, recovered bundles, embedded quotations, figures, data, and code. Confirm who can grant the needed permissions; retain existing authorship and copyright notices.
- [ ] Review every third-party component and dependency at its exact revision. Preserve applicable licenses and notices, and omit or reference materials whose redistribution rights are unclear. See [source provenance and rights](docs/SOURCES_AND_RIGHTS.md).
- [ ] Choose appropriate licenses only for materials whose rights are established. Code, prose, and datasets may need different treatment. Do not apply a blanket license to third-party work or treat public availability as permission.
- [ ] Review the final reachable Git history and every release asset for personal information, credentials, private communications, and unintended metadata. Automated marker checks assist but do not establish an exhaustive privacy guarantee.
- [ ] Verify archive hashes, source/export mappings, and restoration instructions against the exact release candidate. Preserve privacy-transformation disclosures; do not silently rewrite frozen mathematical certificates.
- [ ] Keep **UNSOLVED** prominent. Retain rejected claims, negative controls, conditional hypotheses, audit distinctions, and the limits of finite tests and formal trust boundaries.
- [ ] State the mathematical snapshot cutoff and how later corrections and independently reviewed contributions will be added. Do not imply that packaging validates every proof or makes every historical replay portable.

Until those decisions are complete, keep the repository and its release assets private. This checklist is not a license grant or authorization to change visibility.
